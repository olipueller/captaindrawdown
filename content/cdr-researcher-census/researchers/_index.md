---
title: "CDR Researcher Profiles"
description: "Profiles of the researchers in the public top lists of the CDR Researcher Census: most active and most cited per pathway, worldwide and in Europe."
---

Profile pages exist for the researchers in the public top lists of the census: per pathway the 100 most active and the 100 most cited researchers worldwide since 2021 and the 50 of each in Europe, six pathways, about 1,000 people in release 2026-11.4. Everyone else in the census is counted in the aggregates by country, pathway and sector only: see the [data page](/data/researchers/). The [lookup](/cdr-researcher-census/lookup/) finds a listed researcher by name. Corrections and removal: [info@carbon-drawdown.de](mailto:info@carbon-drawdown.de?subject=Census%20removal%20request), details in the [privacy notice](/privacy/).
