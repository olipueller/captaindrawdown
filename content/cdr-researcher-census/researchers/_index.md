Profile pages exist for the researchers in the top lists per pathway (the 100 most productive worldwide and the 50 most productive in Europe since 2021, seven pathways, about 630 people). Everyone else in the census is counted in the aggregates by country, pathway and sector only: see the [data page](/data/researchers/). Use the [search tool](/cdr-researcher-census/lookup/) to find a listed researcher by name, country or pathway. Corrections and removal: [info@carbon-drawdown.de](mailto:info@carbon-drawdown.de?subject=Census%20removal%20request), details in the [privacy notice](/privacy/).
