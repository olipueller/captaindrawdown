Newest first. A release covers all three datasets and stays available as a folder and a release in the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/dirkpaessler/cdr-databases) and under `/data/v1/<version>/` on this site; the newest is also under `v1/latest/`. Entries before the first release are the version history of the company directory (launched 10 March 2026, rebuilt in April, liveliness tiers since May, monthly refreshes on the 15th) and of the researcher census (launched 18 March 2026); "Census refresh" rows are the monthly database updates on the 11th, which feed the release around the 15th. Corrections between releases are noted here and applied in the next release.
