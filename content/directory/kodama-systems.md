## About

Kodama Systems is a Sonora, California-based forest management and robotics company that thins fire-prone western US forests and sequesters the waste woody biomass in engineered underground "wood vaults." It combines teleoperated/autonomous skidder technology (Kodama Autopilot, RS1) with biomass burial CDR in partnership with the Yale Carbon Containment Lab. Backed by Breakthrough Energy Ventures, Congruent Ventures, and a Frontier/Stripe pre-purchase for ~415 tonnes of CO2.
