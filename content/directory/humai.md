## About

Hum.ai, legally Coastal Carbon dba Hum.ai and formerly called Coastal Carbon, is a San Francisco-area AI startup building earth-observation foundation models. It sells to customers in nature conservation, carbon dioxide removal and government, and does not remove carbon itself. Its earlier work focused on monitoring seaweed aquaculture and coastal or ocean blue carbon.
