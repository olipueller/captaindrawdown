## About

Standard Biocarbon produces engineered biochar from renewable sawmill residuals (spruce and fir wood waste) in Maine using high-temperature pyrolysis. Their biochar is used in agriculture, landscaping, soil enhancement, and for remediation, including reducing PFAS contamination.
