## About

Standard Biocarbon is a Maine-based biochar manufacturer that pyrolyzes softwood sawmill residuals using Pyreg technology, producing roughly 50-60 cubic yards per day of engineered biochar. The company sells bulk biochar to composters, soil blenders, landscapers, and agricultural users, and also supplies material for PFAS remediation research with Northern Tilth, Purdue, and the University of Maine.
