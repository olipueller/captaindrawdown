## About

Inherit Carbon Solutions is a Norwegian BECCS project developer that captures biogenic CO2 from biogas plants processing wastewater and agricultural organic waste and permanently stores it under the North Sea. In March 2026 it entered operation with what it describes as the world's first carbon removal project from biogas, delivering Puro.earth-certified credits to buyers including Microsoft, DNV and Nordea.
