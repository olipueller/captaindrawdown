## About

Alithic is a University of Wisconsin–Madison spinoff (formerly Earth RepAIR) developing an integrated direct air capture and mineralization process that uses a sodium carbonate aqueous solvent to react CO₂ with industrial wastes like coal fly ash and steel slag, producing a supplementary cementitious material (SCM) as a saleable byproduct. The company received a Frontier prepurchase in fall 2024, operates a demonstration plant in Madison, and is raising funds to build a ton-scale pilot at the National Carbon Capture Center in Alabama.
