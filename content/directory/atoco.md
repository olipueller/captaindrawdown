## About

Atoco develops advanced solid-state sorbent modules built on proprietary reticular materials for efficient CO2 capture and separation from the atmosphere (Direct Air Capture, DAC) and industrial emissions. Their DAC innovation leverages nano-engineered reticular materials to adsorb CO₂ at low concentrations.
