## About

Stiftung Risiko-Dialog (Risk Dialogue Foundation) is a Swiss non-profit foundation that hosts the Swiss Carbon Removal Platform and conducts stakeholder dialogues, policy research, and public engagement around carbon dioxide removal. It does not remove CO2 itself but connects CDR stakeholders across industry, government, research, and civil society, and has shaped Swiss negative-emissions policy on behalf of the Federal Office for the Environment (BAFU/FOEN).
