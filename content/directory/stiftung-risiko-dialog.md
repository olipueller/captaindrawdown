## About

Stiftung Risiko-Dialog (Risk Dialogue Foundation) is a Swiss non-profit foundation based in Zurich that runs dialogue and public-engagement work on risk and technology topics, including energy, climate and deep geothermal. It hosts and runs the Swiss Carbon Removal Platform (CDR Swiss), which links industry, research, government and civil society on carbon removal. CDR is one major program inside a broader risk-dialogue organization.
