## About

myclimate is a Swiss non-profit climate protection foundation, spun out of ETH Zurich in 2002, that co-develops and finances voluntary carbon market projects including afforestation and biochar CDR, while also offering footprinting and consulting services. It acts as a project developer, broker and advisory organisation rather than building removal technology itself. CDR is part of a broader portfolio that includes emissions-reduction offsets.
