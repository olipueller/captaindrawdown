## About

Oxford Charcoal Company is a UK producer of ethically-sourced lumpwood gourmet charcoal and biochar, grown in British woodlands. Biochar is described on their own site as a bi-product of the charcoal-making process, used for gardening, horticultural and agricultural purposes as a soil enhancer. They sell biochar in retail bags to gardeners rather than issuing carbon removal credits via registries, making CDR a minor adjacent product line to their BBQ charcoal business.
