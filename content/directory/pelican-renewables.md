## About

Pelican Renewables LLC is developing the Pelican Carbon Sequestration Hub on Rindge Tract in California's Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta, anchored by their Stockton ethanol facility which provides biogenic CO2 for permanent geologic storage via Class VI injection wells. The project also accepts third-party CO2 delivered by rail and barge (via affiliate ZuCO2 Transport), and was selected for a $45.2M DOE CarbonSAFE Phase III award in 2024-2025.
