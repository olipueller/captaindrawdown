## About

BioCarb Solution is a Danish BECCS aggregator that collects biogenic CO2 from Danish biogas plants, handles logistics via Hirtshals Harbour, and delivers it for permanent offshore geological storage under Puro.earth certification. It is wholly owned by Danish Bio Commodities (DBC), a biogas-focused investment/consulting firm, and sells the resulting CDR credits to corporate buyers.
