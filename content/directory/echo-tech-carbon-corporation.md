## About

Echo Tech Carbon Corporation is a Vancouver, Canada-based biochar project developer operating primarily in Africa, converting agricultural residues into biochar for soil amendment and construction materials while generating permanent carbon removal credits. The company is certified/listed via Puro.Earth and Carbonfuture and has inaugurated multiple biochar plants, including a Senegal partnership with rice farmers.
