## About

Woodtek is a family-run Mid-Wales engineering firm that designs and manufactures the C1000 biochar pyrolysis unit and operates its own biochar production at the Carbon Hill site, plus a joint venture (Biodynamic Carbon Ltd) with Shropshire Council. Output is registered on Puro.earth for CORC issuance, and Woodtek has a distribution partnership with Onnu (PyroUnity) to deploy plants internationally.
