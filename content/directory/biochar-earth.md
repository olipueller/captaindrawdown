## About

Biochar Earth is a biochar carbon removal (BCR) supplier focused on converting coconut shell and husk waste into durable biochar through controlled pyrolysis, with operations centered in the Philippines. The company sells removal credits at $199 per tonne CO2 and separately offers avoided-emissions credits at $39 per tonne tied to diverting coconut waste from open burning.
