## About

Altitude Carbon is a Switzerland-based CDR financier that procures biochar carbon removal credits (CORCs) from project developers globally, including facilities in Argentina, West Africa, and Southeast Asia. They do not physically remove CO2 themselves but operate a purchasing facility (Ascent 1) to provide offtake financing to biochar CDR developers. Their entire business exists to scale the CDR market through capital deployment, placing them squarely in the CDR ecosystem.
