## About

Vault 44.01 is a Calgary- and Denver-based CCS project developer, backed by Grey Rock Investment Partners, that partners with ethanol producers and other industrial emitters to capture, transport, and permanently sequester CO2 in deep geologic reservoirs. Their flagship One Carbon Partnership joint venture with Cardinal Ethanol received a final EPA Class VI permit in April 2026 and plans to store up to 450,000 tonnes of CO2 per year. Because corn-fermentation CO2 is biogenic, the storage delivers BECCS-style carbon removal.
