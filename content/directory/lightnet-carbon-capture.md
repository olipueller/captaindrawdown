## About

LightNET Carbon Capture (LNCC) develops industrial solutions for CO2 capture, sequestration, and valorization, inspired by natural CO2 mineralization processes. They focus on an accelerated ex-situ mineralization route to convert CO2 into products like CO2atings and CO2rallites.
