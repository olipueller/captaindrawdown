## About

LightNET Carbon Capture (LNCC) is a Barcelona-based ICN2/CSIC spin-off that develops industrial solutions inspired by natural CO₂ mineralization, based on scientific results from CSIC's IMB-CNM and ICN2 licensed to the company. Their product portfolio includes advanced ceramics (CO₂rallites) that fix CO₂ in their structure, coatings (CO₂atings) that capture CO₂, and mineralization filter units that can be integrated into CO₂ streams and combustion systems. On November 12, 2025, CSIC formalized its entry as a shareholder member of LightNet Carbon Capture.
