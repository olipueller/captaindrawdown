## About

Nitricity is a cleantech company that produces renewable, low-emission nitrogen fertilizer using a non-thermal plasma reactor that extracts nitrogen from air and water with renewable electricity. While their primary focus is fertilizer production, their method of creating nitrogen fertilizer with zero carbon emissions contributes to decarbonization.
