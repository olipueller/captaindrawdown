## About

Kohero Farming CC is a Namibian farming close corporation operating between Omaruru and Wilhelmstal. It runs the Global C-Sink project GCSP1076, where it acts as C-Sink Manager for artisan biochar producers that turn encroacher bush into biochar for soil application. A related brand, Kohero Carbon / Kohero Biochar, produces, processes and trades biochar.
