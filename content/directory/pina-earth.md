## About

Pina Earth is a Munich-based climate-tech company that develops forest conversion and adaptation projects in Germany, Austria, and Switzerland, turning monoculture stands into climate-resilient mixed forests and issuing carbon credits certified under ISO 14064-2 and the eva Forest Climate Standard. In July 2025 it merged with Tree.ly to form Europe's largest forest carbon platform, and it has ongoing active partnerships (e.g. with myclimate on the Herzfelde project in Brandenburg).
