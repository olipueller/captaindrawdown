## About

Mote is a Los Angeles-based BiCRS startup, spun out of Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, that converts wood waste into carbon-negative hydrogen with permanent CO2 sequestration. Its flagship Bakersfield, California facility has completed a FEL-2 study and received a DOE Loan Programs Office Part II invitation, with a second project planned with SMUD in Sacramento. In March 2025 Mote closed $7M in Series A funding to advance its first commercial-scale plant.
