## About

Caspian Environmental Consortium Ltd. is a Kazakhstan-based bio-economy research organization, resident of the Astana International Financial Center since 2023, whose CaspianCDR project adapts Brilliant Planet's microalgae cultivation and burial approach to the eastern Caspian Sea coast in the Mangystau region. The organization also runs bioremediation and education projects and is endorsed as a UN Ocean Decade action, with CDR research at pilot/survey stage.
