## About

Circularity2 is a Minneapolis, Minnesota-based biochar producer founded in 2022 that converts woody biomass into biocarbon products and sells biochar-based carbon removal credits. In August 2025 it announced plans to build a flagship biochar manufacturing facility on a 24-acre site at the Cross City Industrial Park in Dixie County, Florida, sourcing forestry and sawmill residuals from the regional wood basket.
