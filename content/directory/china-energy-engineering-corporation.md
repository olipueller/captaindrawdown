## About

China Energy Engineering Corporation (CEEC / Energy China) is a Chinese state-owned power and infrastructure EPC conglomerate (~RMB 453B revenue in 2025, 115,000+ employees) whose core business is engineering, procurement and construction for power, renewables, grid and infrastructure projects. A CEEC subsidiary, together with Shanghai Jiao Tong University, developed CarbonBox, described as Asia's largest DAC module, which passed reliability testing in July 2024 with intended applications in green methanol, green aviation fuel and carbon trading. CarbonBox remains a pilot-scale R&D effort inside a much larger non-CDR parent, and the envisioned uses lean toward CCU rather than permanent removal.
