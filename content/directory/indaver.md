## About

Indaver is a Belgian waste-management company that treats household and industrial waste in several European countries through recycling, energy recovery and hazardous-waste treatment. It is looking at carbon capture and storage at its waste-to-energy plants in the Antwerp port cluster, and the biogenic share of that CO2 would count as removal. CDR is an add-on to its main waste business.
