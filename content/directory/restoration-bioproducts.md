## About

Restoration Bioproducts is a Virginia-based biochar producer developing pyrolysis-based projects that refine low-value biomass and forestry waste into biochar, bio-oil, and power. Its flagship ~$10M Waverly, VA facility opened in October 2024 and is expected to generate ~10,000 tCO2e/year of removal credits, with Microsoft having pre-purchased credits via Carbon Streaming Corporation.
