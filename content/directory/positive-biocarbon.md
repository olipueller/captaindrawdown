## About

Positive BioCarbon is a biochar brand launched in 2021 by Rectella International Ltd, a Burnley-based family-owned manufacturer best known for the Bar-Be-Quick instant barbecue and lumpwood charcoal brand and historic textile/curtain production. The Positive BioCarbon line supplies FSC-accredited biochar (0–20mm grades) derived from virgin waste-stream biomass for agriculture, horticulture and industrial customers, but represents a small adjacent product line rather than the parent's main business.
