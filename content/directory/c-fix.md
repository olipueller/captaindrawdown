## About

C-Fix is a pre-seed concept for a solar-powered direct air capture system. The C-Fix DAC concept is solar powered and uses alkaline metal sorbents and electrolytic regeneration, and the company is currently raising seed money to hire a team and get the technology off the ground. There is no evidence of a pilot, employees, press coverage, or registry issuances.
