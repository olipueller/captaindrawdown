## About

Renova AB is a municipally owned waste-management and recycling company in Gothenburg, Sweden. It runs the Sävenäs waste-to-energy combined heat and power plant. It is developing a CCS facility there (ReStore), which won EU Innovation Fund support in 2026. Waste management and energy are its core business; carbon capture is an add-on.
