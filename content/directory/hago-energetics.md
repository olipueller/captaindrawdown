## About

Hago Energetics is a California-based startup (founded 2017) developing a carbon-negative hydrogen process that strips hydrogen from biomethane and deposits the carbon as biochar. EcoEngineers' third-party LCA validated strongly negative carbon intensity (as low as -492 gCO2e/MJ with biochar sequestration). The company was selected as a Qualified Hydrogen Supplier by First Public Hydrogen Authority in January 2026 and has won DOE, CEC, NASA and NYSERDA awards.
