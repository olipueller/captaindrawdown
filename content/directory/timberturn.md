## About

TimberTurn is a Canadian CDR supplier that converts uneconomical waste wood from fuel-thinning operations into durable carbon storage through engineered biomass burial. The company pairs wildfire risk reduction with permanent carbon sequestration, offering landowners revenue-sharing partnerships and using geotechnical engineering plus MRV technology to verify storage.
