## About

Adsorb is a South African manufacturer of activated carbon operating a plant in Brakpan, east of Johannesburg. The company pyrolyzes wood chips from pallet repair centres (sourced from responsibly managed forests) at 1,000°C to produce biochar and activated charcoal, supplying the fertiliser, animal feed, cosmetics, water treatment and mining industries. They reported capturing approximately 750 tonnes of CO2 in 2024.
