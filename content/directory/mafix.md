## About

Mafix is an early-stage enhanced rock weathering startup. It treats silicate rock so it weathers faster on farmland, aiming to remove CO2, raise crop yields and cut fertilizer use. AgFunderNews reported that it raised $5.4M in pre-seed funding to scale the technology.
