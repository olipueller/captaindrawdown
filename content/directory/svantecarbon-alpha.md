## About

Svante, which acquired Carbon Alpha, develops and manufactures nanoengineered filters and modular rotating contactor machines for carbon capture and removal from industrial emissions and the air. Carbon Alpha, now integrated with Svante, focuses on developing carbon dioxide removal (CDR) projects and industrial carbon capture and storage (CCS) solutions, including BECCS.
