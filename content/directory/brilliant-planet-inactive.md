## About

Brilliant Planet was a London-headquartered startup (founded 2013) that grew marine microalgae in open-air raceway ponds on Morocco's coastal desert, spray-drying the biomass and burying it in engineered landfills for 1,000+ year storage. It raised a $12M Series A from Union Square Ventures and Toyota Ventures in 2022 and sold 1,500 tons of CDR to Block via Watershed in 2023, but appears to have wound down: the brilliantplanet.com domain now hosts unrelated content, no new funding or construction milestones have been reported since 2023, and a 2025 independent LCA by Below280/Decerna describes the work in past tense.
