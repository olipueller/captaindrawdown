## About

Carbon Hill is a biochar producer owned by the Jones family at Caebardd farm near Welshpool in mid-Wales. It is a sister company of Woodtek Engineering, which makes wood chip dryers. Carbon Hill sells biochar carbon removal credits through Puro.earth, with Accend AS listed as the supplier, and it is a 50/50 partner with Shropshire Council in Biodynamic Carbon Ltd, a joint venture set up to expand biochar capacity.
