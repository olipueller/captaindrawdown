## About

Reverion is a German BECCS company (2022 TUM spin-off) that builds solid oxide fuel cell power plants converting biogas into clean electricity while capturing and permanently storing biogenic CO2. In November 2025 it signed a $41M offtake with Frontier for 96,000 tons of permanent CO2 removal between 2027-2030, following a $62M Series A in 2024.
