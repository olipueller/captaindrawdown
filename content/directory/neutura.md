## About

Neutura is a Singapore-headquartered carbon removal company operating biochar production facilities in Indonesia using palm-oil empty fruit bunches as feedstock. It has delivered a pilot project in collaboration with Universitas Sumatera Utara and signed partnerships with Indonesian farms to apply biochar to degraded agricultural land.
