## About

Carbon Blade is a San Diego-based startup founded around 2021 that develops modular, containerized direct air capture systems. It ran a pilot with UC San Diego's Center for Energy Research from 2023 to 2025 and partnered with the engineering firm Coffman. In November 2025, its pilot capture system was sold at an industrial liquidation auction, which suggests the company may be winding down.
