## About

Carbon Blade was founded in 2021 as a technology-based company with a strong focus on Distributed Direct Air Capture. The California-based startup develops containerized, renewable-powered DAC units using an electrochemical (EDBM) process to capture CO2, and is piloting its prototype with UC San Diego's Center for Energy Research.
