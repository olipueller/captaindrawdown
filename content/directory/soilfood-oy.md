## About

Soilfood Oy is a Finnish circular-economy company that turns industrial side-streams into recycled fertilizers and soil improvers for agriculture. It is the supplier for the Puro.earth project Soilfood-1-Finland, which issues soil amendment carbon removal credits. Fertilizer and soil-improver sales are its main business, so CDR credits are an add-on activity.
