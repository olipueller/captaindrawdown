## About

Aichi Obiettivo 20 srl is an Italian innovative startup with a social purpose (SIAVS), based in Villa Sant'Angelo near L'Aquila. Through its BlockCO2 brand it designs a methodology for biochar carbon credits, audits biochar projects, and sells blockchain-traced Credi-C credits to companies on the voluntary market. The credits link Italian rural forestry communities and biochar producers with buyers.
