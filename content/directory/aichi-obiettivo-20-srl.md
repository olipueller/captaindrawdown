## About

Aichi Obiettivo 20 is an Italian innovative social-purpose startup based in Villa Sant'Angelo (AQ) that develops biochar-based carbon removal supply chains and issues blockchain-tracked "Credi-C" carbon credits through its BlockCO2 project. The company links rural/mountain forestry communities with voluntary carbon markets, supporting biochar production from sustainably managed woody biomass via pyrolysis.
