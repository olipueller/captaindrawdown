## About

Perivoli Climate Trust is a Namibia-domiciled not-for-profit (company limited by guarantee) funded by the UK-based Perivoli Foundation. It funds African climate research and operates the Perivoli Rangeland Institute, an accredited C-Sink Manager that converts Namibia's encroacher bush into biochar for permanent carbon removal and soil regeneration, generating carbon credits.
