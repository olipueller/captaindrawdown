## About

Arca Climate Technologies is a UBC spin-off based in Vancouver that works in partnership with dozens of mining companies from Canada, Australia, and around the world. The company leverages pre-existing industrial infrastructure and alkaline waste to accelerate the natural process of carbon mineralization and permanently remove carbon dioxide from the atmosphere. In January 2026 Arca signed a 10-year tailings access agreement with Giga Metals for the Turnagain Nickel Project in British Columbia, granting Arca exclusive rights to evaluate the potential of the ultramafic waste rock and mine tailings to permanently remove CO2.
