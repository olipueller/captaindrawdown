## About

CrystalTrade is a dMRV and carbon-data platform built by Crystalchain, a supply-chain traceability company based in Paris. It helps carbon removal project developers, mostly in biochar, track biomass sourcing, emissions and storage so they can get credits issued on Puro.earth. Its customers have included Carbon Plus in Malaysia, and it has a data partnership with ClimeFi. It does not remove carbon itself.
