## About

Re-Vi is an Australian CDR developer building a large-scale biochar project on Kangaroo Island, South Australia, converting 4.5 million tonnes of bushfire-damaged timber plantations into agricultural-grade biochar and durable CDR credits. At full production (targeted 2026) the facility is expected to produce approximately 200,000 CDR credits and 100,000 tonnes of biochar per year. The project is listed on Puro.earth and supplied by related company Kiland, which owns the land and timber.
