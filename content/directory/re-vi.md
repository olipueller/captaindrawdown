## About

Re-Vi is an Australian CDR developer building one of the world's largest biochar projects, on Kangaroo Island, South Australia. The project converts 4.5 million tonnes of bushfire-damaged timber plantations into biochar and durable CDR credits, with full production targeted for 2026 at ~200,000 tCO2/yr. It is listed as a supplier on Puro.earth.
