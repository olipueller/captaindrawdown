## About

INEOS Greensand is an offshore CO2 storage project in the Danish North Sea. INEOS Energy operates it, with Harbour Energy and Nordsøfonden as partners. Commercial operations began in late 2025 with capacity of about 400,000 t CO2 per year, scalable to 4–8 Mt, and the first CO2 stored was biogenic. It is a storage project inside an oil and gas company, not a standalone CDR firm.
