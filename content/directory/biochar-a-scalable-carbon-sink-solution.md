## About

SUEZ is a global water and waste management company that operates a dedicated biochar carbon-removal business line through joint ventures. It co-owns Carbonity, Canada's largest biochar plant in Port-Cartier (Québec), with Airex Energy and Groupe Rémabec, and partners with PYREG to produce biochar from sewage sludge. SUEZ targets 350,000 tonnes of biochar production per year by 2035.
