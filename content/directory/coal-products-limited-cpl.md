## About

CPL Industries (Coal Products Limited), now part of the Invica Industries group, is a UK maker of smokeless solid fuel briquettes, with headquarters in Chesterfield and its main plant in Immingham. With BEIS Greenhouse Gas Removal funding, and working with Severn Trent Green Power and the University of Nottingham, it is building a biochar plant at Immingham that it says will remove about 6,000 tCO2 a year.
