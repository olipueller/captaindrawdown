## About

Prana Climatech Private Limited is an Indian climate-tech company and carbon project developer, incorporated in 2025 in West Bengal. It turns industrial by-products and agricultural wastes into carbon removal projects, chiefly enhanced rock weathering, to generate verifiable carbon credits. It employs scientists for carbon conversion work and runs field soil-sampling programs.
