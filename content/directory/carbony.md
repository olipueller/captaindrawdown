## About

Carbony is an Austria-based CDR project developer focused on Enhanced Rock Weathering in managed forests across Central and Eastern Europe, differentiating itself from the more common cropland-based ERW approach. It is a member of the Enhanced Weathering Alliance and has corporate partnerships including with Refurbed.
