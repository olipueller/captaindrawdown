## About

Carbony is an Austrian company focused on Carbon Dioxide Removal (CDR) using Enhanced Rock Weathering (ERW). They aim to remove 500 million tons of CO2 by 2040 by spreading finely crushed silicate rocks on land, which react with CO2 and rainwater to form stable carbonate minerals.
