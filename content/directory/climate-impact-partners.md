## About

Climate Impact Partners is a UK/US-based voluntary carbon market specialist formed from the merger of Natural Capital Partners and ClimateCare, offering carbon credit sourcing, portfolio development, MRV support and project origination across 600+ projects in 56 countries. They are a carbon market intermediary rather than a physical CDR supplier — their business is trading and developing credits from third-party projects, including nature-based removals.
