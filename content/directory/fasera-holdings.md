## About

Fasera Holdings is an Australian company founded in 2005 that operates oil mallee plantations in the Western Australian wheatbelt, producing eucalyptus oil as its primary product, with biochar and wood vinegar generated by pyrolyzing the spent biomass left over from steam distillation. The company is a Puro.earth-registered biochar supplier (Fasera Biochar Plant 1) and also generates ACCUs from its plantations, but eucalyptus oil (sold 95% in bulk to domestic and international buyers) remains the core business.
