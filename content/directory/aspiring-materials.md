## About

Aspiring Materials is a Christchurch, New Zealand cleantech company that processes olivine rock into critical minerals, with magnesium hydroxide as its primary product used for point-source CO2 capture via mineralization. The company opened Stage One of its purpose-built pilot plant in Christchurch in 2025 and ran a demonstration with PepsiCo capturing CO2 from a snacks manufacturing line. Co-products (reactive silica, iron, NCM hydroxide) support decarbonization of cement, battery, and industrial supply chains.
