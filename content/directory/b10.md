## About

B10 (B10 Char Trading AG) is a Swiss-incorporated biochar producer with operations in South Africa, founded in mid-2023 by commodities traders Philip Edmonds and Tobias Munk. Its ~$2M pilot pyrolysis facility near Nelspruit (Mpumalanga) launched in 2025, targeting 7,000 tonnes of biochar per year from ~22,000 tonnes of sawmill/timber waste, equivalent to roughly 15,000 tCO2 removed annually. Revenue is expected from biochar sales to farmers plus carbon credit sales.
