## About

B10 (B10 Char) is a biochar producer with a Swiss holding (B10 Char Trading AG) and operations in South Africa. Its first pyrolysis plant near Nelspruit became operational in Q3 2025 with capacity of around 6,000-7,000 tonnes of biochar per year from timber waste, equivalent to roughly 15,000 tCO2 sequestered annually. The company plans to generate most of its revenue from carbon credits while distributing biochar to farmers.
