## About

B10 (B10 Char Trading AG) is a biochar producer with a Swiss holding company and operations in South Africa. Its first pyrolysis facility near Nelspruit (Mpumalanga) was launched in 2025, with a planned output of around 7,000 tonnes of biochar per year from ~22,000 tonnes of timber/sawmill waste, equating to roughly 15,000 tCO2 removed annually. Revenue is expected to come primarily from carbon credits, with biochar sold or provided at low cost to farmers.
