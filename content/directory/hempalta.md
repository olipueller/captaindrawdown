## About

Hempalta Corp. (TSXV: HEMP) is a Calgary-based AgTech and climate-technology company pioneering nature-based carbon removal solutions through its subsidiary Hemp Carbon Standard (HCS) and its proprietary blockchain-based registry, Trusted Carbon, leveraging industrial hemp to generate certified carbon credits under ISO 14064-2. Following the wind-down of its hemp processing operations announced September 8, 2025, the Company has transitioned from capital-intensive processing to a streamlined, carbon-credit-focused model. For the 2024 growing season, Hempalta verified 29,448 tonnes CO₂ of carbon credits across 12,669 acres and 38 farms in Canada, USA, UK, Ukraine, Sweden, Germany, and Australia.
