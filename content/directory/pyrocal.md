## About

Pyrocal is a Queensland, Australia-based supplier of proprietary continuous carbonisation (pyrolysis) systems that convert biosolids and organic biomass into biochar. They operate Australia's first biosolids carbonisation facility at Loganholme WWTP (Logan Water) and are building a system for the Capital Regional District in British Columbia, Canada, also supporting clients with carbon credit generation via their Terix Biochar brand.
