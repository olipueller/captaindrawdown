## About

Carbon TerraVault Holdings (CTV) is the carbon management subsidiary of oil and gas producer California Resources Corporation (CRC). Some of its projects run through a joint venture with Brookfield. It is building California's first CCS project at Elk Hills: capture equipment was finished in 2025 and the project received EPA Class VI permits. Most of the CO2 it handles comes from fossil point sources, so only a small share of the business is carbon removal.
