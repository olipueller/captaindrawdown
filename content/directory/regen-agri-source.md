## About

Regen Agri-Source is a small Missouri-based agricultural retailer in Stover, MO selling high-grade biochar (82%+ fixed carbon) alongside its flagship BLOOMFIELD microbial and enzyme soil-health product line and CharLit biochar/poultry-litter pellets. They market biochar primarily for soil improvement, odor sequestration, and plant health rather than issuing verified carbon removal credits. The company's main business is regenerative-ag soil products, with biochar as one product line.
