## About

NARA Climate is a Scottish agro-ecology startup founded in 2021. It restores salt-affected and arid land by farming halophytes with saline water and builds carbon sinks. Its community- and refugee-led biochar project in Turkana, Kenya, run with Criou Energy and Planboo, is certified under the CSI Global Artisan C-Sink standard and issued and sold its first 144 tCO2e of credits in June 2026.
