## About

Planetary Technologies is a Canadian ocean alkalinity enhancement (OAE) supplier headquartered in Nova Scotia, with active projects in Halifax (Tufts Cove) and Virginia. In 2025 they delivered the world's first independently verified OAE credits (625.6 tonnes) to Stripe, Shopify, and British Airways under Isometric's OAE protocol, and signed a $31M offtake with Frontier.
