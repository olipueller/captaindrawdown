## About

VERBIO SE is a listed German biofuel producer making bioethanol, biodiesel and biomethane in Germany, the US and other markets. It produced a record 1.218 Mt of liquid biofuels and 1,410 GWh of biomethane in FY2025/26. CO2 capture and storage is a small, add-on part of a business built mainly on biofuels.
