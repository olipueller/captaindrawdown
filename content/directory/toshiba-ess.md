## About

Toshiba Energy Systems & Solutions (Toshiba ESS) is the energy division of Toshiba Corporation that developed and operates a large-scale BECCS demonstration facility at the 50 MW Mikawa biomass power plant (operated by its subsidiary SIGMA POWER Ariake) in Omuta, Japan, capturing more than 500 tonnes of CO2 per day from palm kernel shell-fueled flue gas. The project, funded by Japan's Ministry of the Environment, began operations in 2020 and expanded under a FY2021–FY2025 demonstration including CO2 liquefaction and transport. Toshiba ESS's core business remains power generation equipment; CDR is an R&D/demonstration activity within the division.
