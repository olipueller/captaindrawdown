## About

GreenCap Solutions AS is a Norwegian environmental technology provider of carbon dioxide removal solutions, applying Direct Air Capture technology to closed greenhouse systems and industrial applications. Founded in 2016 and headquartered in Sandnes, Norway, the company is also the DAC technology provider to Removr, a joint venture 60% owned by Vanir Green Industries and 40% owned by GreenCap Solutions, which aims to build a 1-million-ton solid sorbent DAC plant.
