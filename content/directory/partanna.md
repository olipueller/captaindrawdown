## About

Partanna Global is a Bahamian advanced materials company co-founded by former NBA player Rick Fox that produces a carbon-negative cement alternative made from magnesium compounds derived from desalination brine and recycled ingredients such as steel slag. The product cures at ambient temperature, avoiding clinkering, and absorbs CO₂ over the building's life, generating Verra-certified carbon credits under the VM0043 methodology. The company is scaling internationally with a manufacturing facility in Abu Dhabi (ADIO partnership) and a 2025 MoU with Exeed Industries in the UAE.
