## About

EnviraPAC Monticello LLC is a biochar maker in Monticello, Arkansas, founded around 2014. It turns softwood pine residues into engineered carbon products for farming and soil cleanup, and for industrial uses as a renewable replacement for petroleum- or coal-based carbon black.
