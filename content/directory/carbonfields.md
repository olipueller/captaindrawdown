## About

Carbon Fields is a Madrid-based biochar company that transforms biomass waste into biochar and sustainable bioproducts for agriculture and industry. The company participates in the EU-funded FENIX Project, producing biochar-digestate mixtures at TerraWatt SAS that are being field-tested by Universidad de Granada, INRAE, and the Soil & Water Resources Institute ELGO-DIMITRA.
