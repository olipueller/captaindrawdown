## About

GE Vernova is a publicly traded global energy company (NYSE: GEV) whose DAC program, run out of its Advanced Research Centers in Niskayuna, NY and Bengaluru, India, is developing proprietary solid-sorbent direct air capture technology. The company commissioned a 10-ton/year DAC test facility in New York and in September 2025 announced a partnership with Deep Sky to deploy a 1,500 tonne/year DAC unit at Deep Sky Alpha in Innisfail, Alberta, with operations scheduled to begin in late 2026.
