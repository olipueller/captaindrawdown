## About

TVERF (Tees Valley Energy Recovery Facility) is developing a facility in the North-East of the UK that will process general rubbish to generate electricity, with operations expected to begin in 2029. While it is an energy recovery facility, the provided search results do not explicitly state it is a Carbon Dioxide Removal (CDR) company using BECCS, but rather an Energy Recovery Facility.
