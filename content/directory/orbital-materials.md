## About

Orbital Materials is a UK/US advanced materials startup founded in 2022 that uses proprietary AI models (LINUS, Orb) to design novel sorbents, with its first commercial focus on data center-integrated direct air capture. It is piloting containerized DAC hardware with Amazon Web Services and UK cloud provider Civo, leveraging data center waste heat and airflow to lower DAC energy costs.
