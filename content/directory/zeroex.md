## About

ZeroEx is a Munich-based startup, founded in 2022, that develops enhanced rock weathering projects on agricultural land in Germany, for example ZX-24 Vulkaneifel in the Eifel region. It is one of Isometric's lead ERW suppliers. It works with Anglo American to use quarry rock fines for ERW, and it sells its credits directly and through marketplaces such as Senken.
