## About

ZeroEx is a Munich-based climate tech startup founded in 2022 by Tony Oehm, Hannes Steinle, and Marcel Kuhs that develops Enhanced Rock Weathering projects on German farmland using basaltic rock powder sourced as a quarry byproduct. The company has screened 150+ quarries, runs field trials in the Eifel region, and is building proprietary MRV technology (including soil-buried ion sensors/SIAs) with partners like Yale, LMU, and the University of Copenhagen. It is listed as a supplier on Puro.earth, Isometric, and Senken.
