## About

ZeroEx is a German climate tech startup founded in 2022 by Tony Oehm, Hannes Steinle, and Marcel Kuhs, established as one of Europe's leading enhanced rock weathering companies. The company develops ERW projects in Germany using basaltic rock powders derived as a by-product of existing mining operations, having tested over 100 quarries to secure feedstocks with high CDR potential and low heavy-metal concentrations. They are listed as a supplier on both the Puro.earth and Isometric registries.
