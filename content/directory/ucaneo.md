## About

Ucaneo is a Berlin-based DAC startup founded in 2022 that operates an industrial pilot capturing 30-50 tons CO2/year and is building what it claims will be Germany's largest DAC demonstration plant, targeted for commissioning in H1 2026. The company raised €6.75M in seed funding in September 2024, with Aramco Ventures joining the round in March 2025.
