## About

NAWARO ENERGIE Betrieb GmbH is an Austrian bioenergy producer headquartered in Zwettl, operating biomass and wood-gas power plants across the Waldviertel region. At its Perg location (in operation since 2023), wood gasification yields biochar as a co-product, which the company sells for agricultural, construction and metallurgical uses. The firm is listed as a member of the European Biochar Industry Consortium.
