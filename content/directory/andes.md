## About

Andes is a climate tech company that uses beneficial microorganisms applied alongside crop seeds to accelerate the natural mineralization of atmospheric CO₂ into inorganic carbon in agricultural soils. They have developed the world's first Microbial Carbon Mineralization (MCM) methodology.
