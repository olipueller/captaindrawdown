## About

Zenthos Energy (previously Flow Aluminum) develops next-generation rechargeable Aluminum-CO₂ batteries for energy storage. Their technology can operate with an open cathode design where CO2 is provided from a CO2 source (e.g., a Direct Air Capture installation) during discharge and released as a stream during charge.
