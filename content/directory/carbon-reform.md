## About

Carbon Reform is a Philadelphia/Wilmington-based climate tech startup (founded 2020) that retrofits commercial building HVAC systems with modular hardware that captures indoor CO2 and contaminants while lowering ventilation energy use. Captured CO2 is mineralized on-site into limestone byproduct for use in concrete and paint. The company raised a $5.5M seed-plus round in January 2025 (bringing total to ~$9M) and has active customer deployments with utilities such as Baltimore Gas & Electric and New Jersey Natural Gas.
