## About

Regionalwerke AG Baden (RWB) is a Swiss municipal utility supplying the Baden region with electricity, water, gas and heat. Through its joint venture CO2 Energie AG (with Recycling Energie AG), it operates a BECCS-style CO2 capture and liquefaction plant at the Nesselnbach biogas-upgrading facility, and is building a third plant in Niederwil (commissioning spring 2026) designed to deliver ~5,000 t/yr of biogenic negative emissions via mineral storage in partnership with Airfix/South Pole. The original Nesselnbach output is sold to Messer Schweiz for industrial reuse (CCU), but the Niederwil expansion extends the operation into true permanent CDR.
