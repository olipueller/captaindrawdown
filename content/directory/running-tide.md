## About

Running Tide was a Portland, Maine-based ocean carbon removal startup founded in 2017 that operated out of Iceland and delivered early ocean CDR credits to buyers including Shopify, Stripe, and Microsoft. The company shut down in June 2024, laying off all remaining staff after raising over $50M, citing insufficient voluntary carbon market demand and inability to secure further financing. It is no longer operational.
