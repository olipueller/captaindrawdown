## About

Running Tide was a Portland, Maine-based ocean carbon removal startup founded in 2017 that deployed limestone-coated wood chips and macroalgae buoys in the North Atlantic off Iceland, combining ocean alkalinity enhancement with biomass sinking. The company delivered credits to Microsoft, Shopify, Stripe and ~25 other enterprise buyers before shutting down in June 2024, laying off its remaining ~32 staff after raising ~$54M (Series B led by Lowercarbon Capital) and citing insufficient voluntary-market demand and inability to secure further financing.
