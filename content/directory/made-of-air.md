## About

Berlin-based materials company founded in 2016 that turns wood-waste biochar into carbon-negative thermoplastic granules and composites for long-lived industrial applications (e.g., Audi dealership facades, H&M sunglasses). Each ton of material reportedly sequesters multiple tons of CO2 in products, positioning the firm as a biochar-based CDR supplier via durable material pools.
