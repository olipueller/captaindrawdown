## About

Airfix is a Swiss subsidiary of climate consultancy South Pole, backed by the Migros Pioneer Fund, dedicated to scaling the European BECCS/BiCRS market. Its flagship project with CO2 Energie AG near Zurich will capture biogenic CO2 from a biogas plant and ship it to INEOS's Greensand storage site in Denmark, expected operational in Q2 2026.
