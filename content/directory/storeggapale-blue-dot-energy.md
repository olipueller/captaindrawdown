## About

Storegga (which acquired Pale Blue Dot Energy) is a UK-based independent decarbonisation developer building carbon capture, hydrogen and permanent CO2 storage infrastructure. It partnered with Carbon Engineering on a planned 0.5–1 Mt/yr DAC facility in Scotland, though the DAC project is reported as stalled and Storegga announced in late 2025 it would sell its stake in the flagship Acorn CCS project.
