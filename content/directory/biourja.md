## About

BioUrja Group is a Houston-based company that trades energy and agricultural commodities. It runs ethanol production through BioUrja Renewables, including a plant in Peoria, Illinois. With Lapis Carbon Solutions, it is exploring a project to capture CO2 from the Peoria plant, truck it to a site nearby and inject it underground.
