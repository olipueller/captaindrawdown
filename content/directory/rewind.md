## About

Rewind is a Marine CDR company that submerges agricultural and forest residues to the oxygen-free bottom of the Black Sea and in underground mines to store carbon for centuries. They aim to launch their first marine CDR project in 2025 and expand to a global network of deep mines, sediments, and marine basins.
