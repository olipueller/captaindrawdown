## About

Rewind is an Israeli biomass carbon removal and storage (BiCRS) company that sequesters surplus plant residue in anoxic environments such as deep mines and marine basins. In October 2025 it launched DMS Georgia, billed as the world's first commercial deep mine carbon storage project, 1.3 km underground in western Georgia, with over 400 tCO2e already stored and first certified credits expected in early 2026. The company targets 1 MtCO2/yr of removals by 2030 and is certified via Isometric and Puro.earth with a BlueLayer-based dMRV system.
