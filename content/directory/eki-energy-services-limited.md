## About

EKI Energy Services Limited (EnKing International) is a listed carbon credit developer, trader and climate consultancy based in India, founded in 2008. It says it has supplied more than 200 million offsets, and its services cover generating, supplying and managing carbon credits and carbon footprints. Most of its portfolio is emission-avoidance credits, not permanent CDR.
