## About

Ocean Nourishment Corporation (ONC) is an Australian marine CDR company founded in 2004 that uses ocean fertilization by adding balanced macronutrients to nutrient-poor waters to stimulate phytoplankton growth and sequester CO2. Together with its sister WhaleX Foundation, ONC is a Top 100 XPRIZE Carbon Removal team and is running small-scale biopod and photobioreactor experiments while seeking partners in green hydrogen, green ammonia, shipping, and offshore platforms.
