## About

Kairos Carbon is a UK-based CDR startup (incorporated April 2024) developing a supercritical water oxidation process that treats high-moisture biogenic waste streams, captures the resulting CO2 for permanent underground storage, and recovers phosphorus and other nutrients. The company is building a pilot facility and has received backing from Zero Carbon Capital, Grantham Foundation, Innovate UK, and a £1M Defra Farming Innovation grant. Milkywire's Climate Transformation Fund selected them as a 2025 CDR purchase.
