## About

Ozen Sp. z o.o. is a Polish charcoal and biochar manufacturer based in Wałcz that uses a patented wood-pyrolysis technology to produce charcoal, briquettes, and green electricity from pyrolysis heat. The company obtained a European Biochar Certificate (EBC) C-Sink certification for a batch produced in 2021-2022, qualifying it as a biochar CDR supplier, though its primary commercial focus remains industrial/BBQ charcoal rather than CDR credit sales.
