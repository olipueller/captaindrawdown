## About

Groundwork BioAg is an Israeli bioagriculture company headquartered in Mazor that produces Rootella® mycorrhizal fungi inoculants applied to cropland to accelerate soil carbon sequestration via Mineral-Associated Organic Matter (MAOM) formation. Its Rootella Carbon® program generates Verra VM0042 v2.0 CDR credits (validated/verified by SCS Global), and in September 2025 it announced a partnership with Anew Climate to commercialize ~500,000 tCO2e of credits over three years. Rootella inoculants are deployed on 5.5M+ acres across 23 markets.
