## About

Groundwork BioAg is an Israeli bioagriculture company producing mycorrhizal fungi inoculants (Rootella®) that, when applied to croplands, catalyze the formation of Mineral-Associated Organic Matter (MAOM) for durable soil carbon sequestration. Their Rootella Carbon® program generates Verra VM0042-verified CDR credits, with a September 2025 collaboration with Anew Climate to commercialize 500,000 tCO2e of mycorrhizal CDR credits over three years. They are an active CDR supplier, not a buyer.
