## About

Pratt Energy, LLC operates a corn and milo fuel ethanol plant in Pratt, Kansas. In late 2023 it filed an EPA Class VI application to build a CO2 sequestration well to store biogenic CO2 from ethanol fermentation, though as of late 2025 the first Kansas Class VI permit went to PureField, not Pratt. Ethanol production remains its primary business; CCS is an adjacent add-on tied to 45Q/low-carbon-fuel incentives.
