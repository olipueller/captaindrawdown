## About

Vycarb develops modular autonomous systems for ocean alkalinity enhancement, using naturally existing minerals to increase alkalinity in water and enhance the ocean's ability to store carbon in stable forms. They focus on converting CO2 in high-CO2 coastal waters into stable bicarbonate and carbonate ions.
