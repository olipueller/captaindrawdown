## About

Brooklyn-based climate tech startup founded in 2022 by Dr. Garrett Boudinot that deploys modular autonomous OAE systems to permanently store CO2 as bicarbonate in natural waters. Vycarb operates a flagship pilot at Brooklyn Navy Yard on the East River (~100 tCO2/yr) and targets integration with cement, steel, power, bioenergy and port facilities. Closed a $5M seed round in October 2025 led by Twynam with MOL Switch, Hatch Blue, Clocktower Ventures, Idemitsu, SGInnovate, and Shell.
