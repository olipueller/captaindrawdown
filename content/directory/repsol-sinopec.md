## About

Repsol Sinopec Brasil is an offshore oil and gas exploration and production joint venture (Repsol 60%, Sinopec 40%) based in Brazil. As an R&D side activity it sponsors the DAC.SI direct air capture program at PUCRS, which has a 300 t/yr pilot plant. In 2025 it signed an agreement with Port of Açu to study combining DAC with conversion of the captured CO2 into maritime and aviation fuels.
