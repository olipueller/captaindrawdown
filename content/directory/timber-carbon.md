## About

Timber Carbon (Timber Carbon Solutions AG) is a Zurich-based Swiss firm that develops methodologies and issues carbon removal credits for long-term carbon storage in mass timber buildings. It is the carbon arm of the Timber Finance group and partners with real estate developers, timber construction firms and forest managers to certify buildings and sell credits on the voluntary carbon market.
