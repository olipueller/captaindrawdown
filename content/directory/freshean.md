## About

Freshean is a Phoenix, Arizona nanotechnology startup founded in 2017 by Dr. Xiwang Qi, developing a nano-engineered solid sorbent for Direct Air Capture alongside indoor air pollution solutions. The company claims a proprietary "Nano-in-Nano" platform for improved CO2 capture efficiency and cost, and participated in the Venture for ClimateTech Cohort 4 in mid-2024 and presented at SEMICON West 2024.
