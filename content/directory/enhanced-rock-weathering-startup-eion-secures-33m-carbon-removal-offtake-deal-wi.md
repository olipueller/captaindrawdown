## About

Eion is a U.S.-based enhanced rock weathering (ERW) carbon removal supplier that spreads crushed olivine on farmland across the Midwest, South, and Mid-Atlantic. It has delivered ERW credits to Stripe and signed offtakes with Microsoft and Frontier (78,707 tons, ~$33M). In February 2026, Eion was acquired by Terradot to form a combined global ERW platform, making it a division of Terradot.
