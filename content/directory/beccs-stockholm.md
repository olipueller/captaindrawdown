## About

Beccs Stockholm is Swedish district energy provider Stockholm Exergi's large-scale Bio-Energy Carbon Capture and Storage project at their bioenergy-generation plant in Stockholm's Värtan neighbourhood. It is designed to capture 800,000 tonnes of CO2 per year, with the captured CO2 liquefied and permanently stored under the seabed where it is mineralised, with start-up planned for 2028. Funded by SEK 20 billion Swedish government support, €180 million from the EU Innovation Fund, and long-term carbon removal offtake agreements with buyers including Microsoft.
