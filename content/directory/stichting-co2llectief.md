## About

Stichting CO2llectief is a Dutch non-profit foundation with ANBI status. The Dutch CCS/CDR consultancy Darel set it up, and it builds on the earlier Sinkit cooperative. It lets consumers, energy cooperatives and employers pay a subscription (from about €10/month) to fund permanent CO2 removal projects. Its first project partner is the biochar startup Carboneers.
