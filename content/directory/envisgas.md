## About

Envigas builds, owns, and operates high-precision, large-scale pyrolysis plants to convert organic residuals into premium quality BioCarbon and energy. They produce and sell high-quality fossil-free products from a specially developed pyrolysis process using biomass such as forest residues as raw material.
