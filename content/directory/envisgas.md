## About

Envigas is a Swedish cleantech company that builds, owns and operates large-scale pyrolysis plants converting forest-industry residues into biocarbon, pyrolysis oil and renewable energy. Its existing Bureå facility produces ~5,000 t/yr of biocarbon and ~3,000 t/yr of pyrolysis oil, primarily supplying the metallurgical/steel industry as a fossil-coal replacement, with soil, asphalt and cement also cited as sink applications. In 2023 Outokumpu acquired a 20% stake (€9.9M), and in 2025 Envigas announced plans with Skellefteå Kraft to explore a >25,000 t/yr biocarbon facility in Hedensbyn.
