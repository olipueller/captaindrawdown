## About

CarbonCure Technologies is a Halifax, Canada-based carbon utilization company that licenses CO2 mineralization systems to concrete producers worldwide. Its technology injects captured CO2 into ready-mix concrete where it permanently mineralizes, and the company sells verified carbon credits generated by its global producer network. As of late 2025 its partners have delivered over 10 million truckloads of CarbonCure concrete.
