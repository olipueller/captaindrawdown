## About

Ankur Scientific Energy Technologies is a gasification and waste-to-energy equipment maker in Vadodara, India. Dr. B.C. Jain founded it in 1986. Its core business is biomass and municipal solid waste gasifiers for industrial heat and power. It brought out a pyro-gasifier/biochar generator in 2010-11, now promotes biochar and carbon sequestration, and started an initiative in 2025 to make activated carbon from biochar.
