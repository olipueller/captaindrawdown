## About

Northern Lights is a joint venture of Equinor, Shell, and TotalEnergies offering commercial CO2 transport and storage as a service from its terminal in Øygarden, Norway. It began injecting CO2 in August 2025 and has BECCS removal contracts with Stockholm Exergi (900kt/yr biogenic CO2 from 2028) and Ørsted's bio-power plant, alongside industrial CCS customers. Phase 2 FID announced in March 2025 will expand capacity from 1.5 to 5+ Mt CO2/year.
