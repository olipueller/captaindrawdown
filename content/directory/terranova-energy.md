## About

TerraNova Energy is a Düsseldorf-based technology provider and licensor that builds Hydrothermal Carbonization (HTC) and Thermal Hydrolysis (THP) plants, converting wet organic waste streams such as sewage sludge and biowaste into stable hydrochar. The company markets its TerraNova®ultra "wet pyrolysis" process as a permanent carbon removal pathway under EU Regulation 2024/3012 (CRCF), with industrial installations in Jining (China), Pau-Lescar (France, with Suez), Mexico City, and a plant under construction in Turkey.
