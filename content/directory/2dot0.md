## About

2dot0 Farms is a small Indian regenerative-agriculture enterprise in Anantapur, Andhra Pradesh. It runs a biochar production unit in Poolachetlapalli village, Gorantla mandal, and publishes a biochar playbook for farmers. Its stated mission is to improve soil health and remove carbon from the atmosphere. No registry-certified credits were found.
