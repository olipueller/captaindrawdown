## About

Cirkular Origin develops and finances biochar carbon removal projects with smallholder coffee farmers, working mainly in Colombia with its field partner Biodiversal. Coffee brands such as Libertario and Equation Coffee fund its biochar program, and the impact investor Incofin is also involved. BioFlux names it as a project developer in its work on distributed biochar production.
