## About

Snam is an Italian energy infrastructure company involved in natural gas transport and storage. They are exploring BioEnergy Carbon Capture and Storage (BECCS) and Direct Air Capture (DAC) as part of their decarbonization efforts, including considering a stake in Eni's carbon capture unit.
