## About

Pathway Energy is a Houston-based developer of carbon-negative sustainable aviation fuel (SAF) projects and a wholly-owned subsidiary of Nexus Holdings. Its first project in Port Arthur, Texas, aims to pair biomass gasification + Fischer-Tropsch SAF production with a BECCS system expected to remove ~1.9 million tonnes of CO2 annually, with construction targeted for 2026 and operations in 2029. Biomass will be supplied under a heads-of-terms agreement with Drax Group.
