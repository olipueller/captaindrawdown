## About

Natural Capital ReBank (NCRB) is building a trading platform for carbon and other natural-capital markets. It focuses on trust, transparency and trade, and turns carbon credits, biodiversity and ecosystem services into digital assets for institutional investors. Its team appears to be based in Vancouver, Canada.
