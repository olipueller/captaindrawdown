## About

Tongao Greenchar is a biochar carbon removal project developer in China. It was set up as a joint venture between Tongao Group and Greenchar Climate Solutions Pte. Ltd. (Singapore). It is one of the first three Chinese developers to sign with Isometric for certification of carbon removal credits.
