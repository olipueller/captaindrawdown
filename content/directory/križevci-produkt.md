## About

KRIŽEVCI-PRODUKT d.o.o. is a small Croatian company founded in 1992 in Križevci whose primary business is producing wood charcoal and charcoal products (grilling briquettes, metallurgical charcoal) from hardwood. They also offer CarboCris, a biochar-based animal feed additive, but have no public presence in any CDR registry or carbon-credit market.
