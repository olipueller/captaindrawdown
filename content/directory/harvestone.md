## About

Harvestone Low Carbon Partners (HLCP), a portfolio company of Energy Capital Partners formed in 2022, owns three Midwest ethanol biorefineries (Blue Flint and Dakota Spirit in North Dakota; Iroquois Bio-Energy in Indiana) producing ~220 million gallons/year. Blue Flint began sequestering biogenic CO2 in October 2023 via an on-site Class VI injection well — the third U.S. biorefinery to do so and the first post-IRA — and HLCP is developing similar CCS at Dakota Spirit (including a trucking-based transport plan) under its Vision Carbon Zero initiative.
