## About

"Transformando la Zoca en Biochar" (also called Coffee Biochar) is a community biochar project in Colombia. It is run by Biodiversal S.A.S BIC, a regenerative-agriculture and soil-biotech company, together with Circular Agro and the MRV/credit partner Planboo. It is listed on the Global C-Sink registry and received a Sylvera estimated rating of AA–BBB in late 2025. The seed website (zocacoffeecolombia.com.co) belongs to an unrelated specialty-coffee family farm in Manizales, not to this project.
