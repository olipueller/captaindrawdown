## About

Clean Air Tech is a UK company commercialising the ZAP-C³ (Zero Air Pollution Carbon Capture Capsule) and ZAP-Microbubbler reactor technology, which uses water-catalysed mineralisation to convert CO2 and alkaline industrial wastes (BOS slag, recycled concrete aggregate, fly ash) into calcium carbonate and a volumetrically stable aggregate. The company collaborates with Swansea University's Energy Safety Research Institute (Prof. Andrew Barron) on the science and reports ~309 kg CO2 sequestered per tonne of slag.
