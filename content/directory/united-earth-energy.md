## About

United Earth Energy LLC is a US waste-to-energy technology company that markets the UNI-Box mobile pyrolysis system. It pitches the unit for tire, plastic, agricultural and wood waste and for on-site power, including for AI data centers. Biochar sequestration is one of several selling points, not the core business.
