London-based Cocoon Carbon (founded 2023) converts electric arc furnace slag into LoopCem™, a scalable low-carbon supplementary cementitious material (SCM) that cuts concrete's embodied CO₂ by up to 40%. The process cools molten slag up to 100× faster than conventional granulation, producing a reliable SCM with consistent reactivity indexes. Raised $15M in March 2026 to scale.

**Category:** Industrial Process
