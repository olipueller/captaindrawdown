## About

Interholco AG is a Swiss timber company (founded 1962) that manages 1.16 million hectares of FSC-certified tropical forest in the Republic of Congo via its IFO subsidiary. It operates a biochar carbon removal project converting sawmill residues into stable carbon, with C-sink certification through the EBC / Global C-Sink Registry. While timber is its core business, the biochar project is an integrated CDR supplier operation.
