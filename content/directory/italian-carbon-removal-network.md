## About

The Italian Carbon Removal Network (Rete Italiana Rimozione Carbonio, RIRC) is Italy's first dedicated national carbon removal industry network, launched on 14 April 2026 in Padova during Padova Climate Action Week in collaboration with the Negative Emissions Platform. It serves as a coordinating industry body for CDR stakeholders in Italy, analogous to France's AFEN, Germany's DVNE, and the Nordic NCRA, promoting deployment, governance, and public understanding of carbon removal.
