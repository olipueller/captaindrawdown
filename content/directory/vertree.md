## About

Vertree is a carbon-solutions firm founded in early 2020 and accredited by ICROA. It partners with project developers to secure carbon credit supply, finances projects through equity and forward purchases, sells credits and advises clients on net-zero strategy. Its portfolio is mainly avoidance credits, not CDR, so it is at most a peripheral market intermediary.
