## About

Woodcache PBC is a U.S. public benefit corporation that buries waste woody biomass (from wildfire mitigation and forestry thinning) in engineered, cold/dry/anaerobic underground pits for durable CO2 removal. They completed their La Veta, Colorado production-scale project in 2024 with support from a 4 Corners Carbon Removal grant and sell Puro.earth-certified CORCs.
