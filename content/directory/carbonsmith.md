## About

Carbonsmith is a data-centric marketplace that facilitates transactions of high-impact Carbon Dioxide Removal (CDR) credits, powered by blockchain technology to enhance transparency in MRV and lower funding barriers. The company is based in Los Angeles, California, and is listed as an Environmental Services company with a very small team. It operates as a CDR ecosystem player (marketplace/broker) rather than a physical CDR supplier.
