## About

CO280 is a Vancouver, BC-based developer of large-scale BECCS carbon dioxide removal projects that finances, develops, owns, and operates retrofits of pulp and paper mills to capture biogenic CO2 for permanent underground storage. The company has more than 10 projects in development, with five high-priority Gulf Coast projects targeted by 2030, and signed one of the largest-ever engineered CDR offtakes — 3.685 Mt over 12 years — with Microsoft in April 2025 on top of a $48M Frontier deal.
