## About

CO280 Solutions is a Vancouver, BC-based developer of large-scale BECCS carbon removal projects that finances, develops, owns, and operates carbon capture retrofits at pulp and paper mills in the US Gulf Coast and Canada. The company has contracted over 4.3 million tonnes of CDR with buyers including Microsoft and JPMorgan Chase, and has 10+ projects in development with first deliveries expected around 2029-2030.
