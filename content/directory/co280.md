## About

Vancouver-based CO280 develops biomass carbon removal and storage projects in the pulp and paper industry, including financing, developing, owning and operating large-scale CDR projects in partnership with pulp and paper companies, and is currently developing more than 10 projects including 5 high-priority projects anticipated to be active by 2030. Microsoft signed one of the largest-ever engineered CDR purchase deals, agreeing to offtake nearly 3.7 million tonnes of carbon removal over 12 years from a CO280 project at a U.S. Gulf Coast pulp and paper mill.
