## About

AtmosClear BR, LLC is a portfolio company of Houston-based Fidelis New Energy developing a BECCS facility at the Port of Greater Baton Rouge, Louisiana. In April 2025 it signed a 15-year, 6.75 million-ton carbon removal agreement with Microsoft — at the time the largest permanent CDR contract on record — and in September 2025 selected ExxonMobil for CO2 transport and Class VI storage. Construction is planned to start in 2026 with commercial operations targeted for 2029.
