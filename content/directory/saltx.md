## About

SaltX is a Swedish greentech company listed on Nasdaq First North that develops electrification technology for emission-intensive industries such as lime and cement. Frontier awarded $2M in R&D grants to Leilac (UK) and SaltX (Sweden) to support production of zero-carbon lime on behalf of Stripe, Shopify, and Google. SaltX builds electric arc calciners using plasma torches to heat limestone, producing high-quality quicklime and releasing high-purity CO₂ for storage, with a compact low-cost reactor design.
