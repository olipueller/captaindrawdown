## About

Renual, Inc. is a startup based in San Francisco that does enhanced rock weathering in Southeast Asia, mainly Indonesia. It spreads volcanic rock on smallholder farms, says this raises crop yields by 10–30%, and sells the resulting carbon removal. It says it uses its own solid-phase MRV approach and picks rock feedstocks with high removal potential.
