## About

Carbon Limit is a Boca Raton, Florida climate tech company founded in 2020/2021 that developed CaptureCrete®, a powder additive for concrete that enables CO2 mineralization, permanently storing atmospheric CO2 in concrete as stable carbonates. The company licenses the technology to concrete producers and manages carbon credit generation and sales.
