## About

Through its Swiss subsidiary Kanadevia Inova, it supplies amine-scrubbing carbon capture technology as an EPC contractor for waste-to-energy plants — including the UK's first full-scale WtE CCS project at the Protos Energy Recovery Facility (notice-to-proceed October 2025, handover mid-2029). Carbon capture is an adjacent offering bolted onto its core WtE business rather than the company's primary purpose.
