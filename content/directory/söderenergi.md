## About

Söderenergi is a Swedish municipal energy utility (owned by Södertälje, Huddinge, and Botkyrka) that was developing a BECCS project at its Igelsta CHP plant. In August 2025 it signed an offtake deal with AstraZeneca, but in February 2026 the Board paused the BECCS project citing excessive risk exposure, insufficient financing, and a slow Nordic voluntary carbon market.
