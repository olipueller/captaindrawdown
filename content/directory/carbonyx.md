## About

Carbonyx Limited is a New Zealand carbon removal startup building a biochar project that uses forestry slash and harvest residues from commercial plantations as feedstock. The project received a pre-issuance AA rating from Sylvera and is planned to scale in phases. It is a separate company from the Vancouver-based Carbonyx.
