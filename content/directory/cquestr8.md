## About

Cquestr8 is a UK-based company that uses an industrial mineralization reactor to accelerate the weathering of alkaline minerals in the presence of CO₂, converting it into materials for construction and industrial sectors, and also capturing CO2 as bicarbonate to reverse ocean acidification.
