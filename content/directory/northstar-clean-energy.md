## About

NorthStar Clean Energy is a company that develops, owns, and operates renewable energy projects, including biomass with carbon capture and sequestration (BECCS). They plan to convert a coal-fired plant to a biomass-fueled plant capable of capturing 500,000 tons of CO2 annually.
