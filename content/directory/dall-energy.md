## About

Dall Energy is a Danish cleantech company, now part of Dall Group, that designs and supplies biomass gasification boilers (about 4–21 MW) for district heating and industrial plants in Denmark and France. Its main business is renewable heat and power equipment. Any carbon removal comes from biochar as a side product, not from the core business.
