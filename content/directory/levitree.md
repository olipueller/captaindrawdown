## About

Levitree is a San Rafael/Berkeley, California-based startup founded in 2021 that sequesters carbon by injecting wood-chip slurries 5-100 meters underground using AI-enabled robotic equipment, a technique called Subterranean Wood Injection (SWI). The process also raises the ground surface, providing a flood-protection co-benefit for infrastructure. The company was a finalist in NYU Tandon's 2025 Urban Future Prize Competition and is backed by Berkeley SkyDeck.
