## About

Varme Energy is a Canadian developer (subsidiary of Norway's Varme Energy AS) building Canada's first commercial-scale waste-to-energy facility with integrated carbon capture and storage in Strathcona County, Alberta. The AIH1 project is expected to divert ~205,000 tonnes of municipal solid waste from landfills annually and capture over 130,000 tonnes of CO₂ per year for permanent storage, targeting commercial operations in 2029.
