## About

Varme Energy is a Canadian subsidiary of Norway-based Varme Energy AS developing Canada's first commercial-scale waste-to-energy facility with integrated carbon capture and storage in Alberta's Industrial Heartland. The AIH1 project targets commercial operations in 2029 and plans to deliver approximately 130,000 tonnes/year of BECCS carbon removal credits, with Carbon Direct marketing the credits. The project has received AUC approval, Alberta government funding, and partnership commitments from Canada Growth Fund and Gibson Energy.
