## About

enfinium is a leading UK energy from waste operator with four operational sites in West Yorkshire, Kent and Flintshire, and two in construction, diverting 2.3 million tonnes of unrecyclable waste from landfill. The company is installing CCS technology on its EfW facilities — with active pilots using Kanadevia Inova (HZI) and Nuada at Ferrybridge and Parc Adfer — to turn them into net-negative carbon removal sources. enfinium's Net Zero Transition Plan targets up to 1.2 million tonnes of carbon removals annually by the 2030s, backed by an investment program of up to £1.7 billion.
