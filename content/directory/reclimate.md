## About

Reclimate is a Malaysia- and Singapore-based biochar project developer that partners with farmers and agricultural operators across Asia to convert agricultural waste into biochar, which is returned to soil and sold as carbon removal credits. The company operates projects in Malaysia (rice straw in Kedah, bamboo with Orang Asli communities via SEAD Bamboo, lemon myrtle waste) and Sri Lanka (coconut mill), and holds European Biochar Certificate / World Biochar Certificate (Carbon Standards International).
