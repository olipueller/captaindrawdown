## About

Tapovanam (Tapovanam Organic Farming Systems LLP) is an India-based biochar producer that pyrolyzes agricultural residues into biochar for soil amendment and durable carbon storage. The company is a member of CDR30 and aligns its methodology with Puro.earth and Isometric standards; it also runs an organic-produce marketplace ("Organic Tap") and an agriculture consultancy alongside its CDR activity.
