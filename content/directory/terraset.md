## About

Terraset is a US 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in October 2022 by Alex Roetter and Chris Wedding. It puts philanthropic money into buying carbon removal from early-stage startups working on enhanced weathering, ocean alkalinity, biochar and other pathways. In 2025 it launched a philanthropic revolving fund for CDR, with the Schmidt Family Foundation as anchor funder. It does not remove carbon itself.
