## About

Verdox is a Boston-based electric carbon capture company founded in 2019 out of MIT, commercializing an all-electric electro-swing adsorption process that captures CO2 using electricity instead of heat or pressure. In November 2025 it completed a successful two-month industrial trial capturing CO2 from 1%-concentration aluminum smelter off-gas at Hydro's Sunndal plant in Norway, and commissioned a pilot-scale unit in October 2025 ahead of 2026 customer pilots. The company is backed by $80M from Breakthrough Energy Ventures, Prelude Ventures, Lowercarbon Capital, and strategic investor Hydro.
