## About

Tierra Prieta (Biochar de Nogal SA de CV) is a Mexican biochar producer based in Parras, Coahuila that converts waste pecan shells into biochar via pyrolysis for soil amendment and durable carbon removal. The company operates an industrial facility with ~500 t/yr capacity inaugurated in May 2023 and sells Puro.earth-certified CORCs, with a multi-year full-capacity offtake agreement with US-based Wonderchar.
