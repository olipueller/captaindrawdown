## About

G2E (Green to Energy) is a Mexican company that sells biomass gasification plants that supply energy to rural communities and industry. Biochar is a co-product of the gasification process, and a G2E engineer co-taught an International Biochar Initiative / UNAM biochar masterclass in March 2025. The main business looks like energy from gasification, and biochar carbon removal is an add-on.
