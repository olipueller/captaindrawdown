## About

Swiss ClinX GmbH is a small GmbH founded in December 2021, based at Bernapark in Stettlen (Bern) and managed by Beat Näf. It is the Swiss partner for the ClinX power-and-heat-from-wood technology developed in Cottbus, Germany (pyropower / B+K Energy Systems). Its Pyro-ClinX project, which can also produce biochar, received a funding commitment from the Swiss Climate Foundation.
