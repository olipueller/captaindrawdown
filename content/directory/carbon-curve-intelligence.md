## About

Carbon Curve Intelligence is a carbon removal market-intelligence platform launched in October 2026 by Na'im Merchant, Executive Director of Carbon Removal Canada. It sits alongside his Substack newsletter, The Carbon Curve, and has a free tier and a paid Pro tier. It does not remove carbon itself.
