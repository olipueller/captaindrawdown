## About

Southern Green Gas (SGG) is an Australian DAC technology developer founded in 2018 and based in Southbank, Victoria. They build solar-powered DAC modules using MOF-based solid sorbents, originally partnering with AspiraDAC on a Frontier/Stripe-backed solar DAC project, and now focused on coupling DAC with e-fuel production (methanol, SAF, renewable methane). Activity is real but modest — ~5 employees and CCU-leaning, though permanent storage is still an advertised pathway.
