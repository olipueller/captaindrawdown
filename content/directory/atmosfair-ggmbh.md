## About

atmosfair gGmbH is a Berlin-based German non-profit founded in 2005 that develops and finances climate protection projects in the Global South, including a growing portfolio of biochar carbon removal projects in India, Ghana, and Ecuador. Alongside CDR project development, it operates emission calculators and sells carbon offsets/removals to corporate and individual clients, and has invested in biochar startup BIOSORRA.
