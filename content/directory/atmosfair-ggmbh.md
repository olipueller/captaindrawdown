## About

atmosfair gGmbH is a Berlin non-profit founded in 2005. It is best known for flight-emission calculators and voluntary carbon offsetting through climate-protection projects such as efficient cookstoves, renewable energy, forest protection and a power-to-liquid fuel plant. In recent years it has added a portfolio of negative-emission projects, mainly biochar, plus a DAC pilot in Kenya.
