## About

Alkamy Carbon is an early-stage startup in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region, founded by IIT Bombay alumni Rishi Rathi and Rushil Koppaka. It develops wastewater-based ocean alkalinity enhancement using a closed-system approach and sells durable carbon removal credits. It was one of eight suppliers in Milkywire's Salesforce-backed pre-purchase round.
