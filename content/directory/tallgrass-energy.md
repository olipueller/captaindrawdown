## About

Tallgrass Energy is a Blackstone-backed midstream energy company based in Leawood, Kansas. It mainly runs natural gas and crude oil pipelines and storage. Since September 2025 it has also run the Trailblazer CO2 pipeline, which moves CO2 from ethanol plants to permanent underground storage in Wyoming.
