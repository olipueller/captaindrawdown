## About

GreenChar Climate Solutions is a pioneering company that provides cutting-edge waste management solutions by converting agricultural, food, and forestry waste into biochar, which enhances soil health, boosts crop yields, and locks away carbon for centuries. They sell internationally verified Biochar Carbon Removal Credits from China.
