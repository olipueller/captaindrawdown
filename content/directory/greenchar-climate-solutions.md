## About

GreenChar Climate Solutions is a Singapore-headquartered biochar project developer operating across China that transforms organic waste into biochar and sells internationally verified biochar carbon removal credits under standards including Puro.Earth, Verra, Isometric, EBC, and CSI. The company was endorsed by Carbon Standards International as an Artisan C-Sink Manager in September 2025 and serves as the carbon accounting and certification partner for the Yangtze Delta Biochar-Based Zero-Carbon Countryside Collaborative Initiative.
