## About

Pyrogreen Energy (Wuxi Powermax Renewable Energy Technology Co., Ltd.) is a China-based manufacturer of biomass pyrolysis, torrefaction and carbonization equipment that produces biochar for carbon sequestration and renewable energy applications. They offer rotary kiln and screw conveyor carbonization systems at scales from 50kg/hr to 5MT/hr and support customers in generating biochar carbon removal (BCR) credits, including through a pre-approval by Isometric.
