## About

Removall Carbon is a France-based carbon project developer that designs, operates, and invests in nature-based carbon projects (including mangrove restoration and reforestation), with a joint venture "Summit Removall" launched with Sumitomo Corporation to scale its portfolio. The company also helps corporates finance high-integrity carbon credits. Portfolio includes both removal (restoration) and avoidance/reduction projects.
