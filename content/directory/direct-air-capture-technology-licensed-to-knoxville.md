## About

Holocene Climate Corporation is a Knoxville, Tennessee-based direct air capture company founded in 2022 that licensed ORNL's BIG (Bis-iminoguanidine) chemistry to build a low-temperature, water-based DAC process. In late 2024 it signed a record $100/ton, $10M removal deal with Google and demonstrated a ~10 t/yr pilot. In April 2025 Holocene was acquired by Occidental Petroleum's Oxy Low Carbon Ventures, joining Carbon Engineering in Oxy's DAC portfolio.
