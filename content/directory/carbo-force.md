## About

Carbo-FORCE GmbH is a pyrolysis technology company in Kassel, Germany. Malte Graf and Kai Alberding founded it in 2017, and BAUER Resources has owned 50% of it since 2023. It builds and licenses biochar pyrolysis plants for international partners, including Canadian Biochar in Western Canada and S.C. Pure Carbon Solution in Romania.
