## About

Pacific Rim Design & Development (PRDD) is an environmental engineering firm in Shingletown, California, founded in 1989. Its main business is technology for removing airborne toxics and odors and for treating industrial flue gas. Through an NOAA-funded research partnership (FY2023–2025) with Scripps, NOAA PMEL and the University of Miami, it is testing a pilot system that turns captured CO2 into carbonate salts for ocean alkalinity enhancement.
