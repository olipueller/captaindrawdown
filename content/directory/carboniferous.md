## About

Carboniferous places densified crop byproducts (biomass) in the hypersaline and anoxic Orca Basin area of the Gulf of Mexico, where the extreme environment inhibits biomass decomposition and mixing with the water column above. In March 2026, the EPA issued an MPRSA research permit for the company's research project after determining the disposition of materials would not unreasonably degrade or endanger the marine environment. The project runs through 2025–2028 including sampler development with Woods Hole, biomass placement in 2026, recovery at 8 and 14 months, and data analysis through Spring 2028.
