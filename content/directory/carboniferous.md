## About

Carboniferous is a U.S. CDR startup pioneering Marine Anoxic Carbon Storage, which places terrestrial biomass bales in oxygen-free deep ocean brine basins where decomposition is inhibited for thousands of years. The company applied for an EPA MPRSA research permit in 2024 for a 20-tonne field test in the Orca Basin, which advanced to public comment in 2025. It is aligned with the Puro.earth MACS methodology released in early 2025.
