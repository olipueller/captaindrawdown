## About

Provocative (Provocative Science Holdings) is a Boston-based startup founded in 2023 that builds carbon-negative data centers by replacing conventional cooling with direct air capture powered by server waste heat. The company is backed by Samurai Incubate, Greentown Labs, Aera VC, Unbounded Capital, and Valia Ventures, and is a Greentown Labs member. Their goal is to make AI compute carbon-negative by integrating DAC into data-center infrastructure.
