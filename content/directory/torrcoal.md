## About

TorrCoal is a Belgian torrefaction technology specialist (founded 2005, based in Dilsen-Stokkem) that was acquired by Dutch climate-tech firm Perpetual Next in September 2021 and now operates as its subsidiary. The company uses torrefaction (mild pyrolysis) to convert wood and agro-residues — including scrap wood and plastic-containing waste — into bio-carbon powder supplied to steel (ArcelorMittal), chemical and cement industries as a fossil coal replacement. Parent Perpetual Next frames the activity as carbon removal via stable bio-carbon, though a significant share of output is used as a reductant/fuel which raises CCU-vs-CDR questions.
