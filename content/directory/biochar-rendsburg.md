## About

Biochar Rendsburg GmbH (HRB 21733 KI, Kiel) was registered in May 2020 in Borgstedt near Rendsburg, Schleswig-Holstein. Its registered purpose is to build and run a Carbo-FORCE pyrolysis plant, generate energy, and make and sell biochar and biochar products. Biochar-Zero lists the plant at medium capacity (200–2,000 t). Icon-Carbon has sold some of its biochar.
