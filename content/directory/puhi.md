## About

PUHI is a Finland-based biochar and carbon removal company operating its first pyrolysis plant in Hämeenlinna's Industrial Park MORE, with an environmental permit to process up to 20,000 tons of wood- and agriculture-based side streams annually. The company produced its first biochar in March 2024, has received ~€900k in funding (including €500k from Business Finland), and sells Biochar Carbon Removal (BCR) credits alongside biochar products and district heating.
