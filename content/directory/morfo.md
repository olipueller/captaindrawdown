## About

MORFO is a Franco-Brazilian reforestation company (founded 2021) restoring degraded tropical and subtropical ecosystems, primarily in Brazil's Atlantic Forest, Amazon, and Equatorial Africa (Gabon). It combines drone seed-capsule dispersal, computer vision, and microbiology to scale large-area native-species restoration, partnering with NGOs, governments, and carbon project developers.
