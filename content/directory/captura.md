## About

Captura is a Pasadena, California-based Direct Ocean Capture company spun out of Caltech in 2021 that uses high-performance bipolar-membrane electrodialysis to extract CO2 from seawater, allowing the ocean to reabsorb additional atmospheric CO2. The company operates a 1,000 t/yr pilot in Kona, Hawaii (with Equinor) and is designing its first 30,000–50,000 t/yr commercial facility, with a 30,000-credit offtake signed by MOL.
