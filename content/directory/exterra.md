## About

Exterra Carbon Solutions is a Quebec-based cleantech company that transforms legacy asbestos mine tailings into low-carbon critical minerals (magnesium oxide, nickel concentrate, amorphous silica) while permanently mineralizing CO2. The company raised a CAD $20M Series A in May 2025 and is developing Hub I, scheduled to begin construction in 2027, which it claims will become the world's largest asbestos mitigation plant. Exterra partners with BASF, Énergir, WSP and Winsome Resources to deploy its LOW and ROC processes at industrial scale.
