## About

KuroVia GmbH is a German climate-tech startup based in Meerbusch that develops, operates and finances industrial pyrolysis projects, turning agricultural, industrial, and municipal plant residues into certified biochar, renewable process heat, and carbon removal credits. The company offers turnkey project services and markets the resulting CO₂ certificates.
