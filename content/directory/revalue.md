## About

Revalue Nature is a UK-based climate tech company (founded 2022, London) that provides end-to-end tools and services to nature-based carbon project developers, including rapid project assessment, financing, monitoring via its TERRA/Verse spatial intelligence platform (LiDAR, eDNA, ML-based Earth observation), and credit issuance support. They are a platform/service provider in the nature-based carbon ecosystem rather than a direct CO2 remover themselves. Raised ~$10M Series A in August 2023 co-led by Ecosystem Integrity Fund and SJF Ventures; total funding per PitchBook around $21M.
