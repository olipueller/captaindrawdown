## About

Varaha is a climate tech company that develops carbon dioxide removal (CDR) projects, primarily using biochar, regenerative agriculture, agroforestry, and enhanced rock weathering. They work with smallholder farmers and industrial partners in India, Bangladesh, Nepal, and Côte d'Ivoire to generate carbon credits.
