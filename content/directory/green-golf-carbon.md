## About

Green Golf Carbon is a Miami-based startup founded in 2024 by CEO Brian Morrison, a Dartmouth graduate. It sells a patented basalt top sand and soil amendment to golf course operators and turf managers to improve soil health and cut chemical inputs, and it markets the product's enhanced rock weathering as a way to remove CO2. The company came out of Dartmouth's Greenshot accelerator.
