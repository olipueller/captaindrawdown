## About

Green Golf Carbon is a Miami-based startup offering carbon sequestration for golf courses and managed turf via a patented soil amendment that replaces existing top-sands and removes CO2 through enhanced rock weathering. Co-founded and led by CEO Brian Morrison, the company deploys enhanced rock weathering to remineralize soils, reduce fertilizer dependency, and durably capture atmospheric carbon. It presented at CERAWeek 2025 and participated in the Greenshot Accelerator cohort.
