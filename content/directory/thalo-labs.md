## About

Thalo Labs develops modular direct air carbon capture (DAC) units that remove CO₂ and harmful airborne particulates from indoor air, storing the carbon in a safe, solid medium for use in building materials. They focus on building-integrated DAC for cities and offer a decarbonization platform with proprietary hardware.
