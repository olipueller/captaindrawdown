## About

Tera (Tera Carbon) is a Kenyan climate-tech startup producing biochar from sugarcane bagasse waste collected from mills in the Kisumu/Lake Victoria sugarcane belt. In 2025 it became the first African project developer independently validated on the Riverse carbon registry, and uses a custom digital MRV system (built with partner CYNK) to track carbon removal from biomass collection through pyrolysis and field application.
