## About

RubiscoBlack Environmental Technologies and Services Private Limited was incorporated on May 04, 2023 and is registered with the Registrar of Companies, Hyderabad, with active status. The company is developing microbial technologies to increase the efficiency of CDR via enhanced olivine weathering, with operations in Nacharam, Hyderabad and Madipakkam, Chennai in India.
