## About

Spiritus is a US-based climate-tech company developing low-cost Direct Air Capture using a novel solid sorbent and a passive "Carbon Orchard" contacting system with low-temperature desorption. The company raised a $30M Series A in March 2025 led by Aramco Ventures, and is building a 1,000-ton/year pilot in New Mexico and a 2 Mt/year "Orchard One" DAC+S facility in Wyoming.
