## About

Premier Forest Products is a UK timber importer and distributor based in South Wales that also operates a pyrolysis facility converting wood waste into biochar bricks. The biochar project is registered under the Puro.earth biochar methodology and issues CO2 Removal Certificates (CORCs). Biochar is one revenue stream within a larger timber/panel-products business.
