## About

MK Collect is an Australian startup spun out of the University of Melbourne that is developing low-energy direct air capture technology based on nanocatalysts and green solvents. It works with the university's Department of Chemical Engineering and the engineering firm INNACO on a project to scale up the technology. That project received A$1.6M in government funding and aims to turn the captured CO2 into carbonate-based rock.
