## About

Carbonex is a French charcoal and biocarbon maker founded in 1993 and based in Gyé-sur-Seine (Aube). It sells biocarbon, biocoal, charcoal and biochar made from wood and biomass to the metallurgy, construction-materials and agriculture sectors, and it also produces renewable electricity. Biochar for soil or materials is only one product line. Most of its output is charcoal and reductant that gets burned, so carbon removal is a side activity.
