## About

Wild Asia is a social enterprise based in Kuala Lumpur, founded in 2003. Its main work is the Wild Asia Group Scheme, which helps smallholder palm oil farmers get certified and move to regenerative farming (WAGS BIO). It also produces certified biochar carbon-sink credits with smallholders in Sabah, backed by partners such as EXIM Bank Malaysia and CIMB.
