## About

Carbonity is a joint venture between Airex Energy, Groupe Rémabec, and SUEZ that inaugurated Canada's first industrial-scale biochar plant in Port-Cartier in May 2025, starting at 10,000 tonnes/year and planning to triple capacity by 2026 to become the largest such facility in North America. Carbonity generates certified carbon credits offered on the voluntary market via First Climate and has secured a deal with Microsoft to purchase 36,000 credits over the first three years of operations.
