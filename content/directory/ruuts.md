## About

Ruuts is an Argentina-based regenerative agriculture platform that co-developed the SARA programme with Anthesis Group and local partners Ovis 21, De Raiz and Efecto Manada, supporting more than 130 farms across roughly 150,000 hectares of rangeland in Argentina, Paraguay and Chile. SARA's first verified monitoring period (2019–2023) resulted in issuance of more than 200,000 carbon removal credits under Verra's VM0042 methodology. Across Ruuts programs, over 400 producers managing more than 1.3 million hectares have adopted regenerative practices, with over 400,000 hectares under signed carbon contracts.
