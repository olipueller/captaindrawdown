## About

Grain Ecosystem was a Boston-based digital platform (founded 2022) providing software and workflows for biochar project developers, connecting operators, investors, and equipment manufacturers in the voluntary carbon market. It did not physically produce biochar itself — it offered financing tools, equipment selection software, and project development infrastructure. In April 2026, Mangrove Systems acquired select operating assets from Grain Ecosystem, folding its operator customer base onto Mangrove's MRV platform.
