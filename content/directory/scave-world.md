## About

Scave is a Netherlands-based company that converts agricultural land into carbon sinks through regenerative agroforestry, working with local farmers to plant trees on farmland. They sell carbon removal credits accounted under the ONCRA (Open Natural Carbon Removal Accounting) framework.
