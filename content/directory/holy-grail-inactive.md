## About

Holy Grail is a Mountain View, California DAC startup (YC S19) developing modular electrochemical CO2 scrubbers that capture atmospheric CO2 using electricity and store it as solid mineralized bricks above ground. Its pilot facility, Project Moonstone in Fremont, is certified by Puro.earth with a plan to deliver 2,000 tonnes from pilot in 2025–2026 and 100,000 tonnes from industrial deployment in 2027. Backed by Lowercarbon Capital, Y Combinator, Goat Capital, Starlight Ventures and angels including Patrick Collison.
