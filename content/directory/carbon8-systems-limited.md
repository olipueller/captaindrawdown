## About

Carbon8 Systems was a UK-based CCUS/mineralization company spun out of the University of Greenwich in 2006, deploying its ACT technology at cement and energy-from-waste plants with clients including Vicat, Hanson and AVR. The company was placed into administration on 12 November 2025 with Quantuma appointed as administrator, after cashflow difficulties and unsuccessful investment efforts.
