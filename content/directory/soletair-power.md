## About

Soletair Power is a Finnish DAC technology supplier (founded 2016) that integrates carbon capture modules into building HVAC systems, turning commercial buildings into distributed CO2 scrubbers. Recent deployments include Wärtsilä's Sustainable Technology Hub (Vaasa), a factory in Vantaa, and Danica Pension's building in Aarhus, Denmark; in December 2024 they partnered with Carbonaide to deliver captured CO2 for net-negative concrete.
