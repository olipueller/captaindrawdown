## About

North Star Carbon Solutions LP is a Saskatchewan-based BECCS project developer, a joint venture between the Meadow Lake Tribal Council (MLTC) and Svante Technologies (via its subsidiary Carbon Alpha). The project will capture biogenic CO₂ at the MLTC Bioenergy Centre (fed by sawmill waste biomass) and permanently store it in a saline aquifer southwest of Meadow Lake, targeting ~90,000 tonnes/year with commercial operation planned for 2029.
