## About

Silk Grass Farms is a certified B Corp agribusiness in southern Belize that grows and processes coconut and other tropical produce. Working with ClimeCo, it is developing a biochar carbon project from its farm waste and runs a Verra-registered REDD+ forest conservation project that supports the Silk Grass nature reserve. Farming and food processing are its main business. Biochar is an add-on to that work.
