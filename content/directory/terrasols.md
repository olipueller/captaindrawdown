## About

Terrasols (Terrasols Solutions) is an India-based enhanced rock weathering startup that applies crushed basalt to smallholder farmers' fields to remove CO₂ while improving soil fertility. The company is part of the remove. Carbon Removal Accelerator cohort and positions itself as a sustainability consultancy offering carbon credits alongside its ERW deployments.
