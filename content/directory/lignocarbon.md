## About

LignoCarbon Schweiz AG is a Swiss biochar producer based in Illnau-Effretikon, Zurich, operating a pyrolysis facility that converts wood into biochar products. The company was founded in 2020 and remains active in the Swiss commercial register, with its stated purpose being the charring/pyrolysis of wood and the processing, refining, storage, transport and trading of various coal products.
