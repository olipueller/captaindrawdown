## About

Woodtek Engineering Ltd is a family-run engineering and fabrication company in Welshpool, Mid Wales. It makes wood chip dryers, biomass energy equipment and biochar pyrolysis plants. Its units run at the Jones family's Puro-listed sister company Carbon Hill, at Brodie Biomass, and in the Shropshire Council / Biodynamic Carbon biochar project.
