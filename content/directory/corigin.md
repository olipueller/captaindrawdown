## About

Corigin Solutions is a US-based company that converts agricultural residues (almond shells, orchard trimmings, nutshells) into biochar and bio-oil via vacuum pyrolysis. Their Corichar biochar is sold as a soil amendment, and their bio-oil is supplied to Charm Industrial for deep geological sequestration. They are a standalone CDR supplier, not a subsidiary.
