## About

SCW Systems is a Dutch climate-tech scale-up (founded 2013, ~89 employees) headquartered in Alkmaar that builds and operates the world's first industrial supercritical water gasification facility in partnership with Gasunie, producing green gas from waste while capturing biogenic CO2. It is also developing a parallel Direct Air Capture technology branded CO2 CleanUp that permanently stores CO2 in geological formations or converts it into feedstocks like sustainable cement. The company is backed by Invest-NL and PGGM/PFZW.
