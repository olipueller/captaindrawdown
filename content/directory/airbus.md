## About

Airbus, through its Defence and Space division, has commercialized a modular Direct Air Capture unit derived from International Space Station life-support technology, using a solid amine-based filter with thermal regeneration. In November 2025, Airbus delivered a 250 ton/year DAC unit to Deep Sky Alpha in Alberta, Canada, marking its first operational deployment. Airbus also purchases DAC credits from 1PointFive (400,000 tonnes pre-purchase) and resells them to airlines via its Carbon Capture Offer.
