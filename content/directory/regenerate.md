## About

ReGenerate Livermore Falls, LLC runs a biomass power plant in Livermore Falls, Maine. It burns low-value wood residues from local logging and wood-products industries and generates about 284,000 MWh of electricity a year. It also collects biochar from the plant, uses it as landfill cover, and sells carbon removal credits through Puro.earth.
