## About

Holocene is a Knoxville, Tennessee-based direct air capture startup founded in 2022 by former Climeworks engineers, using a liquid-based, low-temperature thermochemical process with amino acids and guanidine compounds (licensed from Oak Ridge National Laboratory). In September 2024 it signed a landmark $100/ton, 100,000-ton carbon removal deal with Google, and in April 2025 it was acquired by Occidental Petroleum, which is integrating the technology with its Carbon Engineering/1PointFive DAC business.
