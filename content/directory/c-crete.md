## About

C-Crete Technologies is a California-based materials science company that produces a cement-free concrete binder using pulverized non-carbonate rocks instead of Portland cement, eliminating clinker emissions and sequestering ambient CO2 during curing. Founded in 2010 out of MIT research, the company had poured over 3,000 tonnes of its product across commercial, government and residential projects (JPMorganChase HQ, SFO, MnDOT I-94) by early 2025 and received $10.4M in grants from the California Energy Commission and US DOE.
