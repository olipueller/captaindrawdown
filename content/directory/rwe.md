## About

RWE is a major German energy utility (~46.8 GW generation capacity) whose BECCUS initiative at its Dutch Amer and Eemshaven biomass-co-fired power plants is planned to deliver 11–14 Mt/yr of negative emissions from 2030, subject to technical and economic feasibility. The project is still in permitting/development with no final investment decision announced, and RWE's broader 2025–2030 capex plan has been reduced by €10B. CDR is a small part of RWE's overall business, but they would be the physical operator of the BECCS plants.
