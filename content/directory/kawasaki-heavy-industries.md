## About

Kawasaki Heavy Industries is a large Japanese industrial conglomerate that makes motorcycles, ships, aerospace, rail and energy equipment. Its Kawasaki CO2 Capture (KCC) business develops solid-sorbent DAC. In late 2025 it ran one of Japan's largest DAC pilots at its Kobe Works, and it aims to commercialize around 2030 with Southeast Asia as a target market.
