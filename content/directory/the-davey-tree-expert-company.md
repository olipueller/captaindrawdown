## About

The Davey Tree Expert Company is a large employee-owned tree care, landscaping and environmental services firm based in Kent, Ohio, working across the US and Canada. In February 2024 it opened a biochar facility in East Dundee, Illinois, that turns wood waste from client properties into a soil additive. Biochar is a small side activity next to its main tree care and utility vegetation business.
