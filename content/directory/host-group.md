## About

HoSt Group (HoSt Bioenergy Systems) is a Dutch engineering company founded in 1991 and based in Enschede. It supplies biogas, biomethane and biomass energy plants across Europe and other regions. Bioenergy plant construction is its core business, and CDR is at most an adjacent activity via biogenic CO2 capture.
