## About

323ppm is a small French consultancy (bureau d'étude) focused on accelerating biochar adoption in France by advising farmers, municipalities, and companies. The firm does not operate pyrolysis itself but collaborates with certified producers and technical partners to ensure the quality and efficacy of the biochar it recommends, and reports working on a first pilot project with a regional farmer.
