## About

323ppm is a small French consultancy (bureau d'étude) dedicated to developing the biochar market in France. It does not operate pyrolysis equipment itself; rather, it advises farmers, municipalities and companies on biochar applications and sources material from certified producers. The company reports working on its first pilot project with a regional farmer.
