## About

Cool Points Club is a consumer-facing climate brand co-founded by Leanne Bats and Jussara Bierman that sells subscription products (e.g. "Cool Gram" for Instagram) and NFT projects ("Islands of Cool"), using the proceeds to fund third-party durable carbon removal projects such as DAC, mineralisation, biomass and ocean CDR. The company does not operate removal technology itself; it acts as a retail aggregator channeling consumer/brand spend to CDR suppliers. Public activity and web presence appear largely dormant since 2022.
