## About

Cool Points Club is a consumer climate brand co-founded by Leanne Bats and Jussara Bierman (Australia/New Zealand), offering a 'negative carbon rewards' program. It funds emerging carbon removal technologies through a $5/month Instagram subscription and an NFT collection, routing revenue to third-party removal partners.
