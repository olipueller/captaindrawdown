## About

Eion was a U.S.-based enhanced rock weathering (ERW) carbon removal company that applied crushed olivine on agricultural fields to permanently sequester atmospheric CO2 while improving soil pH. It pioneered olivine-based deployments and a patented soil-tracer MRV approach, delivering ERW credits to Stripe and contracting removal with Microsoft. In February 2026, Terradot acquired Eion's assets, contracts (100,000+ tons), IP, and team, effectively ending Eion as a standalone entity.
