## About

Carbon Utility synthesizes PAN precursors from captured CO2 and green H2 to create CO2-derived carbon fiber, offering a high-performance material with a cleaner input stream. They also provide zero-carbon food-grade CO2 and green H2 for power generation and HFC vehicles.
