## About

APD (Auen Pflege Dienst AG) is a Swiss company based in Flaach, Zürich, that produces biochar via certified pyrolysis plants using local biomass feedstocks. It is one of the earliest biochar carbon-credit projects (operating since 2019) and partners with First Climate for credit issuance, with annual audits by Carbon Standards International (EBC certification).
