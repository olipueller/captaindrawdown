## About

SPSC GmbH is a small German engineering company in Ottobrunn near Munich. It builds and sells the VARIO series of biochar pyrolysis plants to farms, forestry businesses and industrial customers. It is listed as a technology-provider partner of Carbonfuture, which supports carbon-removal crediting from its plants.
