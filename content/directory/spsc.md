## About

SPSC GmbH is a small German engineering and plant construction company based in Ottobrunn that designs and sells the VARIO S/L/XS/XL series of biochar pyrolysis retorts for agricultural, forestry, and industrial customers. The firm also provides engineering, sizing, permitting, and turnkey delivery services, and partners with Carbonfuture to enable carbon removal crediting for projects using its technology. It was incorporated in 2016 and remains an active registered company, though public activity signals have been limited since 2024.
