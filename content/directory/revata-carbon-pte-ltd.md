## About

Revata Carbon is a Singapore-incorporated biochar project developer that converts agricultural waste into biochar via controlled gasification for durable carbon removal. Its flagship project is a partnership with Varaha and Valency International in Côte d'Ivoire to convert cashew shell waste into biochar, with credits issued through Puro.earth. Revata handles in-country development and operation of the gasification units.
