## About

Rainforest Builder is a UK-headquartered tropical reforestation company founded in 2022, operating in Sierra Leone, Ghana and Guinea with four active projects targeting 370,000+ hectares of restoration. In February 2026 it signed a 15-year, 1.8M-credit offtake with Microsoft from Project Buffalo (Sierra Leone), and in March 2026 closed a Series A led by BNP Paribas AM Alts with parallel project financing for Project Colobus in Ghana. The company owns the full restoration stack — nurseries, planting operations, MRV (Chameleon data platform), and carbon project development.
