## About

Atlantic Biochar SARL AU is an early-stage biochar carbon removal developer based in Casablanca, Morocco. A Swiss Capital Advisory SA is developing it as a dedicated biochar platform. Its first unit, at Agropolis Meknès, is due to start up in Q1 2027. The company targets 15,000–20,000 tCO2e of removals a year and plans to expand across Morocco and West Africa.
