## About

AlliedOffsets is a London-based carbon market intelligence company. It runs a database of voluntary carbon market projects, prices, transactions and corporate buyers. It also publishes market reports, including an annual State of the Market report on CDR and sector reports such as soil carbon. It does not remove carbon itself.
