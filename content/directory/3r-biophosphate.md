## About

3R-BioPhosphate Ltd. specializes in zero-emission, energy-independent pyrolysis engineering to recover phosphorus and produce industrial-scale biochar, specifically Animal Bone Char (ABC) BioPhosphate, for agricultural use and carbon removal.
