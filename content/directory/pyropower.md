## About

pyropower GmbH is a German cleantech engineering firm based in Cottbus that develops decentralized pyrolysis combined heat and power systems producing biochar, green electricity and heat from wood and biogenic residues. Their flagship Pyro-ClinX plant pairs pyrolysis with an air-bearing micro gas turbine and has been deployed in reference projects including Darmstadt and Görlitz.
