## About

EcoUp Oyj is a Finnish construction materials and circular-economy group listed in Helsinki, with about EUR 29M revenue in 2025. Its businesses include Ekovilla cellulose insulation, Cubecos geopolymer products and WasteX recycling technology. Since 2019 it has sold Puro.earth CO2 removal certificates (CORCs) based on the carbon stored in its Ekovilla insulation, but carbon removal is a small side activity.
