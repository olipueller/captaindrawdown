## About

Agricarbon is a soil carbon measurement company based in Dundee, Scotland. It does direct soil sampling and automated lab analysis for food and beverage companies, carbon project developers and natural capital investors in the UK, Europe and other regions. It does not remove carbon itself. It supplies the measurement data that soil carbon removal claims rely on.
