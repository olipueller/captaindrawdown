## About

Carbon Lock Tech is a Manitoba-based cleantech start-up developing systems and processes for removing carbon dioxide from the atmosphere and sequestering it durably, using a patented prototype reactor that converts biomass pellets into stable biochar. With a team of six and plans to grow, the company is preparing for its first commercial pilot in a southern Manitoba community, where it plans to install a community-based biochar facility that processes local organic waste into carbon-sequestering products while generating carbon credits. The company has signed agreements with AirMiners and Shopify to sell CDR credits generated by its technology.
