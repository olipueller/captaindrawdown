## About

Pure Carbon Blue is a Dutch startup developing Direct Water Capture (DWC) technology that extracts CO2 from water bodies (canals, ocean), exploiting the fact that CO2 concentration in water is ~100x higher than in air. Captured CO2 is either used as bio-carbon feedstock for fuels, food, and materials (CCU) or routed to permanent storage (CDR). As of early 2025, the first pilot in Dutch canals was at TRL 5, with ambitions to scale to 1 Gt/year by 2030.
