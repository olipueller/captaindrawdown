## About

BLOCK Biochar, operated by two brothers in Dithmarschen, Germany, transforms local hedgerow and waste wood into high-quality biochar for carbon removal. BLOCK Bio Innovationen GmbH & Co. KG is a German consultancy specialized in biochar project development.
