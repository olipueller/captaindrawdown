## About

BLOCK Pflanzenkohle (BLOCK Bio Innovationen GmbH & Co. KG) is a family-run German biochar producer in Osterrade, Dithmarschen, Schleswig-Holstein, founded by brothers Mathis and Steffen Block. Their facility is designed to produce roughly 550 tonnes of biochar per year from ~2,000 tonnes of regional biomass, with waste heat feeding a local district heating network. Carbon removal credits from the project are distributed via Klimate.co.
