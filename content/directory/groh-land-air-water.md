## About

Groh Land Air & Water specializes in transformative environmental solutions, harnessing the power of natural alternatives such as biochar, beneficial microbes, bio-polymers, and nanobubbles for sustainable agriculture and environmental management. Their primary CDR method is biochar, which they incorporate into soil to improve structure, enhance water retention, promote nutrient availability, and sequester carbon.
