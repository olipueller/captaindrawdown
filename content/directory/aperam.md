## About

Aperam BioEnergia is the Brazilian forestry/renewable-energy subsidiary of global stainless-steel producer Aperam, historically supplying charcoal to Aperam's Timóteo steel plant and, since 2022, marketing biochar-based carbon removal credits. It is Puro.earth-certified and one of the largest biochar CDR suppliers globally, producing ~10,000 t biochar/yr in 2025 with plans to scale further. The company has sold 100,000+ tonnes of CDR credits, mostly through Puro.earth.
