## About

G6T Group is a UAE-based firm, co-founded and led by Dr. Harald Freissmuth, that calls itself an independent think tank developing business models for waste-to-value and carbon dioxide removal technologies. In 2025 it agreed with UK-based Kyoto Network to co-develop waste-to-value plants in the UAE. The first project is meant to use engineered biochar for soil improvement and carbon credits.
