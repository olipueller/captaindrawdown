## About

Uisa is a large Brazilian integrated sugarcane and corn biorefinery based in Nova Olímpia, Mato Grosso, producing sugar, ethanol, renewable electricity and yeast. The company announced plans in 2023 to add BECCS at its Nova Olímpia corn-to-ethanol plant, targeting up to 200,000 carbon credits per year via CO2 injection into the Parecis Basin, though public progress on the project has been limited compared to peer FS Agrisolutions.
