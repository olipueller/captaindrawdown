## About

Uisa is one of Brazil's largest integrated biorefineries, producing sugar, ethanol, renewable energy, and yeast from sugarcane at its Nova Olímpia plant in Mato Grosso. The company announced BECCS plans to capture CO2 from its corn-to-ethanol operations and inject it into the Parecis Sedimentary Basin ~15 km from the plant, with drilling scheduled for 2024 and construction for 2025. The project is still in pre-operational development and depends on Brazil's CCS regulatory framework.
