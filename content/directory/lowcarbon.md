## About

LowCarbon is a South Korean climate-tech company founded in 2016 in Gangjin County, Jeollanam-do, developing Direct Air Capture ("Zero C" / "carbon tree") and CCUS technologies, including its KLC catalyst that converts captured CO2 into carbonates. The company has signed MOAs with Florida (Space Florida, Governor DeSantis) for DACCS supply and clean hydrogen hub development. CEO Lee Chul remains publicly active as of September 2025.
