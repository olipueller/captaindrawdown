## About

Carbonapp is a France-based multi-sectoral carbon project developer and offset platform founded in 2020, operating as a mandated developer (mandataire) that sources projects and shepherds them through certification under standards such as Label Bas-Carbone, Puro.earth, Riverse and CSI. It does not itself produce biochar or physically remove CO2; its sister company DEMIO produces biochar, while Carbonapp structures and markets the resulting credits to corporate buyers. It sits in the CDR ecosystem as a developer/intermediary rather than a supplier of removals.
