## About

Reverse Carbon is a Swedish climate tech company based in Stockholm, founded by Kenneth Möllersten and Jenny Fellenius, with a project team in Nairobi, Kenya. The company plans to produce biochar via pyrolysis of agri-industrial biomass residues in developing countries and sequester it in deep biochar repositories in closed mining lands as part of eco-restoration. It issues biochar carbon removal (BCR) credits but remains small-scale with limited public activity since 2024.
