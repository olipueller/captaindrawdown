## About

Reverse Carbon is a Stockholm-based CDR startup founded in 2020 by Jenny Fellenius and Kenneth Möllersten, with a project team in Nairobi, Kenya. It combines large-scale biochar production from agri-industrial residues with permanent sequestration in disused mining sites and ecological restoration projects, issuing biochar carbon removal credits.
