## About

Treehouse California Almonds is a California almond grower, processor and supplier of almond ingredients to food companies in more than 50 countries. It partnered with biochar maker Sitos Group on a roughly $9–10M biochar plant in Delano, CA, that uses Treehouse's almond-shell waste. The plant produced its first biochar load in October 2026. Sitos develops and runs the site, and almonds remain Treehouse's core business.
