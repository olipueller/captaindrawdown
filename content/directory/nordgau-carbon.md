## About

Nordgau Carbon GmbH & Co. KG is a farm-based biochar producer in Wernberg-Köblitz, Bavaria. It has been producing since 2020 and makes and sells biochar for animal, soil and garden uses. Its facility is listed on Puro.earth, and Accend AS handles credit sales.
