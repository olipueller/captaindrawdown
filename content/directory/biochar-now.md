## About

Biochar Now is a biochar producer focused on production and sale of biochar, with products serving specialty agriculture, reclamation, oil and gas, odor control, and nutrient removal; it was founded in 2011 and is based in Berthoud, Colorado. The company has spent years developing patented manufacturing and emissions-compliance technologies that turn waste wood into high-quality carbon for a diverse customer base.
