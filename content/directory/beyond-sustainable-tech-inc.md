## About

Beyond Sustainable Tech (BST) is a small Canadian company based in Kedgwick, New Brunswick that develops biochar-based composite materials for packaging and construction. Its products combine biochar with non-recyclable waste feedstocks to create carbon-negative packaging and building components that sequester CO2. BST operates under the Beyond Sustainable Corporation umbrella, which includes affiliate brands in housing, wellness, and analytics.
