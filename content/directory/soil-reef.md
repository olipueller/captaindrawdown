## About

Soil Reef LLC (formerly The Biochar Company) is a Berwyn, Pennsylvania-based biochar product development, marketing and sales firm founded in 2011 that sells pure biochar and biochar-compost blends for gardening, agriculture, and mine/land reclamation. The company merged with Biochar Solutions Inc. in 2014 and distributes products online and through garden retailers. It operates as a very small entity (reported ~1-10 employees and ~$200K revenue) with no public involvement in durable carbon credit registries.
