## About

Accend is a Norwegian consultancy/broker (founded 2020, based in Fredrikstad) that helps biochar and mass-timber producers in Europe and North America qualify for Puro.earth CORC certification through LCA services, and then brokers the resulting carbon removal credits to corporate buyers. They do not operate pyrolysis plants themselves — their clients (OBIO, Biopower Tana, Moelven) are the physical CDR suppliers.
