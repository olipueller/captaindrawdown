## About

Aerial is a San Francisco-based consumer app and platform that tracks users' carbon emissions from travel, NFTs, and cryptocurrency activity and routes contributions to offset/removal projects. It acts as a broker/retailer, channeling funds to partners such as forest conservation projects and Charm Industrial rather than performing removal itself. Activity has been limited since its 2021 seed round.
