## About

Aerial is a San Francisco-based consumer carbon-footprint tracking and offset app founded around 2020 by Andreas Homer, Ari Sawyers and Ebby Amir. It sells credits from partner projects (largely forest conservation via ACR/CAR registries, plus some removal such as Charm Industrial) and has done brand partnerships such as Etihad's NFT collection. It does not remove carbon itself.
