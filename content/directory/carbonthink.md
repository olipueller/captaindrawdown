## About

CarbonThink is a biochar-based carbon dioxide removal supplier operating in Thailand, working with local farmers and co-ops to convert agricultural waste biomass (including bamboo offcuts and hemp) into biochar via pyrolysis. The company sells CDR certificates and partners with organizations such as Planboo and Wongphai to scale artisanal biochar production in rural Thai communities.
