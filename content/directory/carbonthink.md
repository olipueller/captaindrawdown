## About

CarbonThink is a Thai biochar carbon removal developer that works with local farmers and cooperatives to turn crop and bamboo waste into biochar. With Planboo, it ran a bamboo-offcut biochar pilot in Prachinburi, central Thailand, starting in 2023. Thailand's first biochar CDR project, registered with Carbon Standards International, has been reported as theirs.
