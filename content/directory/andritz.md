## About

ANDRITZ is a listed industrial technology group based in Graz, Austria. It supplies plants and equipment for pulp and paper, hydropower, metals and environmental applications. Carbon capture for biogenic CO2 is a small add-on to its main engineering business. It supplied the carbon capture pilot plant at Metsä Group's Rauma pulp mill, which started up in 2025.
