## About

Lillianah Technologies is a Houston-based ocean CDR startup that cultivates native diatoms in photobioreactors and disperses them into nutrient-polluted nearshore dead zones (primarily on the Gulf Coast) to drive carbon sequestration via marine sedimentation. It is an IndieBio/SOSV portfolio company founded by Ben Slotnick that pitches its removal approach as significantly cheaper than direct air capture.
