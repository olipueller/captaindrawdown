## About

Húmica is a Mexican soil-biotechnology startup founded in 2020 that works with smallholder farmers — particularly women producers in Puebla, Veracruz, Tabasco, and Oaxaca — to produce tailored biochar and transition citrus, vanilla, bamboo, and honey operations to regenerative agroforestry. The company conducts soil diagnostics, formulates biochar-based biofertilizers, and helps farmers access carbon-credit markets.
