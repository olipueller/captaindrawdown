## About

Verde Resources (OTCQB: VRDR) is a US-listed sustainable infrastructure company that produces BioAsphalt™, incorporating engineered biochar into road construction materials to create durable carbon sinks. The company generated the first Puro.earth-verified carbon removal credits from an asphalt application (8 tons) via its NCAT pilot, and in March 2026 signed a supply deal with Biochar Solutions LLC for up to 38,500 tons of biochar annually, alongside a $2M strategic investment from Ergon.
