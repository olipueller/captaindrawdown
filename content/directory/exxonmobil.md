## About

ExxonMobil is a major integrated oil and gas company whose Low Carbon Solutions division operates a proprietary DAC pilot (started February 2024 in Baytown, TX) after ending its partnership with Global Thermostat. The division also runs large-scale point-source CCS operations leveraging Denbury's CO2 pipeline infrastructure, with CEO Darren Woods calling DAC the "holy grail" of emissions technologies while targeting a 50% cost reduction from the current $600-$1,000/ton range.
