## About

Deep Sky is a Canadian project developer focused on scaling direct air capture (DAC) and direct ocean capture (DOC) technologies. They develop and operate carbon removal facilities, including a test facility (Deep Sky Alpha) and an innovation center (Deep Sky Labs), and partner with various DAC technology providers.
