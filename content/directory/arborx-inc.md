## About

ArborX, Inc. is a small biochar startup in Naples, Florida, led by President Chris Borgeson. It turns woody green waste into biochar for soil, filtration and carbon credits. In January 2025 Collier County approved incentives for a planned $2.5M headquarters and first plant in Immokalee, with 14 jobs expected. It is not related to Arbor Energy.
