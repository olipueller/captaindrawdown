## About

reverce is a German enhanced rock weathering (ERW) startup spun out of Carbon Drawdown Initiative GmbH, launched in 2023 with a mission focused on end-to-end ERW operations and direct-measurement MRV. The company was involved in Project Carbdown in Germany and the Olivine Project in Greece, collaborating with farmers to tailor rock materials, measure CO2 removal, and generate carbon removal credits. Founding CEO Klaus Gumpp left in early 2025, and as of February 2026 the company is led by a new management team (Ralph Koczwara, Andre Philipp Perzl, Alexander Schmid).
