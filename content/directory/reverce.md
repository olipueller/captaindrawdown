## About

reverce is a German enhanced rock weathering (ERW) startup that launched in 2023, spun out of Carbon Drawdown Initiative GmbH, with a focus on direct-measurement MRV and an end-to-end operational standard for ERW carbon removal. The company collaborates with farmers to tailor rock materials, measure CO2 removal in field trials, and generate carbon removal credits, with participation in Project Carbdown in Germany and the Olivine Project in Greece.
