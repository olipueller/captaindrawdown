## About

Stockholm Exergi is a Swedish energy utility company that is building and operating a large-scale Bioenergy Carbon Capture and Storage (BECCS) facility at its bioenergy-generation plant in Stockholm's Värtan neighborhood to capture and permanently store biogenic CO2.
