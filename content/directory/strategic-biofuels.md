## About

Strategic Biofuels is a US project developer advancing the Louisiana Green Fuels (LGF) project, a BECCS facility that will burn regional forestry waste to generate 75 MW of dispatchable electricity while capturing and sequestering roughly one million tonnes of CO2 per year in deep saline formations. The project recently received a draft Class VI well permit and has a partnership with Carbon Direct to supply durable carbon removal credits to Carbon Direct's clients.
