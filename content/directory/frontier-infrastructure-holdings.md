## About

Frontier Infrastructure Holdings (formerly Frontier Carbon Solutions) is a Tailwater Capital-backed developer of carbon capture and storage infrastructure headquartered in Irving/Dallas, Texas. The company operates the Sweetwater Carbon Storage Hub in southwest Wyoming — holding Class VI permits, drilling the deepest Class VI well in the U.S. (18,437 ft), and building a CO2-by-rail "Stack to Sequestration" model that targets BECCS credits from Midwest ethanol producers via partnerships with Gevo/Verity, Union Pacific, and Baker Hughes. Signed a 120,000-tonne BECCS offtake agreement with Wild Assets in June 2025.
