## About

CAESG (Central Asia ESG Platform) is an early-stage biochar carbon removal developer that targets cotton-stalk residue in Kazakhstan's Turkestan Region. It recently registered an operating company in Turkestan and presented the project to the regional investment council for the Turan special economic zone. Its plant has not been built yet and it has produced no removals so far.
