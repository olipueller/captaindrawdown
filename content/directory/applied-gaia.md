## About

Applied Gaia Corporation is a Houston, Texas-based pyrolysis technology manufacturer that designs, builds and sells modular continuous-pyrolysis machines (the B3 and larger B5) for converting biomass into biochar, wood vinegar, wood tar and syngas. The company also assists clients with registration and sale of CO2 Removal Certificates (CORCs) from biochar production. Its B3 unit is deployed at the ReGen Monterey waste management facility in California.
