## About

Biochar Innovations Limited is a small UK startup founded in 2022 (Companies House no. 14173416, registered in Halifax, England), with ties to North Berwick, Scotland. With Innovate UK support and the University of Edinburgh's School of Geosciences, it has developed a biochar-based, carbon-negative fertiliser. In 2023 it raised about £116k from Apollo investors, matched by a £316k Innovate UK grant.
