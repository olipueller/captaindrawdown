## About

Dair Capture is a U.S.-based DAC startup commercializing technology developed within Los Alamos National Laboratory, backed by three pending U.S. patents, that produces clean water as a byproduct and claims carbon capture for sequestration, storage, and utilization at rates 10x cheaper than current technologies. The company markets captured CO2 into verticals like cement, healthcare, and beverages, framing its offer as a circular-economy CO2 supply. Public footprint is thin: no registry issuances, no funding announcements in 2024-2025, and 0 tracked LinkedIn employees.
