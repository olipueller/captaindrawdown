## About

Climate Vault Solutions is a Chicago-based CDR ecosystem company (spun out of a University of Chicago nonprofit) that combines compliance-market carbon allowance vaulting with a curated portfolio of carbon removal credits sourced via its Tech Chamber RFP program (chaired by former U.S. Energy Secretary Ernest Moniz). It resells CDR credits from vetted suppliers (e.g., Octavia Carbon, Carbon Lockdown) through its sales channel and Climate Solutions Platform software to corporate clients. It does not itself remove CO2; it is a curator/broker/platform.
