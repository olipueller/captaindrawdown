## About

Founded in 2021, Bakz4ever is an early-stage Indian climate-tech startup with two revenue streams: Energy Storage as a Service for renewable projects and grid operators, and carbon removal certificates from capturing CO2 in solid state and placing it in the ocean for permanent storage. The team is developing direct air capture combined with long-duration energy storage based on cryogenics and membrane separation, claiming 99% CO2 capture per charge/discharge cycle. Bakz4ever has not raised any funding rounds, and Parth Verma is the sole listed founder.
