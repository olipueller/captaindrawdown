## About

Bakz4ever (OPC) Pvt. Ltd. is a very small, unfunded startup in Noida, India. Parth Verma founded it in 2021. It is developing CarbonBank, a system that combines direct air capture with grid-scale energy storage. It plans to sell carbon removal credits and is also looking at industrial uses for the captured CO2. The company is pre-commercial and has no registry issuances.
