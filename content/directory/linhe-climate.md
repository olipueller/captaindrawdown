## About

Linhe Climate Technology (霖和气候科技, Carbon Loop / co2loop) is a Beijing-based climate tech startup founded in 2021 that develops distributed CCUS solutions, including a humidity-swing DAC system using proprietary MSCCM ion-exchange resin materials and a modular flue-gas capture unit. The company operates what it claims to be Asia's first industrial-scale DAC plant in Xi'an (~2,000 kg CO2/year) and targets downstream CO2 utilization in carbon-rich agriculture, enhanced oil recovery, and mineralization of solid waste/tailings. Backed by CITIC Capital and other Chinese investors through a pre-Series A round.
