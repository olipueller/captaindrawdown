## About

Australian Carbon Vault Pty Ltd is an Adelaide-based company, registered in 2023, that develops permanent onshore geological CO2 storage. It sells storage as a service to both point-source emitters and carbon removal technologies. It received A$10 million in Australian government funding to show integrated direct air capture and storage with Airhive at a multi-user CCS hub.
