## About

Arbor is a US-based (El Segundo, CA) carbon-negative power company founded in 2022 by SpaceX veterans, developing a BECCS system that gasifies waste biomass and uses oxy-combustion with supercritical CO2 turbomachinery to generate clean electricity while capturing >99% of CO2 for permanent geologic storage. Its first commercial facility is planned for Lake Charles, Louisiana with operations expected in 2028, backed by a $41M Frontier offtake for 116,000 tons (2028–2030) and a 25,000-ton Microsoft agreement starting 2027.
