## About

Carbonauten is a German company that develops and produces technical biocarbons, pyrolysis oils, and renewable energy from woody biomass residues through proprietary pyrolysis technology. They use biochar as a CO2 sink, locking it into innovative products like CO2-negative materials for durable seat shells.
