## About

Carbonauten GmbH is a German cleantech company founded in 2017 and headquartered in Giengen an der Brenz, producing biochar and bio-oils via proprietary modular pyrolysis. Its "Minus CO2 Factory 001" in Eberswalde is operational (~3,000 t/yr biochar) and a much larger plant is under construction in Chibi, Hubei Province, China, targeting roughly 30,000 t biochar/yr from waste bamboo. The biochar is embedded into NET Materials® plastic compounds, construction materials, and agricultural substrates, with pilot customers including Deutsche Bahn, VW, Mercedes-Benz, Siemens and Porsche.
