## About

Carbonauten GmbH is a German biochar producer based in Giengen an der Brenz that operates the "Minus CO2 Factory" in Eberswalde (~3,000 tonnes biochar/year) and is building a larger plant in Chibi, China, designed to process 100,000 tonnes of bamboo annually. The company produces technical biocarbons, pyrolysis oils and renewable energy, and locks biochar into CO2-negative plastic granulates used by partners such as Deutsche Bahn and Arburg.
