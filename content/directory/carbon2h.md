## About

Based in Berlin, Germany, Carbon2H specializes in advanced biomass gasification systems, converting biomass into clean syngas using modular, customizable gasifiers housed in shipping containers for easy deployment and scalability. Their mobile biomass gasification systems convert waste into renewable energy and biochar, which enhances soil health and sequesters carbon. They sell equipment rather than directly operating removal facilities, but their technology enables biochar-based CDR.
