## About

Scape Carbon is a Los Angeles startup founded in 2022 developing modular coastal seawater reactors using olivine to sequester industrial CO2 as bicarbonate, with critical-metals recovery as a co-product. The company raised ~$500K pre-seed (most recently Dec 2024) from investors including Wildcard Incubator, Anthropocene Ventures, E8, Five Two Five, and Brinc. The founder/former CEO is now listed as leading a successor company called FlueVault (olivine reactor patent holder), suggesting Scape Carbon's activity has largely migrated to that entity.
