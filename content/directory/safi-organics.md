## About

Safi Organics is a Kenyan MIT-spinout that makes biochar-blended organic fertilizer (Safi Sarvi) from rice husks and other crop residues via pyrolysis. The biochar component sequesters carbon in soils while improving fertility for smallholder farmers. The website is live with active product ordering pages and KEBS-certified products distributed across Kenya.
