## About

North Star Carbon Solutions LP is a joint venture between Svante Technologies (via its Carbon Alpha subsidiary) and the Meadow Lake Tribal Council (MLTC) developing Canada's first majority Indigenous-owned BECCS project in Saskatchewan. The facility will capture biogenic CO2 from the MLTC Bioenergy Centre (fueled by sawmill waste biomass) and permanently store it in a deep saline aquifer, with Phase 1 designed for up to 140,000 tonnes CO2/year. In April 2026 it signed a 626,000-tonne, 15-year offtake with Microsoft; commercial operation is planned for 2029.
