## About

PyroGenesys is a Birmingham, UK pyrolysis technology developer. It builds, owns and operates container-scale waste-to-energy units and licenses its patented Pyrochemy technology at industrial scale, producing renewable power, heat, biofuels and biochar. It signed a biochar CDR prepurchase agreement with Carbonfuture and ran Innovate UK-funded projects in Nigeria.
