## About

PyroGenesys is a UK-based technology developer with a patented "Pyrochemy" pyrolysis system that converts agricultural and biomass waste into renewable heat/electricity, bio-oil, and biochar. The biochar produced is used for carbon capture/credits, soil amendment, and other applications, with project deployments targeted at Sub-Saharan Africa (Nigeria, Ethiopia).
