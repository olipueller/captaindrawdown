## About

HerbaCarbo GbR is a small biochar business in Andernach, Rhineland-Palatinate, Germany, run by Timo Ferdinand and Andreas Scherer. It sells EBC-certified plant biochar and feed biochar under the HerbaCarbo brand, plus starter kits for soil use. It is listed as a member of the German Biochar Association (Fachverband Pflanzenkohle). No carbon-credit issuances or registry listings were found.
