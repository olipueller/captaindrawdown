## About

Carbondrop is a carbon removal startup in Vienna, Austria, founded in 2023, that works on enhanced rock weathering. It makes a basalt-based soil amendment that it pitches as a replacement for agricultural lime, aiming to improve soils and remove CO2 at the same time.
