## About

Ecomerit Technologies is a Santa Barbara, California-based marine renewable energy product design studio founded by Jim and Brent Dehlsen. Its product portfolio spans Aquantis marine current turbines, Anacapa wave energy systems, SeaWell desalination buoys, and the Precipitor ocean alkalinity enhancement/mCDR system, so CDR is one adjacent activity within a broader renewable-energy R&D business.
