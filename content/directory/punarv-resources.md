## About

Punarv Resources Pvt Ltd is an Indian biochar producer based in Raipur, Chhattisgarh, incorporated in November 2019. The company pyrolyzes agricultural residues such as cotton stalk and pigeon pea stalk from Akola district farms to produce biochar for soil application, targeting issuance of CO2 Removal Certificates (CORCs) under the Puro.earth standard. Founder Nitin Agrawal is also engaged in Indian biochar policy advocacy (advising NITI Aayog).
