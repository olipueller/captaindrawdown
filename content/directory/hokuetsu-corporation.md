## About

Hokuetsu Corporation is one of Japan's leading paper manufacturers and is the biogenic emission-source host for a BECCS project at its Niigata Mill, where CO2 from a black-liquor-fired chemical recovery boiler is being captured. It is one of four partners (with JAPEX, Tohoku Electric, and Mitsubishi Gas Chemical) in JOGMEC's Advanced CCS Project in the Higashi-Niigata area, targeting operations by 2030, and launched a CO2 capture demonstration with MHI's CO2MPACT Mobile system in November 2024.
