## About

CarbonBuilt is a Torrance, California company that produces ultra-low-carbon concrete masonry units using its proprietary Reversa® binder, which replaces up to 50% of Portland cement with upcycled industrial byproducts and chemically mineralizes CO2 during the curing process for permanent storage. It won the NRG COSIA Carbon XPRIZE, has a commercial deployment at Blair Block in Alabama using biogenic CO2 from forestry waste, and sold carbon removal credits to Stripe Climate. In 2024 the company announced a scale-up partnership with Meta.
