## About

Ag-e-Gen is an early-stage agtech startup based in Binghamton, New York. It is building a mobile waste-to-biochar system that processes farm residues on site, mainly manure and hemp waste. A subsidiary, The Farmer's Log, also aims to make fertilizer, livestock bedding and potting soil from the same waste.
