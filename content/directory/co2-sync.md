## About

CO2-Sync is a biochar producer based in Utah's Wasatch Back and led by CEO Darrell Huntsman. In April 2026 it merged with Circle Soil, a company that makes biochar-based soil products. In August 2026 it opened a biochar and clean-energy plant in Price, Utah.
