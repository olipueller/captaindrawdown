## About

Biochar Engineering Corporation was a biochar technology company founded in 2009 in Golden, Colorado, by Jim Fournier. It built mobile biochar production equipment and listed biochar in the USDA BioPreferred catalog around 2010, when it was a finalist for the Virgin Earth Challenge (Branson Prize). Its domain is now listed for sale, and the founder has moved on to other ventures.
