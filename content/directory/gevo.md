## About

Gevo (NASDAQ: GEVO) is a US-based diversified bioenergy company that operates an ethanol plant in Richardton, North Dakota with an integrated BECCS system, capturing biogenic CO2 from fermentation and sequestering it in an onsite Class VI well. The site is permitted for 180K tCO2/year with capacity to scale to 1M tCO2/year, producing Puro.earth-certified CORCs. In September 2025 Gevo signed a ~$26M, 5-year CDR offtake deal with Biorecro, and made its first CORC delivery in November 2025.
