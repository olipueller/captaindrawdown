## About

Fruedal Pyrolyse ApS is a Danish company that develops and runs pyrolysis projects. The plants make biochar and supply green heat to district heating. It is owned by two farmers, is linked to the developer Fruedal Energy, and runs one plant near Lem in northwest Jutland, with plans for more. It is also seeking approval for a site in Viborg to store and process wood chips and biochar.
