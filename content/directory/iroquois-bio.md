## About

Iroquois Bio-Energy Company (IBEC) runs a corn ethanol plant in Rensselaer, Indiana, and is owned by Harvestone Low Carbon Partners. Ethanol production is its main business. Harvestone has filed a Class VI permit application for CCS at IBEC. In November 2026, The Andersons agreed to buy Harvestone, including IBEC, for $915M.
