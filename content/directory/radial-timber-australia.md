## About

Radial Timber is a Victorian (Australia) hardwood timber company founded in 2001. It grows, mills (radial sawing at Yarram) and distributes native hardwood cladding, decking and flooring from Dandenong South. Carbon removal is a side activity: plantation sequestration and a Victorian-government-funded biochar pilot using mill residues.
