## About

Valorify is the trading name of Pacific Heat & Power Pty Ltd, a Melbourne-based developer of utility-scale bioenergy projects focused on renewable gas and liquid fuels. Its projects include Riverina Bioenergy, which has an MOU with Jemena for grid biomethane injection, and Ararat Bioenergy. Ararat Stage 1 is listed on Puro.earth as the first BECCS project in the Asia-Pacific region to pass Puro's preliminary assessment.
