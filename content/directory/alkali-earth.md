## About

Alkali Earth was a US-based CDR startup that attempted to commercialize carbon mineralization using steel slag aggregates in gravel roadbeds, with projects including an Ohio motocross raceway and sales to Frontier and Watershed buyers. The company has wound down operations and its co-founder Dan Maxbauer is now publishing learnings openly via a Substack titled "Notes from Alkali Earth."
