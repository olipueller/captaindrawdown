## About

The Big Biochar Company produces biochar for carbon removal using distributed, modified Kon-Tiki kilns. It says it wants to sell methane-free, durable carbon credits at artisanal cost and sets a goal of fifty million (presumably tonnes) a year by 2035. It is not the same company as Nigeria's Biochar Industrial Group (BIG).
