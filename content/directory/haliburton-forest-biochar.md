## About

Haliburton Forest Biochar (HFB) is a subsidiary of Haliburton Forest & Wild Life Reserve Ltd., an Ontario forestry and sawmill company. HFB pyrolyzes hardwood sawmill residues into biochar at its Kennaway Road facility in Haliburton County, supplying engineered biochar for advanced manufacturing (e.g. automotive foam replacement) and soil amendment markets. The subsidiary is expanding with $4.6M in provincial funding announced in 2025.
