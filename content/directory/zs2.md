## About

ZS2 Technologies is a Calgary-based manufacturer of magnesium cement building materials (boards, panels, tiles, sheathing) that mineralize CO2 into the cement matrix, reportedly at ~10% by weight. The company also develops a proprietary direct-air-capture process to source CO2 for sequestration, and was a Carbon Removal XPRIZE Milestone 1 top-60 company. In May 2025 it launched its commercially-ready Gen 2 product platform with $9.9M+ in grant backing.
