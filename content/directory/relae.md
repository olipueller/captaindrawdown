## About

Relae is the new name of Carbon Direct, which rebranded in September 2026. It is a New York-based energy and climate advisory company covering carbon removal, power and energy, environmental markets, natural capital and climate strategy. Its CDR work includes vetting projects and advising buyers, and it does not generate removals itself.
