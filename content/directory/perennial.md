## About

Perennial (formerly Cloud Agronomics) is a Boulder, CO-based MRV platform for soil-based carbon removal, using AI, remote sensing, and digital soil mapping to quantify soil organic carbon at scale. They led the development of Verra's VT0014 tool and serve project developers, food/ag companies, and sustainability teams — they are part of the CDR ecosystem as a verifier/MRV provider, not a CO2 remover themselves.
