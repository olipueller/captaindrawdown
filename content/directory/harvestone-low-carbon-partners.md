## About

Harvestone Low Carbon Partners was formed in 2022 by Harvestone Group and Energy Capital Partners. It owns and runs the Blue Flint (Underwood, ND), Dakota Spirit (Jamestown, ND) and Iroquois Bioenergy (Indiana) ethanol plants. Since 2023 it has captured and stored nearly all of Blue Flint's CO2 underground, and it is looking at trucking about 230,000 t/yr of CO2 from Dakota Spirit to storage.
