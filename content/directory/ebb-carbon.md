## About

Ebb Carbon is a South San Francisco-based marine CDR pure-play that integrates bipolar membrane electrodialysis with desalination plants to convert brine into alkaline seawater that permanently stores atmospheric CO2 as bicarbonate. The company runs Project Macoma in Port Angeles, WA and a demo at PNNL-Sequim, and holds offtake agreements with Microsoft (up to 350,000 tonnes) and Google (3,500 tonnes), plus a partnership with the Saudi Water Authority.
