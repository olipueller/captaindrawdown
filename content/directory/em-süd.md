## About

EM-Technologie Zentrum Süd GmbH (EM-Süd) is a German family business based in Haldenwang, Allgäu, that produces biochar under the "Pflanzenkohle Allgäu" brand alongside Effective Microorganisms (EM) products for agriculture, animal husbandry, and soil improvement. Their biochar is EBC-certified and FiBL-listed, made from hardwood (beech), and marketed for uses including soil improvement, biogas fermenters, livestock bedding/manure, and concrete additives. They are members of German Biochar e.V. and operate an active online shop and farm shop.
