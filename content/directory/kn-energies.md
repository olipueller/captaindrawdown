## About

KN Energies (formerly Klaipėdos Nafta) is a state-controlled Lithuanian company that runs the Klaipėda LNG and oil terminals. It is developing a CO2 transshipment terminal of about 2.8 Mt/yr with Akmenės cementas, Schwenk Latvija, Larvik Shipping and Mitsui O.S.K. Lines. The terminal would serve a CCS chain, but its main business is still fossil-fuel terminals.
