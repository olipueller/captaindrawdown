## About

<cite index="11-2,19-15,19-16,19-17">CO2Rail Company is a Houston, Texas-based climate tech company whose main service is a proprietary rail-based system that captures carbon dioxide from ambient air and diesel locomotive emissions using energy generated by train regenerative braking.</cite> <cite index="2-11,2-12">Its purpose-built rail cars capture energy from regenerative braking to charge onboard batteries while leveraging the slipstream of the moving train in a ramjet-type process to avoid the need for fans, directing air into a cylindrical CO2 collection chamber.</cite> The company remains pre-commercial, with no deployed railcars or registry-issued credits; it is still fundraising from venture capital as of recent LinkedIn activity.
