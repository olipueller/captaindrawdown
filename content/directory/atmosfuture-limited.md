## About

Atmosfuture Limited is a UK-based early-stage startup developing REVFRACC, a patented cryogenic DAC technology that liquefies air to capture CO2 and generate zero-carbon power for data centres. The company was selected for Remove's Europe CDR accelerator in early 2025 and has raised approximately $1.93M. It pitches containerised modular units producing up to 30 MW, targeting pilot and demonstration projects.
