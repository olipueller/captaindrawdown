## About

CharLine GmbH is an Austrian biochar-products subsidiary of composting company Sonnenerde GmbH, founded in January 2016 by biochar pioneer Gerald Dunst and based in Riedlingsdorf, Burgenland. The company develops feed charcoal, manure charcoal and plant biochar certified to the European Biochar Certificate (EBC) standard, and participates in the EU-funded BIOCHAR-LIFE-EU project with partners NGE and CarStorCon to develop biochar-based climate-positive building material additives.
