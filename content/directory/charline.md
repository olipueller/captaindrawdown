## About

CharLine GmbH is an Austria-based (Riedlingsdorf, Burgenland) producer of biochar-based products, particularly feed charcoal, manure charcoal, and plant biochar for the livestock and agriculture sectors. The company works in cooperation with Sonnenerde GmbH and certifies its biochar to the European Biochar Certificate (EBC) standard.
