## About

INPEX is Japan's largest oil and gas exploration and production company and operates the Ichthys LNG project in Australia. As part of its INPEX Vision 2035 decarbonisation strategy it is building carbon capture and storage projects, led by the Bonaparte CCS project in Australia, and it also runs smaller DAC research. Carbon management is an add-on to its core hydrocarbon business, and most of the captured CO2 is fossil, so it reduces emissions rather than removing CO2 from the air.
