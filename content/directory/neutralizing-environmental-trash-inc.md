## About

Neutralizing Environmental Trash Inc. (N.E.T. Inc), headquartered in Medford, Oregon, converts waste biomass into biochar for carbon removal and soil amendment. In November 2024 the company entered a 50/50 joint venture ("NET of the Villages LLC") with BluMountain Carbon (a BluSky Carbon joint venture) to build a biochar production facility in the Orlando area using local wood waste, targeting ~50,000 tonnes of annual carbon removal.
