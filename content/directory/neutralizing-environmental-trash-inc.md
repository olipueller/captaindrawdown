## About

Neutralizing Environmental Trash Inc. (N.E.T.) is a very small biochar developer based in Medford, Oregon, led by President Nicolaas Vanderwey. In November 2024 it formed a 50/50 joint venture with BluMountain Carbon (BluSky Carbon / Red Mountain Biochar) to deploy Vulcan pyrolysis units, beginning with a planned project near Orlando, Florida. It has a supplier profile on CDR.fyi but no registry issuances that we could find.
