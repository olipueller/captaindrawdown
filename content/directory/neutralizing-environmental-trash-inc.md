## About

Neutralizing Environmental Trash Inc. (N.E.T. Inc.) is a Medford, Oregon-based biochar producer led by President Nicolaas Vanderwey that converts waste biomass into biochar for use as a carbon-negative soil amendment. In November 2024 it entered a joint venture with BluSky Carbon's BluMountain Carbon vehicle to develop biochar production facilities, with initial project LOIs targeting Orlando, Florida and Tanzania.
