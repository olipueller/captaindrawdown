## About

Net-Zero Richardton LLC is the Gevo, Inc. subsidiary that owns the former Red Trail Energy ethanol plant and its carbon capture and storage assets in Richardton, North Dakota. Gevo bought them in early 2025 for about $210M. The site stores up to about 165,000 t/yr of biogenic CO2 and sells removal credits, including through a September 2025 offtake deal with Biorecro. Ethanol (and planned SAF) production is still the plant's core business.
