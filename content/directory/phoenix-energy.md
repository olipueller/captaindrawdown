## About

Phoenix Energy is a California-based developer and operator of community-scale BECCS plants that convert forestry and agricultural waste into renewable energy and biochar (sold under the Earthworks Biochar brand). Its North Fork Community Power plant is operational and it is constructing the 3 MW Blue Mountain Electric Company (BMEC) plant, with a third project planned at Napa Recycling & Waste Services. The company sells carbon removal credits alongside wildfire-mitigation services to agencies like Cal Fire and the US Forest Service.
