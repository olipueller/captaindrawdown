## About

BIOCHAR GmbH & Co. KG is a German biochar producer part of the CONVORIS Unternehmensgruppe, operating a pyrolysis facility at the Reuden/Draschwitz industrial park near Leipzig. They produce biochar from natural wood feedstocks (softwood chips from German forestry) using BEHORE Technology pyrolysis plants, with syngas recovered as process heat and power. Primary market is agriculture (feed additive, soil amendment) and they are listed as a Puro.earth registered producer.
