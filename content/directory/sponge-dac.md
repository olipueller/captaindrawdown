## About

Sponge DAC, Inc. is an early-stage Direct Air Capture startup based in Cambridge, Massachusetts, led by CEO Marek Herrmann-Nowosielski. The company is developing a moving sorbent-web DAC apparatus that shuttles between cool absorption and hot desorption zones, with multiple US patent applications and a "SPONGE" trademark filing.
