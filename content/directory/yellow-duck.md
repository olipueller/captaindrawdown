## About

Yellow Duck Inc. is a Kobe, Japan startup led by CEO Shigeo Nakayama developing the Wave-DAC system, an egg-shaped floating device that uses wave motion to generate electricity, capture CO2 from the atmosphere, and produce hydrogen via seawater electrolysis. The company is at prototype/basic-research stage, having won the NEDO Prize and Marinetech Grand Prix 2023, and exhibited at Expo 2025 Osaka.
