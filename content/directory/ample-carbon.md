## About

Ample Carbon is a California-based startup founded in 2023 to turn retiring U.S. coal plants into BECCS facilities that produce durable carbon removal, while building local biomass supply chains. It raised seed funding from Prime Coalition's Trellis Climate in 2024. In summer 2026 it published a progress update titled "Lessons Learned From the Shifting U.S. BECCS Landscape (2023–2026)".
