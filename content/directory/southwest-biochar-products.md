## About

Southwest Biochar Products (SOWBiochar) is an Arizona-based biochar producer in Eagar, AZ, operating since 2022 and founded by Tyson Nicoll. It uses slow pyrolysis (on ARTi-supplied reactors) to convert wood chips and forest-thinning/sawmill residues into biochar sold as a soil amendment, and sells carbon credits through 3Degrees. In fall 2024 the company scaled from a single reactor to a 5-reactor system with capacity of over 12,000 yards per year.
