## About

Ocean-based Climate Solutions, Inc. (now operating as The Sea Upwelling Company Inc dba Sea-Up) is a Santa Fe, New Mexico-based marine CDR developer founded as a spin-off of Atmocean. It builds wave-powered artificial upwelling pumps ("Oxygenators") that bring nutrient-rich deep water to the surface to stimulate phytoplankton growth and enhance biological ocean carbon sequestration. Led by managing director Philip Kithil, the company has pursued field deployments including a planned project near Bermuda with BIOS.
