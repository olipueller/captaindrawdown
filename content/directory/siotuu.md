## About

SIOTUU GmbH is an Austrian biochar producer based in Fließ/Landeck, Tyrol, that uses Syncraft pyrolysis technology to co-generate electricity, heat and biochar from residual Alpine wood. Biochar is marketed under the Alpenkohle brand for barbecue, agriculture, construction and metallurgy, and carbon removal credits are distributed via partners including Klimate.
