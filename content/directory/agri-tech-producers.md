## About

Agri-Tech Producers (ATP) is a provider of torrefaction technology whose process converts cellulosic biomass such as wood chips and bio-crops into a renewable coal substitute, energy pellets feedstock, and gasification feedstock. The company also markets a patented CRBBP Process that plants fast-growing bio-crops to capture CO2 and convert the resulting biomass into biochar to sequester captured carbon, or into a bio-coal co-fire fuel. Agri-Tech Producers was founded in 2005 and is headquartered in Columbia, SC.
