## About

Green Sequest is a company that provides environmental services, specifically focusing on carbon dioxide removal from the atmosphere using Enhanced Rock Weathering (ERW). They aim to accelerate natural rock mineralization, utilizing serpentinite to capture and permanently store CO2.
