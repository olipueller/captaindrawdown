## About

Carbon Char Store (CCS) is the UK-based carbon-removals sales and marketing arm of Standard Gas Technologies, a waste-to-energy company whose SG100 Advanced Thermal Cracking technology pyrolyzes non-recyclable waste into syngas and a high-carbon (~95%) biochar. CCS markets the resulting biochar-based carbon removals (bound into concrete/asphalt) and is developing the SYLVA platform to issue digital removal certificates (SYLVA CRTs). Standard Gas has worked with Carbon Standards International and the Ithaka Institut on a methodology to certify removals from the biogenic fraction of mixed waste.
