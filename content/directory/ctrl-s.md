## About

CTRL-S (Climate Tech Rescue, License, & Scale) is an early-stage US platform founded by Jason Hochman, co-founder and former executive director of the Direct Air Capture Coalition. It aims to keep knowledge from failed direct air capture companies from being lost by archiving it and licensing it to other developers. It started with DAC and plans to cover other climate technologies later.
