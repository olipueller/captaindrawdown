Ctrl-S is a startup focused on salvaging and preserving valuable IP, experimental data, and 'negative learnings' from direct air capture companies that fail or pivot. By acquiring this IP and making it accessible to materials-discovery AI models and new entrants, Ctrl-S aims to accelerate DAC development and prevent institutional knowledge loss.

**Website:** [https://heatmap.news/carbon-removal/ctrl-s-direct-air-capture](https://heatmap.news/carbon-removal/ctrl-s-direct-air-capture)

**Location:** United States
**Category:** Direct Air Capture
**Method:** DAC IP Preservation
**Tech:** Acquires and preserves IP from failed or pivoting DAC startups; conducts techno-economic diligence and lifecycle analysis; provides negative-learnings data to materials-discovery AI models
