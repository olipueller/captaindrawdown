## About

Parallel South, formerly Deep Carbon Africa Pvt Ltd, is an early-stage carbon removal project developer with entities in Mauritius and South Africa. It works with farmers, communities and African governments to develop biochar projects from agricultural and forestry residues, and some biogas projects. It focuses in particular on vulnerable island nations.
