## About

EcoFix Kenya runs a biochar facility in Nanyuki, Kenya, that turns croton nut husks from its agricultural operations into biochar. Terraton, a carbon project developer, made its final investment decision on the facility in August 2025. Biochar production began in May 2026, and Isometric issued the first credits in October 2026. The facility is also listed on Puro.earth as preliminarily assessed.
