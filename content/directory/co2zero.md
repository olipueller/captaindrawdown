## About

CO2Zero is a Rotterdam, Netherlands-based Direct Air Capture startup that was incubated through the YES!Delft accelerator program. The company describes its approach as a tech-agnostic modular integrator that combines multiple DAC technologies with power-to-heat and water co-production, targeting capture costs below €150/ton. They are an independent startup (not a division of a parent company — YES!Delft is an incubator, not a parent).
