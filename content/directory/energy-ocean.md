## About

eoc energy ocean GmbH produces EBC-certified biochar in Luthern, canton Lucerne, Switzerland, binding CO2 while delivering heat to the village and promoting the circular economy. Its first pyrolysis plant went into operation in Luthern in September 2023. Products are marketed under the Swiss Biochar brand.
