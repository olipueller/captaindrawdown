## About

Stiesdal SkyClean A/S is the biochar/pyrolysis subsidiary of Danish climate-tech group Stiesdal A/S, founded by wind-power pioneer Henrik Stiesdal. The parent group operates multiple non-CDR subsidiaries in floating offshore wind, green hydrogen electrolyzers and thermal energy storage, with SkyClean as its dedicated carbon removal arm. Its first commercial-scale 20 MW plant in Vrå, North Jutland was inaugurated in October 2024.
