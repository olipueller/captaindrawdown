## About

Aeon Blue is an early-stage startup in Sydney, Nova Scotia, founded in 2018 by siblings Lark Meadows and Deóis Ua Cearnaigh. It combines wind-powered seawater electrolysis with direct air capture to produce drop-in e-fuels. Its Cape Breton project, listed on Puro.earth, plans geologic storage of about 6,800 tCO2 per year, with the first facility targeted for 2027.
