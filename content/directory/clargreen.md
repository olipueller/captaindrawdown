## About

Clargreen (also known as Clargreen Technologies) converts agricultural waste, specifically palm kernel shells, into high-carbon biochar. This biochar is used in a soil amendment product (CO₂ Savior™) to capture CO₂ from the atmosphere, regenerate soil, and increase crop yields for farmers in Nigeria.
