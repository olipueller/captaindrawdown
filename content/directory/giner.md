## About

Giner, Inc. is a Newton, Massachusetts-based electrochemical R&D firm founded in 1973 with a broad portfolio spanning fuel cells, electrolyzers, batteries, biomedical devices, and aerospace technologies. Within that portfolio they are developing a lab-scale Direct Air Capture system that uses a concentrated KOH air contactor paired with a hydrogen-assisted carbonate electrolyzer to capture and regenerate purified CO2, a project funded through DOE's DAC Pre-Commercial Technology Prize. DAC is one of several research lines rather than the company's principal business, but Giner is building the removal technology itself rather than buying credits.
