## About

Greenerth ClimaTech and Impact Solutions Pvt. Ltd. (Greenerth Solutions) is a climate tech startup in Pune, India, founded in January 2025. It runs nature-based biochar carbon removal projects with farming communities. It reports more than 400 tCO2e removed and 50+ farmers onboarded, and is acting as C-Sink Manager for a Global Artisan C-Sink project.
