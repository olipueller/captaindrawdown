## About

Ocean Carbon Removal Watch is an investigative journalism platform supported by the Pulitzer Center. Journalists Alexandra Talty and Allison Gacad (Carbon Pulse) created it to track the finance, science and governance of marine carbon dioxide removal (mCDR). It reports on the mCDR sector but does not supply removals.
