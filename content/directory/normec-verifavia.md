## About

Normec Verifavia is an accredited verification body, part of the Dutch testing and inspection group Normec, that grew out of the aviation emissions verifier Verifavia. It verifies emissions for EU ETS, CORSIA and Airport Carbon Accreditation, and also does third-party validation and verification of carbon removal, reduction and avoidance projects. It does not remove carbon itself, and CDR verification is only one part of a broader GHG verification business.
