## About

LapWall Oyj is a Finnish maker of prefabricated wood building elements, founded in 2011, based in Pyhäntä and listed in Helsinki. Puro.earth accepted it as a supplier of carbon removal certificates in 2021, based on the carbon stored in its wood elements. Construction elements are its main business, and carbon removal is a side activity.
