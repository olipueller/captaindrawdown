## About

Earthly Biochar is a small UK-based biochar producer (incorporated 2018, Salisbury) that makes organic biochar from waste wood and sells it, along with purpose-built smokeless biochar kilns, to gardeners, horticulturists, and land managers. A related entity, Earthly Biochar Industries Ltd (2023), appears aimed at scaling production. The company positions biochar as a negative-emission technology and soil amendment.
