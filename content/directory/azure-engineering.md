## About

Azure Engineering is a US power-generation engineering consultancy founded by Kevin Tangen. It handles biomass energy system design, balance-of-plant engineering, electrical and SCADA work for projects such as Project Azure in Costa Rica and a pyrolysis facility at a Wyoming sawmill. Its main business is engineering services for biomass and renewable natural gas power. Biochar and carbon sequestration are add-ons, not the core business.
