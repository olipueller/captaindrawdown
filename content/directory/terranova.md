## About

Terranova is a San Rafael/Berkeley, California startup founded in 2021 that uses AI-guided terraforming robots to inject waste-wood slurry deep underground, simultaneously lifting flood-prone terrain and permanently burying biomass for carbon removal credits. The company emerged from stealth in November 2025 with a $7M seed round led by Outlander and Congruent Ventures (valuation $25.1M), with plans to deploy its first projects in San Rafael. Its primary commercial pitch is climate adaptation (flood defense), with CDR credits as a revenue co-product.
