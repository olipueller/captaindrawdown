## About

CarbonEnergy Inc. is a Daegu-based climate-tech startup founded in March 2022. It develops an electrochemical direct air capture process that captures CO2 as a carbonate and generates electricity at the same time. A US DOE report listed it as a "direct storage DAC" company. It raised a Series A of about KRW 3.7 billion and is now working on getting cement materials made from the captured carbonate to market.
