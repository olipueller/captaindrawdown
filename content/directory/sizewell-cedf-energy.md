## About

Sizewell C, developed by EDF, is a 3.2 GW nuclear power station project in Suffolk, UK that leads a government-funded consortium (with the University of Birmingham, University of Nottingham, AtkinsRealis, Altrad Babcock and Helical Energy) developing a heat-driven Direct Air Capture demonstrator at the Port of Lowestoft. The 100-tonne/year demonstrator was funded by a £3m Phase 2 Greenhouse Gas Removals competition award in 2023, with the longer-term vision of a 1.5 Mt/yr DAC facility powered by low-carbon nuclear heat from Sizewell C. The CDR activity is an R&D arm of a nuclear utility project, not its core business.
