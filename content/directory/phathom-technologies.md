## About

pHathom Technologies is a Halifax, Nova Scotia cleantech startup (founded 2023/24) developing a hybrid BECCS + ocean alkalinity enhancement pathway it calls Bioenergy Carbon Capture and Ocean Storage (BECCOS). It bolts onto coastal biomass power/heat plants, dissolves captured CO2 in seawater with limestone to form bicarbonate, and releases it for durable marine storage. The company closed a $4M seed round led by Propeller Ventures in Feb 2026 (>$12M total committed capital), was selected for Frontier's advance purchase program, and is running a completed pilot at NSCC Lunenburg with a Huntsman Marine Science Centre trial planned for summer 2026.
