## About

Energy Farmers Australia is a Western Australia-based biochar and bioenergy project developer founded in 2010 that builds pyrolysis systems, produces biochar from organic waste streams, and sells biochar to agriculture, horticulture, livestock and industrial customers under the FarmChar brand. The company also develops its own pyrolysis kilns (including mobile units) and runs field trials with farmer groups.
