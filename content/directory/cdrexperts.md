## About

CDRexperts is a Bremen-based carbon dioxide removal consultancy led by geochemist Dr. Elisabete Trindade Pedrosa. A small team provides R&D, project management and carbon-credit certification services, mainly for enhanced rock weathering and biochar projects. It also offers an enhanced-weathering modelling tool.
