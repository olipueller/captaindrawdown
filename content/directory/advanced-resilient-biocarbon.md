## About

Advanced Resilient Biocarbon (ARB) is a small company in Cold Spring, New York. It is the US affiliate of Advanced Resilient Technology Ltd, an international technology developer, and was registered as a Wyoming LLC in 2019. ARB turns organic waste into char and biochar for carbon sequestration and environmental uses, and also produces clean energy.
