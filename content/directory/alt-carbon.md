## About

Alt Carbon is an India-based CDR supplier deploying Enhanced Rock Weathering by spreading basalt rock dust from the Rajmahal Traps on tea estates and farm fields in Darjeeling/eastern India. The company raised a $12M seed round in May 2025 (India's largest climate-tech seed), holds a Frontier $500K prepurchase, a NextGen offtake, and a 10,000-ton MOL Group agreement, and is delivering its first credits via Isometric.
