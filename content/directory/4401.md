## About

44.01 is an Omani climate-tech supplier that accelerates natural peridotite mineralization to permanently sequester CO2 captured from DAC or industrial point sources. The company won the XPRIZE Carbon Removal with Project Hajar and operates pilots in Oman and the UAE with partners including ADNOC, Masdar, Aircapture, Mission Zero, and Holcim.
