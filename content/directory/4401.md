## About

44.01 is an Omani carbon mineralization company that permanently sequesters CO2 by dissolving it in water and injecting it into peridotite rock formations, where it turns into stable carbonate minerals within months. Founded in 2020, the company operates pilots in Oman and the UAE with partners including Aircapture, ADNOC, Holcim, and Climeworks, and won both the Earthshot Prize (2022) and XPRIZE Carbon Removal.
