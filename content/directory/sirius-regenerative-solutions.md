## About

Sirius Regenerative is a Colombian biochar producer based in Meta that operates a pyrolysis "BioFábrica" converting biomass into biochar, wood vinegar, and syngas. They sell biochar-based bioinputs (biochar blended with compost and beneficial microorganisms like Trichoderma) to farmers for soil regeneration and long-term carbon sequestration. The company sponsored the ECI Biochar IV Conference in Santa Marta in 2025.
