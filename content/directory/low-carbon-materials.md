## About

Low Carbon Materials (LCM) is a Durham University spin-out based in Seaham, UK, that designs and manufactures carbon-negative aggregates — OSTO® for concrete and ACLA® for asphalt — which sequester biogenic carbon (with some additional carbonation uptake) permanently within construction materials. The company closed a £3M growth round led by Ada Ventures, won the BE100 SCALE competition at Blue Earth Summit 2025, and has deployed ACLA in trials on the M11 and numerous council road projects with National Highways, Skanska, and Tarmac. An EPD (EPD-IES-0016646) reports an A1-A3 GWP of –798 kg CO2e/t for ACLA.
