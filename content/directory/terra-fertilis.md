## About

Terra Fertilis is a French biochar producer based in Argentan (Normandy), operating a pyrolysis plant that converts sustainably-sourced French forest biomass into biochar soil amendments for professional agriculture and consumer gardening. It was the first biochar to receive ANSES market authorization in France and is EBC-certified. In 2024 its activities were consolidated under SYLVA FERTILIS INTERNATIONAL within Groupe SLB.
