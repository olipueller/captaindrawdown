## About

LUCRAT GmbH is a small biochar company in Steinfurt, North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, founded in 2018. It makes EBC-certified biochar products, mainly for improving biogas plant processes and for the construction industry. It has also run R&D projects, including "Carboferro" with the German Biomass Research Centre (DBFZ), which combines biochar and iron for biogas production.
