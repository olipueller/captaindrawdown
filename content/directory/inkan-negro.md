## About

Inkan Negro is a Peruvian biochar company, founded 2018 and linked to Dr. Brenton Ladd's Peruvian Biochar Research Centre, that manufactures biochar soil amendments from agricultural and urban green waste. They have participated in Puro.earth pre-CORC biochar project development and received Peruvian government innovation funding (ProInnóvate) for low-emission agriculture applications.
