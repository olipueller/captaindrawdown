## About

Marnova is an early-stage climate-tech startup that wants to manage Sargassum seaweed offshore and use it for marine biomass-sinking carbon removal. Its website describes a scalable floating system called FloCS. It also plans to sell ocean monitoring and forecasting, act as a private fish-aggregating device service, and issue carbon and biodiversity credits within the next few years.
