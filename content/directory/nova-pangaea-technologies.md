## About

Nova Pangaea Technologies is a UK cleantech company headquartered at Wilton International, Redcar, that has commissioned an end-to-end demonstration plant for its patented REFNOVA® process, converting woody and agricultural residues into 2G ethanol (for SAF) and biochar. While SAF is its primary commercial focus (through Project Speedbird with British Airways and LanzaJet), the biochar co-product delivers net-negative CO₂ emissions, placing NPT within the biochar-supplier segment of the CDR market.
