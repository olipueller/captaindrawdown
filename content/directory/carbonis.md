## About

Carbonis GmbH & Co. KG is a German biochar producer based in Garrel, Lower Saxony, founded in 2016 as part of the Voßmann agricultural group. The company produces GMP+ certified biochar via dry pyrolysis and sells it across agricultural, livestock, biogas, and industrial segments, with the agricultural application delivering durable carbon storage.
