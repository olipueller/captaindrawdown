## About

Metalplant is a US-headquartered CDR supplier operating field sites in Albania on serpentine-rich soils of the Mirdita Ophiolite. Its "Hyperweathering" approach couples enhanced rock weathering of olivine with nickel phytomining to co-produce carbon removal credits and critical minerals for the EV/green-steel supply chain. The company is working toward Puro.earth ERW methodology compliance and announced a commercial trial for 2025.
