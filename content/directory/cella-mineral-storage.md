## About

Cella Mineral Storage is a New York-based startup operating carbon mineralization storage in Kenya's Rift Valley, injecting captured CO2 into basaltic volcanic rocks for permanent sequestration. They partner with multiple DAC companies (Octavia, Sirona, RepAir, Greenlyte, Carbon Atlantis) to provide storage services and have sold credits to Frontier (Stripe/Shopify).
