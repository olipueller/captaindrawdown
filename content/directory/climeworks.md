## About

Climeworks is a Swiss-based CDR company that primarily uses solid sorbent-based Direct Air Capture (DAC) technology to remove CO2 from the air. They also offer tailored carbon removal portfolios that combine DAC with other solutions like biochar, BECCS, and enhanced rock weathering.
