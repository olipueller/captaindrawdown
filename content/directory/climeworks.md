## About

Climeworks is a Swiss-based pioneer in Direct Air Capture, operating the Orca (4,000 tCO2/yr) and Mammoth (36,000 tCO2/yr) DAC plants in Iceland with solid sorbent technology. The company also offers blended carbon removal portfolios combining DAC with nature-based and other engineered solutions for corporate buyers.
