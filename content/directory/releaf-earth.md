## About

Releaf Earth is a Nigerian climate-agritech company that processes palm nuts from smallholder farmers using its proprietary Kraken de-sheller, producing palm oil products while converting the resulting palm kernel shell waste into biochar via pyrolysis. In May 2025 it launched Nigeria's first industrial-scale biochar carbon removal facility in Iwuru, Cross River State, targeting 40 ktCO2e removal by 2030, with credits verified through the Riverse registry. The company is backed by Y Combinator, Breakthrough Energy Ventures, and AirMiners.
