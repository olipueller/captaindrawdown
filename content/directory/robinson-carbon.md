## About

Robinson Carbon is an industrial biochar maker based in New Orleans. It is the carbon products arm of Robinson Lumber Company, a family-owned wood products business founded in 1893. It announced a $16.3M biochar manufacturing facility in Rapides Parish, Louisiana, which is expected to create 42 jobs.
