## About

Recycle Roots Ltd is an early-stage UK cleantech startup based in Crewkerne, Somerset. It turns problem biomass waste streams into biochar using its own patented reactor installed at the waste source. It markets the biochar as an agricultural and industrial input and as permanent carbon storage.
