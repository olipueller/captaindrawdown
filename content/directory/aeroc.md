## About

AEROC (Aeroc GmbH) is a German startup based in Cologne that develops and scales Enhanced Rock Weathering (ERW) projects by spreading basalt rock flour on agricultural fields to permanently remove CO2 while improving soil quality and crop yields. The company handles procurement, logistics, application, and MRV of the ERW process and works with farmers via a participation model.
