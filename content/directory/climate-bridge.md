## About

Climate Bridge is a carbon finance company founded in 2006 and based in China, focused on Asia. It develops carbon projects, trades carbon credits and renewable energy certificates, and advises companies on decarbonisation. Most of its portfolio is avoidance credits, alongside some nature-based removal projects such as China's first blue carbon project (Zhanjiang mangroves). It does not run engineered removal itself.
