## About

Prometheus Materials is a Longmont, Colorado-based startup producing ProZERO™, a carbon-negative supplemental cement blend made by combining microalgae with natural binders, mimicking how corals and seashells form. The company sells ready-mix, precast and block products and licenses its formulation to concrete manufacturers, and was awarded a role in a 2024 $10M DOE grant on CDR measurement in cement and concrete.
