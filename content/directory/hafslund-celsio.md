## About

Hafslund Celsio is Norway's largest district heating supplier and operator of the country's largest waste-to-energy plant at Klemetsrud, Oslo. It is a partly-owned subsidiary within the Hafslund/Infranode/HitecVision consortium developing a flagship BECCS project under Norway's Longship program, with FID reached in January 2025 and operations targeted for Q3 2029. The project has secured major CDR offtakes including a 10-year, 1.1 Mt deal with Microsoft and a Frontier prepay.
