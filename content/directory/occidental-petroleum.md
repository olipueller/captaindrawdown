## About

Occidental Petroleum (NYSE: OXY) is a major U.S. oil and gas company that operates a significant Direct Air Capture business through its wholly-owned subsidiaries 1PointFive and Carbon Engineering (acquired 2023). Its flagship STRATOS facility in Ector County, Texas — designed to capture up to 500,000 tonnes of CO2 per year — is in final startup, with Phase 1 expected online in Q2 2026 and CDR credit offtakes signed with Microsoft, Amazon, BlackRock, JPMorgan, Palo Alto Networks, and Bain & Company.
