## About

Avnos is a US-based (LA/New Jersey) startup developing Hybrid Direct Air Capture (HDAC) technology that captures CO2 while producing clean water as a byproduct, using a moisture-swing sorbent that eliminates the need for external heat. The company has secured over $100M in funding from Shell, Mitsubishi, NextEra, ConocoPhillips, JetBlue, DOE and the US Navy, and in November 2025 announced Project Cedar, its first commercial-scale facility (3,000 tCO2/yr) coming online in late 2026.
