## About

Ephemeral Carbon Inc. is an early-stage marine CDR startup in Santa Clara, California. Josh Perfetto, who also co-founded OpenPCR and Chai, started it in 2023. It works on oceanic enhanced weathering with silicate minerals, has raised no outside funding and has no registry issuances.
