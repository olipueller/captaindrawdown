## About

The Carbon Removal Standards Initiative (CRSI) is an independent US nonprofit. Anu Khan, formerly of Carbon180, founded it in early 2024. It helps policymakers and regulators set science-based standards for counting carbon removal, and has been a fiscally sponsored project of Multiplier since April 2025.
