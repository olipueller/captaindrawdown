## About

Parallel Carbon is a UK-based (Manchester) direct air capture startup founded in 2021 that combines mineral-sorbent DAC with water electrolysis to co-produce CDR credits and green hydrogen. In February 2026 it signed a pre-purchase deal with Zurich Insurance Group for 1,200 tonnes of CDR, and it is joining the 2025 Shell StartUp Engine UK cohort while preparing a commercial pilot (100 t H2 and 1,000–2,000 t CO2/yr).
