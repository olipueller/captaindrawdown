## About

Packaging Corporation of America is a large NYSE-listed U.S. maker of containerboard and corrugated packaging, headquartered in Lake Forest, Illinois. Its 2024 sustainability report describes a successful carbon capture pilot at a southern U.S. mill and a follow-on CCS study. It targets capturing and permanently storing 1.75 Mt of biogenic CO2 per year by 2040, but packaging remains its core business.
