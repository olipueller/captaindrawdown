## About

Hydrogen Naturally (H2N) is a Canadian startup founded by Brian Fehr and Ian MacGregor that plans to build BECCS hubs gasifying forestry residuals into carbon-negative "Bright Green" hydrogen while permanently sequestering the biogenic CO2 in saline formations. The company is backed by Peak Renewables and North West Capital Partners, with a first hub planned for Alberta's Industrial Heartland, and received $3M from Emissions Reduction Alberta in April 2025 for a FEED study.
