## About

The Negative Emissions Platform (NEP) is a Brussels-based membership association representing CDR technology developers, researchers and sponsors before EU institutions. It does not remove CO2 itself; instead it lobbies for the Carbon Removal Certification Framework, responds to EU consultations, and convenes the CDR community (e.g. hosting the CDR30 Pavilion at COP30). It is a core ecosystem/advocacy body dedicated solely to the CDR industry.
