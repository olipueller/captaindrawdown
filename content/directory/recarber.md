## About

ReCarber is a Stockholm-based startup founded in 2024 that operates a digital platform to commercialize and finance BECCS projects across the Nordics, partnering with biomass-fired heat and power operators such as Skellefteå Kraft. It raised SEK 60M (~$5.3M) seed in February 2025 from Luminar Ventures, Spintop Ventures, and Course Corrected, and acts as a matchmaker/commercial developer between CDR buyers and biogenic CO2 emitters rather than owning capture infrastructure.
