## About

WeAct Pty Ltd is an Australian carbon project developer and credit trader. It is the proponent of plantation-forestry ACCU projects in Victoria and Tasmania, a Verra-registered South Africa grassland project and a Madagascar clean-cookstove project, and it sells and auctions the credits through platforms like Xpansiv. Its portfolio mixes avoidance and removal credits, and trading is a core activity, so it is not a pure CDR supplier.
