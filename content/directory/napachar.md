## About

Napachar is a small biochar-as-a-service company in Napa, California, founded by Eric Mayer, who holds a Stanford PhD in environmental engineering. It turns woody waste from vineyards and forest fuel-reduction work in Napa and Sonoma counties into biochar on site, replacing open burn piles. The company has not listed on any carbon-credit registry.
