## About

Puro.earth is a leading carbon removal standard and registry that also acts as a marketplace, connecting buyers with suppliers of engineered carbon removal methods like biochar, enhanced rock weathering, and direct air capture (DAC). They issue CO2 Removal Certificates (CORCs) and provide a platform for tracking and managing CDR efforts.
