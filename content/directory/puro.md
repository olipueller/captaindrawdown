## About

Puro.earth is a Finland-based carbon removal standard and registry (majority-owned by Nasdaq since 2021) that certifies engineered CDR projects such as biochar, BECCS, DAC, and enhanced weathering. They issue and track CO2 Removal Certificates (CORCs) representing verified, durable removals of 100+ years, and operate a supplier marketplace connecting buyers with certified CDR project developers. They do not physically remove CO2 themselves — they are the leading CDR-focused registry and standards body.
