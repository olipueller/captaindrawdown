## About

Airhive, founded in 2022, is a UK-based DAC company developing a low-cost, energy-efficient and rapidly scalable technology to capture carbon from the atmosphere. The company is now capturing CO2 from the atmosphere with its system in Alberta, Canada, using a novel DAC method based on fluidised beds combined with heat recovery to dramatically lower costs. Commercial pilots include a 1,000 t/y unit being integrated with a Coca-Cola Europacific Partners bottling plant.
