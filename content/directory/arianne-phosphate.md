## About

Arianne Phosphate is a development-stage phosphate mining company listed on the TSX Venture Exchange (TSXV: DAN). It is advancing the Lac à Paul phosphate project in Quebec's Saguenay region. It has a joint venture framework agreement with Travertine Technologies to make purified phosphoric acid, with a carbonate by-product that stores CO2 and could earn carbon removal credits. Mining and fertilizer feedstock are its main business, so CDR is an adjacent activity.
