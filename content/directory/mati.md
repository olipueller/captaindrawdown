## About

Mati Carbon is a carbon removal company based in Houston. Shantanu Agarwal founded it in 2022, and it runs enhanced rock weathering on smallholder farms in India, Zambia and Tanzania. It won the $50M grand prize of the XPRIZE Carbon Removal competition in 2025 and has issued CO2 removal certificates (CORCs) under Puro.earth's enhanced rock weathering methodology.
