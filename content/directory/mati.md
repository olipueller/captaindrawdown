## About

Mati Carbon is a carbon removal supplier that deploys pulverized basalt onto smallholder farms in India, Tanzania, and Zambia to accelerate enhanced rock weathering. The company won the $50M XPRIZE Carbon Removal grand prize in 2025 and secured blended finance from J.P. Morgan backed by the Schmidt Family Foundation to expand across the Global South.
