## About

XPRIZE Carbon Removal was a $100M incentive competition funded by the Musk Foundation, launched on Earth Day 2021 and concluded in April 2025 when Mati Carbon won the $50M grand prize for enhanced rock weathering. Runners-up included NetZero (biochar), Vaulted Deep (biomass burial), and UNDO Carbon (ERW), with XFACTOR awards to Planetary and Project Hajar. The competition page is run by the XPRIZE Foundation, a nonprofit that designs and operates incentive prizes — not a CDR supplier itself but a major catalyst of the CDR ecosystem.
