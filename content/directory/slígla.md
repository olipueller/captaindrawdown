## About

Slígla is an early-stage biochar startup based in Sligo, Ireland. It turns forestry, sawmill and invasive-species residues into biochar for farmland. It is backed by Enterprise Ireland and won InterTradeIreland's 2025 Seedcorn Deep Tech award for Connacht and Leinster.
