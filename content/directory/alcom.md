## About

Alcom is a Singapore-based renewable energy and carbon developer that operates the first biochar production facility in the Philippines — the Nueva Ecija rice husk project registered under Puro.earth. The Alcom-THL joint venture in India converts cotton stalks and an invasive tree species into biochar and bio-oil, with projects fully integrated from feedstock sourcing to on-site pyrolysis and verification. ACM is the carbon project development arm of Alcom, a local energy trading company involved in the physical trade of fuels and first- and second-generation biofuels.
