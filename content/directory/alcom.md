## About

Alcom is a Singapore-based renewable energy and carbon project developer that operates biochar production facilities, including the Puro.earth-certified NuevaChar rice husk biochar plant in Nueva Ecija, Philippines, and a joint venture with THL in India converting cotton stalks and invasive tree species. The company oversees end-to-end biochar carbon removal — from feedstock sourcing through pyrolysis to credit issuance — and also sells bio-oil, heat, and bio-fertilisers as co-products.
