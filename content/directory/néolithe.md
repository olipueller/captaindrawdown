## About

Néolithe is a French industrial startup (based in Anjou, ~100-200 employees) that operates "Fossilizers" — machines that transform non-recyclable, non-hazardous waste into mineral aggregates via its Accelerated Fossilization process, sequestering biogenic carbon contained in the waste. The aggregates substitute natural aggregates in concrete and are claimed to store ~337-420 kg CO2e per tonne. The company raised €60M in 2023 and is scaling plants across France.
