## About

Carbonloop is a Paris-based startup launched in 2021 by the investment firm Kouros. It sells industrial clients a decarbonization service: local biomass is turned on site into renewable heat, power or hydrogen, and the biochar co-product is stored as a carbon sink. In 2023 it ordered HYNOCA equipment from Haffner Energy for its first two hydrogen and biochar sites, with the hydrogen to be sold through Hyliko, Kouros's sister company.
