## About

MOVA Technologies develops advanced air filtration systems that capture airborne contaminants, such as ammonia, from industrial and agricultural environments, particularly poultry houses. While they mention "air capture technology" and "selectively capture and harvest air contaminants as valuable byproducts," the results do not explicitly state they are a Direct Air Capture (DAC) company for CO2 removal.
