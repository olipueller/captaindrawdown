## About

Desert Ocean is developing cutting-edge technology solutions to capture, store, and utilize CO2 to solve the climate crisis and provide carbon-neutral renewable fuels. Their method involves creating organisms that produce butanol through direct secretion into cultivation liquid, indicating a bio-based carbon capture and utilization approach.
