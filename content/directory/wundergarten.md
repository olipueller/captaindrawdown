## About

Wundergarten is a German consumer gardening brand founded in 2019 that sells biochar-based soils, fertilizers, and biochar-coated seeds to retail customers via its webshop, Amazon, and selected supermarkets. The biochar is made from cocoa shells and herbs via pyrolysis (EBC-certified), and the company markets CO2 storage in soil as a co-benefit. They function as a biochar product formulator/distributor rather than a credit-issuing CDR project developer.
