## About

bionero GmbH is a small biochar producer based in Thurnau, Bavaria (Germany), founded in 2018. They carbonize organic residues using PYREG pyrolysis systems to make EBC-certified biochar, which they incorporate into peat-free potting soils and related products sold to consumers and horticultural markets.
