## About

Bio-Restorative Ideas (BRi) is a Puerto Rico-based biochar producer that transforms bamboo residues into biochar using ARTi pyrolysis equipment installed in 2023. The project is registered on Puro.earth with Zurich among its credit buyers, and is managed in partnership with carbon developer GECA Environnement.
