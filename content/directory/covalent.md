## About

Covalent (Covalent Climate Tech, Inc.) is a San Francisco-based climate fintech that operates a forward-crediting platform, certification standard, and blockchain-based registry for permanent carbon dioxide removal projects. It does not remove CO2 itself; it originates credits and connects CDR developers (e.g. Carbon Limit's CaptureCrete) with financing, requiring a 1,000-year permanence threshold and third-party validation (Bureau Veritas). The company has more recently extended its platform toward EU CBAM reporting for importers.
