## About

Covalent Climate Tech is a San Francisco climate fintech startup that runs a forward-crediting platform, certification standard and registry for permanent carbon removal projects. It launched in October 2023, and its first issuance was close to 1 million forward credits from Carbon Limit's CaptureCrete cement additive. It does not remove carbon itself.
