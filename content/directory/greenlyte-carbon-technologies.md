## About

Greenlyte Carbon Technologies is a German DAC company founded in 2022, headquartered in Essen, that has developed a liquid-sorbent Direct Air Capture process which co-produces green hydrogen alongside captured CO2. The company operates both CDR (via a partnership with Deep Sky in Quebec) and CCU pathways (DAC-to-eMethanol, DAC-to-SAF, DAC-to-SNG), with its first LiquidSolar SNG plant inaugurated in Duisburg in November 2025 and a 500-ton commercial module planned at Chempark Marl in 2026.
