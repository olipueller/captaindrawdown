## About

Hourglass Climate is a US nonprofit that studies how safe and effective mineral-based ocean alkalinity enhancement (OAE) is as a way to remove carbon. It ran the independent multi-year monitoring program for Vesta's coastal enhanced weathering pilot in Duck, North Carolina, and contributes to OAE field-research frameworks. It does not remove or sell carbon itself.
