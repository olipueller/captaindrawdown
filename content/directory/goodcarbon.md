## About

goodcarbon is a Berlin-based platform that sources, assesses, and sells nature-based solutions carbon credits (reforestation, regenerative agriculture, blue carbon) to corporates building carbon portfolios. It is a marketplace/broker in the CDR ecosystem rather than a physical removal supplier, though it includes some removal projects alongside avoidance credits.
