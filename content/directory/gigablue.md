## About

Gigablue is an ocean-based CDR startup (founded in Israel, headquartered in New York) that uses geo-specific engineered particles to grow phytoplankton and sink captured carbon to the deep ocean. It has completed multiple trials off New Zealand, signed a 200,000-ton offtake with SkiesFifty, and is pursuing a Puro.earth methodology, though its approach has drawn scientific scrutiny over parallels to iron fertilization.
