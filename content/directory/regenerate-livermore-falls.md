## About

ReGenerate Livermore Falls, LLC is a biomass power facility in Livermore Falls, Maine (operated by ReGenerate Energy Holdings / ReEnergy Biomass Maine) that produces biochar as a byproduct of its 39MW biomass electricity generation. The facility is Puro.earth-certified under the Biochar 2022 methodology with a crediting period running Feb 2024 – Feb 2029 and has been issued CORCs. It is one of the notable US biochar producers alongside Oregon Biochar Solutions and Wakefield Biochar.
