## About

The Carbon Contract is a Swedish marketplace that connects companies with certified, traceable carbon removal credits from Swedish biochar producers. The consultancy Ecotopic built the platform, with part of the funding from the Swedish research council Formas. The Carbon Contract does not produce biochar itself; it sells credits made by partner producers such as Solör Bioenergy.
