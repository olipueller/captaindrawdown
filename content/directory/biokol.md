## About

Biokol.se is a Swedish biochar company founded in 2018 that builds pyrolysis boilers (via sister firm Brachio AB) and aggregates small-scale producers who convert forestry residues into high-carbon biochar. In September 2025 the project became the first to have biochar credits validated under the Rainbow Standard for a 1,000-year permanence horizon, with roughly 2.6 tCO2e sequestered per tonne of biochar produced. In early 2026 Biokol.se announced a scale-up partnership with ZeroMission to distribute Swedish biochar carbon credits.
