## About

Wongphai Co., Ltd. is a community-based bamboo-to-biochar operation in Prachin Buri, eastern Thailand, founded by Komchalat Thongting and Saranrat Tanthiptham. It registered Thailand's first biochar CDR project with Carbon Standards International (Global Artisan C-Sink), working with Planboo, and the project received a Sylvera estimated rating of AA–BBB. It also supplies biochar for soil restoration and biochar concrete.
