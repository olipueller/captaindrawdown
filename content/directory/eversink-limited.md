## About

Eversink Limited is the supplier listed on Puro.earth for two biochar projects in China: the Nian'da Biochar Facility in Nanjing (Puro calls it the first Puro biochar project in China, operating, with 330 CORCs in its first crediting period) and the Baoshun Biochar Facility. Its website, eversink.com ('Eversink Carbon', Hillsboro Beach, Florida), describes a business set up only to remove carbon, using biomass agriculture and carbon-negative product replacements. Climeworks' biochar page shows imagery from Eversink's China project.
