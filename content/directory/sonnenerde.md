## About

Sonnenerde GmbH is an Austrian biochar producer based in Riedlingsdorf, Burgenland, operating since 2012 what it claims is the first waste-law-approved biochar plant in Europe. The company pyrolyzes agricultural and composting residues into EBC-certified biochar for soils, livestock and Terra Preta products, and issues Puro.earth carbon removal credits. A new ~10x larger pyrolysis facility ("KOHLOSS") opened on April 30, 2025.
