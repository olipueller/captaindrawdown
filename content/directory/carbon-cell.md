## About

Carbon Cell is a UK-based company that produces a carbon-negative, fully biodegradable, and plastic-free foam from biochar and natural binders, designed to replace conventional polymer-based foams. This process sequesters carbon, making it a BiCRS (Biochar Carbon Removal and Storage) method.
