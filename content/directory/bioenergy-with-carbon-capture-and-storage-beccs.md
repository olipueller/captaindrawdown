## About

<cite index="1-8,1-9">Drax's BECCS program captures and permanently stores CO2 generated during electricity production from sustainable biomass, with the captured CO2 stored in saline aquifers.</cite> <cite index="2-10,2-11">Drax planned two full-scale BECCS units by the end of the decade, with planning permission granted in January 2024.</cite> However, <cite index="2-2,2-3">the company has recently scaled back its CCS funding, pledging around £1m in H1 2025 versus £2m the prior year</cite>, and its US arm operates under the Elimini brand.
