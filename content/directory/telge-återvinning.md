## About

Telge Återvinning AB is a Swedish municipal waste management and recycling company that began producing biochar as a treatment for green waste in 2021 using first-of-its-kind-in-Europe CharMaker batch pyrolysis technology supplied by Australian firm Earth Systems. The Södertälje-based facility processes oversized green waste logs into biochar, and the project was successfully installed and commissioned in 2020 and registered for C-sink credits in 2021 by Telge. It operates as a subsidiary of the municipally owned Telge AB group.
