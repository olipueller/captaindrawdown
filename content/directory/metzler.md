## About

Metzler Biochar, a division of Metzler Forest Products LLC in Reedsville, Pennsylvania, produces wood-based biochar from local sawmill co-products using an industrial-scale rotary kiln pyrolysis system. The company sells biochar and biochar-amended soil blends for agriculture, stormwater management, and livestock applications, and recovers excess process heat to displace propane in firewood drying kilns.
