Amsterdam-based DAC startup (~15 employees) using electrochemical electrolyzers that run on intermittent renewables, with green hydrogen as a co-product to offset costs.

**Website:** [https://www.brineworks.com](https://www.brineworks.com)

**Location:** Netherlands

**Category:** Direct Air Capture

**Method:** DAC

**Tech:** Electrochemical DAC electrolyzer designed for intermittent operation on cheap renewable energy. Green hydrogen as co-product to improve economics. Targeting sub-$100/tonne by 2035.
