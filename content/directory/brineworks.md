## About

Brineworks is an Amsterdam-based climate tech startup founded in 2023 that is developing low-cost electrochemical Direct Air Capture. Its patented saltwater electrolyzer captures CO₂ from the atmosphere while co-producing green hydrogen, with outputs targeted at both carbon removal and e-fuels (SAF, e-methanol) for aviation and shipping. The company raised a €6.8M seed round in September 2025 and was awarded a €1.8M EIC Accelerator grant, aiming for pilot-scale commercial readiness by end of 2026.
