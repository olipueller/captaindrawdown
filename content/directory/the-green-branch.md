## About

The Green Branch is an Amsterdam-based impact-first carbon asset developer founded in 2019 that creates nature-based solutions projects for the voluntary carbon market. It co-develops initiatives like "Trees for Farmers" with Rabobank in Brazil's Amazon and works with the Suledo Forest Community Trust in Tanzania, bringing reforestation and landscape-restoration projects from concept to market readiness.
