## About

Nellie Technologies is a UK-based CDR supplier operating a pilot facility in Mwyndy, South Wales, that cultivates microalgae for CO2 capture and converts the biomass into durable biochar. The company sells Puro.earth CORCs and signed a 17,500-ton, 5-year offtake with Zurich Insurance in January 2025, and won the 2025 IOP Business Start-Up Award.
