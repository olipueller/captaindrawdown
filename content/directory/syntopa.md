## About

Syntopa is a US startup (founded 2023, based in Alameda, California) developing living microbial products that accelerate enhanced rock weathering (ERW) on croplands. They match microbial strains to specific soils using their proprietary Toposight platform, aiming to boost silicate dissolution rates and thereby durable CO2 removal. They sell to EW operators as a performance enhancer and have raised pre-seed funding from Ponderosa Ventures and Carbon Drawdown Initiative.
