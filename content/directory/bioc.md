## About

BioC is a Singapore-based biochar carbon-removal supplier that builds and runs PyroCCS pyrolysis facilities producing certified CDR credits and biocarbon products. They target both carbon-credit buyers (spot and long-term offtake) and industrial customers in steel/metallurgy seeking drop-in replacements for met coal and anthracite. They also offer an in-house MRV software stack ("Sarva Carbon") compatible with Carbon Standards International and Isometric registries.
