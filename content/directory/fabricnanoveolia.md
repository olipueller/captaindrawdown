## About

FabricNano, in partnership with Veolia, specializes in Enhanced Rock Weathering (ERW) for carbon dioxide removal. They use enzyme-treated rock particles, specifically ultramafic silicate rocks like basalt, to accelerate the natural carbon sequestration process.
