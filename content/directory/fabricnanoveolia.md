## About

FabricNano is a London-based biotechnology startup that develops enzyme-immobilization technology, most notably applying the carbonic anhydrase enzyme to basalt rock fines to accelerate enhanced rock weathering. In partnership with Veolia UK, the company ran a world-first field trial near Bicester (UK) spreading enzyme-coated basalt on farmland to accelerate CO2 sequestration from decades to a few years. The firm also applies its enzyme platform to non-CDR applications (bio-manufacturing with Ginkgo/Sumitomo, PFAS research), so CDR is one of multiple product lines.
