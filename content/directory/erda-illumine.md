## About

Erda Illumine Low Carbon Solutions Pvt. Ltd. is a small climate project developer in Kolkata, founded by Etti Khanna and Gagandeep Arya. Its portfolio is mostly emission-reduction projects under voluntary and Article 6 mechanisms, such as cookstoves, biomass pellets and regenerative agriculture. It also runs one carbon-removal line: a biochar project that uses agricultural and forestry residues.
