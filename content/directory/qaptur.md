## About

Qaptur is a Paris-based startup founded in 2024 that helps nature and impact investors and carbon project developers source, screen and do due diligence on early-stage nature-based projects. Its products are a project database (Explorer), deal matching (Launchpad) and Qaptur Academy, which trains grassroots projects. Its listings include biochar, regenerative agriculture and forestry projects. It acts as a market intermediary and does not remove carbon itself.
