## About

Truecoco is a Ghana-based biochar carbon removal developer that originated as an organic coconut processor in 2017 and launched West Africa's first industrial-scale biochar facility in 2025, using coconut husk waste as feedstock. The company is Puro.earth certified and signed a multi-year offtake agreement with Climeworks extending to 2032. It aims to remove 250,000 tonnes of CO2 by 2030 while benefiting smallholder farmers.
