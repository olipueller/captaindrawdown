## About

Moola is a German/Austrian biochar producer that uses pyrolysis to convert plant biomass into EBC-certified biochar, sold as a soil amendment, animal feed additive, and stable bedding. The company describes itself as an early industrial biochar pioneer in Germany, and is an active member of the Fachverband Pflanzenkohle industry association. Products remain available through retailers in 2024–2025.
