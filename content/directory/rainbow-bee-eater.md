## About

Rainbow Bee Eater (RBE) is a Melbourne-based biochar technology company that developed the ECHO2 pyrolysis system. Its first commercial module runs at the Holla-Fresh glasshouse in Tantanoola, South Australia, which was upgraded to a Mark 4 unit in June 2024. A six-module system is being installed at Katunga Fresh in Victoria to replace natural gas heating. RBE has sold biochar removal credits to Microsoft and Shopify.
