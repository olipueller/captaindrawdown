## About

Pacific Biochar is a California-based benefit corporation that partners with biomass power plants and sawmills to produce biochar from forestry residues. The biochar is distributed to agricultural operations, primarily vineyards and compost yards, and the company sells verified carbon removal credits under standards including Isometric's Biochar Protocol.
