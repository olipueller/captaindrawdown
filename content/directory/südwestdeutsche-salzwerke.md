## About

As part of its 2045 climate-neutrality strategy, it commissioned a SYNCRAFT wood-gasification "reverse power plant" at Heilbronn in December 2023 that generates electricity/heat and produces ~2.6 t/day of biochar from regional wood chips as a byproduct, marketed as garden substrate.
