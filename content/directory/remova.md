## About

Remova Carbon Systems is a Calgary-based CDR developer building compact, replicable BECCS facilities in Alberta using forestry residuals as feedstock with geological CO2 storage. In March 2026 it partnered with Mangrove Systems for digital MRV and is pursuing Isometric certification for its first facility. The team has a background in oil & gas EPC and energy finance.
