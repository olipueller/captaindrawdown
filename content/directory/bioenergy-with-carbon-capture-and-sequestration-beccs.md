## About

Babcock & Wilcox Enterprises (NYSE: BW) is an Akron, Ohio-based power and industrial technology firm whose ClimateBright™ suite includes BECCS carbon capture technologies. B&W serves as a technology supplier and EPC partner for biomass-to-energy plants with carbon capture, with active projects including the Filer City, Michigan BECCS conversion (up to 550,000 tons CO2/yr) and Fidelis New Energy's Project Cyclus in Louisiana.
