## About

Svante Inc. (website tagline "Forward-Thinking Carbon Capture") is a Vancouver/Burnaby-based carbon capture technology company that builds solid-sorbent filters and rotary contactor machines for industrial point-source CCS, BECCS and DAC. In May 2025 it commissioned its Redwood gigafactory in Burnaby and acquired Carbon Alpha, adding the North Star BECCS project which signed a 626,000-tonne CDR offtake with Microsoft. It also supplies filters to Climeworks and is partnering with Nikkiso on Canada's first DAC facility.
