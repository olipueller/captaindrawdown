## About

Riziki Nature Based Solutions (nbs-ea.com) produces biofuel from renewable biomass sources, offering a cleaner, high-energy solution for industrial processes. While the company name suggests nature-based solutions, the website focuses on biofuel production rather than direct carbon dioxide removal via biochar.
