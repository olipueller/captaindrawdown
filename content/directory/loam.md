## About

Loam Bio is an Australian agri-biotech company (founded 2019, HQ in Orange, NSW) that develops fungal endophyte microbial seed treatments to increase stable carbon sequestration in agricultural soils. Its CarbonBuilder product and SecondCrop carbon program enable broadacre grain farmers in Australia, the US, Canada, and Brazil to generate soil carbon credits. The company has raised ~$120M and employs around 77 people.
