## About

Wilson Biochar, LLC is a small Oregon business led by mechanical engineer and author Kelpie Wilson. It designed the Ring of Fire Biochar Kiln, now made and distributed under license by Tarter USA (deal announced September 2024), and offers biochar consulting, courses and the Biochar in the Woods outreach effort. It does not appear to sell carbon removal credits itself.
