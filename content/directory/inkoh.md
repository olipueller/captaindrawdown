## About

Inkoh AG is a Swiss biochar producer headquartered in Maienfeld, Graubünden, and a dedicated subsidiary of the construction group Zindel United Holding AG. It operates Biomacon pyrolysis plants producing biochar from FSC-certified forest residues, supplies district heating with waste heat, and issues CO2 removal certificates (via First Climate). A new main production site in Landquart ("Biochar Circle Switzerland") is under construction with commissioning planned for mid-2026.
