## About

Triochar is a biochar carbon removal developer. It works with smallholder farmers to turn agricultural waste into biochar for long-term carbon storage and better soils, and sells the resulting carbon removal credits to corporate buyers. It runs websites at triochar.io and in German at triochar.de.
