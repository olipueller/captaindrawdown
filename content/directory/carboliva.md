## About

Carboliva is a Spanish company based in Osuna (Seville), led by Álvaro Espuny, focused on valorizing olive grove biomass to create high-quality charcoal and clean thermal energy. The company produces biochar from olive pits through a controlled pyrolysis process in the absence of oxygen, fixing carbon in a solid, stable structure. It collaborates on research projects applying its biochar to olive cultivation, tomato agriculture, and concrete/asphalt construction materials.
