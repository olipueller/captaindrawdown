## About

Carboliva produces and commercializes sustainable biochar and vegetable charcoal from olive biomass (pits and pulp) through pyrolysis, which also generates clean thermal energy. This process fixes carbon absorbed by olive trees into a solid structure, preventing its release into the atmosphere.
