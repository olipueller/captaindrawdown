## About

Carbonity is a joint venture equally owned by Airex Energy, Groupe Rémabec, and SUEZ that operates Canada's first industrial-scale biochar plant in Port-Cartier, Quebec, inaugurated in May 2025. The facility starts at 10,000 tonnes/year biochar capacity, set to triple by 2026 to become North America's largest, and at full capacity will sequester 75,000 tCO2e per year. Carbon credits are marketed on the voluntary market by First Climate, and Microsoft signed a 36,000-credit offtake in 2024.
