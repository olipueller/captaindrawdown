## About

Aquarry is a US-based deep-tech CDR startup (founded 2023, Denver/California) applying pit lake alkalinity enhancement — a hybrid of enhanced rock weathering and ocean alkalinity enhancement — to flooded open-pit mines. The process uses alkaline tailings/waste rock to raise lake alkalinity, absorb atmospheric CO2, and form stable carbonates while remediating mine water. The company is partnered with Winsome Resources at the Renard site in Quebec and with Isometric as registry, with limited-scale field trials planned for spring 2026.
