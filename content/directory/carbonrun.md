## About

CarbonRun is a Canadian marine CDR company that uses Ocean Alkalinity Enhancement (specifically River Alkalinity Enhancement) by adding limestone to rivers to convert CO2 into stable bicarbonate ions, which are then carried to the ocean for long-term storage.
