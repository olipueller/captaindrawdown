## About

EcoRegistry is a carbon credit registry based in Medellín, Colombia. It grew into a leading Latin American registry because Colombia's carbon tax accepts credits as an offset. It connects to marketplaces and exchanges such as Carbonmark, ACX and BeZero, and lists mostly nature-based and avoidance credits rather than only engineered removals.
