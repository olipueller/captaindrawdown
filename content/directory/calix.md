## About

Calix Limited is an Australian industrial technology company listed on the ASX as CXL. It builds its business around a proprietary calcination kiln and sells magnesia products for agriculture and water treatment, along with battery materials. Its Leilac subsidiary works on decarbonising cement and lime, which is point-source capture rather than removal. Its only atmospheric CO2 removal link is a global licence to Heirloom for limestone-based direct air capture.
