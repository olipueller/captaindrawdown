## About

The New Black Biochar is a small Tasmanian grassroots biochar operation co-founded by Christina Giudici and Bodie Cavanagh, producing biochar from sawmill and organic waste streams. The company also runs community biochar-making workshops and is active in the Biochar Living Lab in George Town, Tasmania.
