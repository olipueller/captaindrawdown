## About

The New Black Biochar is a small Tasmanian biochar producer co-founded in 2021 by Christina Giudici and Bodie Cavanagh, making biochar from sawmill and other organic waste at a kiln co-located with Dovetail Timbers. The company sells biochar for home gardens, regenerative agriculture, and environmental remediation, and is working toward scaling up to full commercial production.
