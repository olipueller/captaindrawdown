## About

Carbon Collect is headquartered in Dublin, Ireland with a wholly owned subsidiary, Carbon Collect Inc. in Delaware, United States, commercializing the MechanicalTree passive DAC technology developed with ASU. In 2025, Carbon Collect installed its Gen-II MechanicalTree, marking a major advance for the DAC field. In January 2025, ASU received an $11.2 million grant to build a carbon capture hub in the Southwest, in partnership with Carbon Collect.
