## About

Oka, The Carbon Insurance Company is a carbon-credit insurer that sells cover to buyers and sellers in voluntary and compliance carbon markets. It has partnered with Cloverly, the Argentinian carbon exchange BACX, 1089 and Oregon Biochar Solutions, and in 2025 added green credit insurance. It does not remove carbon itself.
