## About

Valero Energy is a major U.S. oil refiner and the world's second-largest corn ethanol producer. Summit Carbon Solutions announced a partnership with Valero to connect eight of its ethanol plants in Iowa, Nebraska, Minnesota, and South Dakota to Summit's pipeline, which is expected to capture 3.1 million metric tons of CO2 each year. CDR is a minor part of Valero's overall business but the ethanol-plant CO2 capture qualifies as BECCS when paired with geologic storage.
