## About

Lynemouth Power Limited operates a 420MW biomass power station in Northumberland, UK, converted from coal to wood pellets, owned by EP UK Investments (part of Czech energy group EPH). The company is developing a BECCS retrofit and has passed the UK Government's Track-1 Phase 2 deliverability assessment, entering formal bilateral talks with DESNZ for potential post-2027 negative-emissions operation. Electricity generation remains the primary business; BECCS is an adjacent future revenue stream.
