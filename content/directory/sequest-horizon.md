## About

Sequest Horizon is the first commercial carbon-removal facility of the startup Sequest, Inc., located in Saudi Arabia and targeting about 1,000 tonnes of CO2 removal. The project passed Puro.earth's Preliminary Assessment, has an MoU with Red Sea Global, and its Puro facility page now says CORCs have been issued.
