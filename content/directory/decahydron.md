## About

Decahydron is an Irish startup developing an integrated natural hydrogen extraction and CO2 mineralization process in ultramafic rock formations, primarily in the UAE (Ras Al Khaimah, Sharjah) and wider MENA. Natural hydrogen production is its principal commercial product, with CO2 mineralization coupled as a climate co-benefit of the same well operations. Backed by a November 2025 strategic investment from Weatherford and collaborating with SNOC, Siemens Energy, and RAK Petroleum Authority.
