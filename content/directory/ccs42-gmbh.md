## About

CCS42 GmbH is an early-stage startup in Lübeck, Germany (HRB 26247 HL), founded around 2025 by Arne Knöchel and Steffen Labitzke. It builds decentralized biomass thermolysis plants that produce biochar, heat and bio-oil, and it markets the biochar, heat and CO2 removal certificates. In September 2025 it was one of the winners of Schleswig-Holstein's Überflieger startup competition.
