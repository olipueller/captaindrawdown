## About

Sinc.Earth (also styled SINC) is an early-stage enhanced rock weathering startup running pilots on smallholder farms around Kumasi, Ghana, and presents itself as Ghana's first agricultural ERW project. It pairs carbon removal with soil restoration and yield co-benefits for farmers. LinkedIn posts name Youp Dumas as the founder/lead and say the team recently added a Head of Science and pilot staff.
