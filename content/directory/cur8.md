## About

CUR8 is a CDR Platform/Marketplace that provides market intelligence, due diligence, and financing solutions for corporate CDR buyers and investors. They facilitate the procurement and management of various carbon removal credits, including biochar, enhanced rock weathering, and direct air capture.
