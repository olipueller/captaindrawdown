## About

La Belle Forêt is a French startup founded in 2021 that develops forest carbon projects on French forests (including the Domaine national de Chambord) and markets certified carbon credits under the French Label Bas Carbone scheme. The company combines carbon sequestration with biodiversity and water preservation, using field inventories, lidar, satellites and AI for MRV. In early 2025 the company entered a court-ordered "procédure de sauvegarde" (safeguard/insolvency-protection proceedings).
