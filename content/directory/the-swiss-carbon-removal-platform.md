## About

The Swiss Carbon Removal Platform (CDR Swiss) is a Swiss member-based industry association launched in 2021 by the Risk Dialogue Foundation (Risiko-Dialog). It brings together 60+ member organizations from research, industry, government, and civil society to promote carbon dioxide removal policy, innovation, and knowledge-sharing in Switzerland. It does not itself remove CO2 but acts as a networking, dialogue, and policy platform for the Swiss CDR ecosystem.
