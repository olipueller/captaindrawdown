## About

The Swiss Carbon Removal Platform (CDR Swiss) is a Swiss multi-stakeholder association that the Risiko-Dialog Foundation launched in 2021. It brings together research, business, government and civil society to support responsible CDR through knowledge sharing, working groups (including a policy group), events and policy white papers.
