## About

Carbfix is an Icelandic company that permanently stores CO2 by dissolving it in water and injecting it into basaltic rock formations, where it mineralizes into stable carbonate minerals within approximately two years. It operates point-source mineralization at the Hellisheiði and Nesjavellir geothermal plants and partners with DAC providers like Climeworks (Orca/Mammoth) for storage. It is developing the Coda Terminal, a large-scale transport-and-mineralization hub.
