## About

FETZER Rohstoffe + Recycling GmbH is a German recycling company based in Eislingen that has been producing biochar from wood waste for over a decade under its Moola brand. EBC-certified since 2013, its three pyrolysis plants produce around 1,500 m³ of biochar per year, which is primarily sold as a soil amendment. Biochar credits are offered via marketplaces such as Klimate.
