## About

Return Carbon deploys integrated solutions for efficient DACCS (Direct Air Carbon Capture and Storage) and BECCS (Bioenergy with Carbon Capture and Storage) developments, enabling accelerated and low-risk technology implementation. They are involved in developing DAC plants and test facilities.
