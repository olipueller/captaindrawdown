## About

Earthcare Solutions is a U.S. biochar producer that deploys patented gasification systems to valorize challenging high-ash feedstocks such as wastewater biosolids, poultry manure, and MSW digestate. Projects are certified under Puro.earth's biochar methodology, with 12 gasifiers running as of mid-2025 and a 13th coming online at Minnich Eggs. The company sells carbon credits alongside biochar products for soil and filtration applications.
