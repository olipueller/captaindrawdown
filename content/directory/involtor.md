## About

Involtor (Involtor Consult SRL) is a Romania-based soil carbon project developer that partners with farmers to implement regenerative agricultural practices — reduced tillage, cover cropping, residue retention, optimized fertilization, and improved water management — across Chernozem black soils. Their flagship Involtor Future Agriculture East-Europe Project (Verra VCS Project 3973) was the first project globally to issue Verified Carbon Units under Verra's VM0042 Improved Agricultural Land Management methodology, verifying 64,645 VCUs in January 2025 across 4,170 hectares.
