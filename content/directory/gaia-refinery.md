## About

Gaia Refinery utilizes a Bio-Direct Air Capture (Bio-DAC) plug-in technology, specifically a liquid DAC capture system, to remove CO2 from the atmosphere and from biomass waste streams by isolating CO2 absorbed during photosynthesis.
