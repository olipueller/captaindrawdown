## About

Eion is a US-based enhanced rock weathering carbon removal company that pioneered olivine-based deployments on Mid-Atlantic and Midwest farmland, delivering early ERW credits to Stripe, Frontier ($33M offtake), and Microsoft (8,000 tons). In February 2026, Eion was acquired by Terradot to form a combined global ERW platform with over 400,000 tonnes of contracted carbon removal; Eion's operations, contracts, and team were folded into Terradot.
