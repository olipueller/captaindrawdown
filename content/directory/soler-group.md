## About

SOLER Group is a French family-owned producer of renewable carbon (biochar, biocarbon, charcoal) operating three biorefineries with ~50,000 t/yr capacity, using proprietary pyrolysis technology that also generates green electricity for ~20,000 households. Biochar is EBC-certified and sold as a durable CDR solution through Carbonfuture, with >4,000 tCO2e of credits issued. The company is expanding internationally, including a planned large biocarbon plant in the US Appalachians.
