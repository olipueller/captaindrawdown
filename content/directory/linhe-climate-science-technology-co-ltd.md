## About

Linhe Climate Science & Technology Co. Ltd (also known as Linhe Climate Technology) is a scientific and technology innovation company focused on low-cost, high-efficiency, distributed, need-based carbon capture technologies, specifically Direct Air Capture (DAC) using solid sorbents, and multi-path utilization technologies.
