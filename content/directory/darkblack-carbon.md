## About

DarkBlack Carbon is a UK start-up based near Market Harborough, Leicestershire, and co-founded by Liz Casely. It makes biochar from council-collected woody green waste and sells the biochar along with Puro.earth-certified carbon removal credits. It presents itself as one of the UK's first large-scale commercial biochar carbon removal providers.
