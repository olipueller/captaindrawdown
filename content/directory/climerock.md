## About

ClimeRock is a French ERW (Enhanced Rock Weathering) startup founded in 2023 and based near Paris, with operations in the Massif Central. It spreads finely ground basalt on agricultural fields to sequester CO2 as bicarbonate while improving soil health, selling removal credits to corporate buyers.
