## About

Kanadevia Inova (formerly Hitachi Zosen Inova) is a Swiss-based Waste-to-Energy and Renewable Gas EPC contractor, subsidiary of Japan's Kanadevia Corporation. Its primary business is building waste-to-energy and biogas plants globally, with carbon capture added as an adjacent technology offering. It is serving as EPC contractor on Encyclis's Protos ERF carbon capture facility (~370,000 t CO2/yr via amine scrubbing, storage via HyNet in Liverpool Bay), scheduled for handover in mid-2029.
