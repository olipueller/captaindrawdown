## About

Freres Lumber (Freres Engineered Wood) is a family-owned Oregon manufacturer of plywood, veneer, and Mass Ply engineered wood products whose biomass cogeneration facility produces biochar as a byproduct. The biochar project is registered on Puro.earth and has supplied CORCs to Microsoft via ACT Group. Biochar is a secondary revenue line to the core lumber business, but the firm is a bona fide supplier of durable carbon removal credits.
