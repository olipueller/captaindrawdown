## About

Summit Carbon Solutions is developing a carbon capture and transport pipeline system across the Midwest to capture CO2 from industrial facilities, primarily ethanol plants, for permanent storage. They offer carbon dioxide removal credits, specifically using BECCS (Bioenergy with Carbon Capture and Storage).
