## About

Carbo Culture is a climate technology company that uses its patented Carbolysis™ reactors to convert waste biomass into stable biochar, locking carbon away for centuries and generating renewable energy. They produce premium biochar for carbon removal, agricultural use, and to decarbonize heavy industry.
