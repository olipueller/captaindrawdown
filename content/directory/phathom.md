## About

pHathom Technologies is a Halifax, Nova Scotia-based cleantech startup founded in 2024 that captures biogenic CO2 from coastal bioenergy facilities and converts it via a limestone/seawater reactor into stable bicarbonate, which is returned to the ocean for durable storage. The company closed a $4M seed round in February 2026 (led by Propeller Ventures, with Carmeuse Ventures, NBIF and Invest Nova Scotia), bringing total committed capital to over $12M including a lead role in a $16M Ocean Supercluster project, and was selected for Frontier's advance-purchase program (~US$500K). It plans a pilot at Huntsman Marine Science Centre in 2026 and a college biomass-boiler deployment in 2027.
