## About

pHathom is a Canadian climate technology startup that captures CO2 from coastal bioenergy plants using a weathering reactor with limestone, seawater, and biocatalysts to create bicarbonate, effectively performing Bioenergy with Carbon Capture and Storage (BECCS).
