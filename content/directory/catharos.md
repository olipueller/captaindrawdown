## About

Catheros (seeded as "Catharos") is a direct air capture startup spun out of the Stanford Sustainability Accelerator's greenhouse gas removal cohort. Steven Chu, Yi Cui and Arun Majumdar led the underlying research. It launched as a venture-backed startup in 2025 and is building a pilot facility to check the energy balance and economics of its electrochemical capture process.
