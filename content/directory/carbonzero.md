## About

CarbonZero.Eco is a Silicon Valley-based biochar startup founded by Harper Moss that converts California almond shells into biochar via pyrolysis, partnering with almond growers in Colusa and Yolo Counties. In December 2025 it commissioned its first commercial biochar facility in Colusa County and announced a carbon removal credit purchase agreement with Climeworks. The company is registered as a biochar supplier on the Puro.earth registry.
