## About

280 Earth is a Google X / Alphabet spin-out (2022) headquartered in Mountain View, CA that operates a 500 t/yr DAC commercial demonstration plant in The Dalles, Oregon (online May 2024). It holds a $40M Frontier offtake for 61,571 tons of CDR between 2024-2030, raised a $50M Series B led by Builders VC in 2024, and is scaling to 5,000 t/yr Gen-1 modules with a targeted 20,000+ t/yr site capacity by late 2025/early 2026.
