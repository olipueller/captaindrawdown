**Carbon Herald just published [Veolia Trials UK-First Non-Amine Carbon Capture Tech](https://carbonherald.com/veolia-trials-uk-first-non-amine-carbon-capture-tech/).**

Carbon Herald reports that resource management firm Veolia has partnered with Sheffield City Council to trial a next-generation carbon capture technology that avoids the use of amines. The pilot is described as a UK first for this type of non-amine capture chemistry. Amine-based solvents are the dominant approach in post-combustion capture but come with degradation, energy penalty and emissions concerns, which is why alternative sorbents are drawing attention. The outlet frames the deployment as a test of whether an alternative capture route can work at municipal scale in a real operational setting.

_Our take (Heads-up):_ A non-amine pilot is worth watching because amine solvent degradation and volatile emissions are real weak points in current capture stacks. The open questions are which sorbent chemistry is actually being used, the energy penalty per tonne captured, and what happens to the CO2 downstream. Until capture rates and cost figures are published, treat this as an early-stage trial rather than a proven alternative.
