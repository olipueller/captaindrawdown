**Carbon Herald just published [UpLink And World Economic Forum To Back Early-Stage CDR Innovations In A New Call](https://carbonherald.com/uplink-and-world-economic-forum-to-back-early-stage-cdr-innovations-in-a-new-call/).**

Carbon Herald reports that UpLink, in partnership with the World Economic Forum, has launched a new innovation call aimed at backing early-stage carbon dioxide removal ventures. The initiative is positioned as a way to surface and support emerging CDR approaches at a stage where capital and visibility are typically limited. Selected innovators are expected to gain access to the WEF and UpLink network, which has previously run similar calls across climate and ocean-focused challenges. The piece frames this as part of a broader effort to widen the pipeline of removal technologies beyond the handful of approaches currently dominating procurement and headlines.

_Our take (Heads-up):_ Early-stage exposure through UpLink can help founders, but selection and network access are not the same as durable funding or offtake. The real test is whether participating CDR ventures convert visibility into project finance and verified tonnes. Worth tracking which removal pathways the cohort skews toward.
