**Carbon Herald just published [UpLink And World Economic Forum To Back Early-Stage CDR Innovations In A New Call](https://carbonherald.com/uplink-and-world-economic-forum-to-back-early-stage-cdr-innovations-in-a-new-call/).**

Carbon Herald reports that UpLink, the open innovation platform of the World Economic Forum, has launched a new challenge aimed at backing early-stage CDR ventures. The initiative is designed to surface and support startups working on durable carbon removal approaches, with selected innovators expected to gain access to WEF networks, mentorship, and visibility within the climate community. The call fits into a broader pattern of WEF-led UpLink challenges that have previously targeted ocean health, freshwater, and other climate themes. Further details on eligibility, timelines, and funding pathways are covered in the full article.

_Our take (Heads-up):_ Programs like UpLink can give small CDR teams useful exposure, but visibility is not the same as durable funding or offtake. The real test is whether selected ventures move into measurable removal projects with credible MRV, rather than staying in the pitch and pilot loop. Worth tracking which cohorts actually deliver tonnes.
