**CDR.fyi just published [2026 Q1 Durable CDR Market Update - From Promise to Proof](https://www.cdr.fyi/blog/2026-q1-durable-cdr-market-update-from-promise-to-proof).**

CDR.fyi's Q1 2026 update finds 2.3 million tonnes of durable CDR contracted, roughly 5.6x Q1 2025 volume. Microsoft anchored the quarter with a single 1-million-tonne deal, while 112+ other buyers contracted 1.3 million tonnes between them, the third-highest non-Microsoft quarterly figure on record. Biochar dominated, making up 93% of contracted volume and all top five transactions. Deliveries reached 145K tonnes and retirements just over 100K tonnes, both second-highest on record, with deliveries up 67% year over year. Intermediaries facilitated 74% of contracted tonnes, a record share.

_Our take (Context):_ The headline number looks strong, but the report itself flags the concentration risk: one buyer drove 43% of volume and biochar accounted for nearly all of it. The pace is also down from mid-2025 highs. Worth watching whether buyer breadth and method diversity expand, or whether the market stays dependent on Microsoft and a few intermediaries.
