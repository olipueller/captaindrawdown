**Heatmap News just published [Carbon Removal After Microsoft](https://heatmap.news/carbon-removal/carbon-removal-after-microsoft).**

Heatmap News reports that Microsoft has informed suppliers it is pausing purchases of carbon removal credits, though the company says the program is not ended and it still aims to be carbon negative by 2030. Microsoft had bought more than 70 million tons of removal credits, including 45 million tons last year, dwarfing the next largest buyer, Frontier, at 1.8 million tons since 2022. The piece notes carbon removal startups have raised over $3.6 billion based partly on the assumption Microsoft would keep buying. Industry advisors quoted in the article say many business models depended on that single customer.

_Our take (Heads-up):_ The concentration risk here was visible for years, and the article makes a fair case that one buyer accounting for 80% of demand was never a stable market. Worth watching whether this is a pacing change tied to Microsoft's AI-driven emissions growth or a genuine retreat. Missing from the picture: how Frontier, oil majors, and any government procurement might absorb the gap.
