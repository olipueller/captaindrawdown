<p>Eight hundred thousand people flush their toilets in the Oslo region. Now, for the first time ever, the CO₂ from processing that sewage is being permanently stored 8,500 feet under the North Sea.</p><p>Inherit Carbon Solutions, HoopCO2, and the Northern Lights joint venture have achieved something the CDR world has been waiting for: the first permanent geological storage of biogenic CO₂ from biogas production. This isn&rsquo;t a pilot. It&rsquo;s not a feasibility study. It&rsquo;s operational BECCS with verified permanent storage.</p><p>As Inherit CEO Kaja Voss put it: &ldquo;For the first time, biogenic CO₂ from biogas production is being permanently stored underground.&rdquo;</p><h2 id=the-infrastructure-chain>The infrastructure chain<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#the-infrastructure-chain>#</a></h2><p>Here&rsquo;s how Oslo&rsquo;s sewage becomes carbon removal:</p><ol><li>The <strong>Veas wastewater treatment plant</strong> in Slemmestad processes waste from 800,000+ people in the Oslo region</li><li>Biogas production at the plant generates biogenic CO₂ as a byproduct</li><li><strong>Inherit Carbon Solutions</strong> and <strong>HoopCO2</strong> capture and liquefy that CO₂</li><li>The liquid CO₂ is transported to the <strong>Northern Lights terminal</strong> at Øygarden on Norway&rsquo;s west coast</li><li>From Øygarden, it&rsquo;s piped to a geological storage formation <strong>8,500 feet beneath the North Sea</strong></li><li>Credits are certified through <strong>Puro.earth</strong></li></ol><p>Every link in that chain is now operational. Northern Lights — a joint venture between Equinor, Shell, and TotalEnergies — has been running since 2025, but this is its first biogenic CO₂ from a wastewater source.</p><h2 id=why-wastewater-is-the-sleeper-cdr-pathway>Why wastewater is the sleeper CDR pathway<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#why-wastewater-is-the-sleeper-cdr-pathway>#</a></h2><p>Every city on Earth processes wastewater. Most of that processing generates biogas, which contains biogenic CO₂ — carbon that was recently in the atmosphere and got captured by plants, eaten by humans, and flushed down the drain. Normally that CO₂ gets vented back into the atmosphere.</p><p>Capture it and store it permanently, and you&rsquo;ve got textbook BECCS: bioenergy with carbon capture and storage. The biomass cycle removes CO₂ from the air, and geological storage keeps it out permanently.</p><p>The elegance is that the CO₂ source is <em>already concentrated</em> at the wastewater plant. You don&rsquo;t need to build a DAC facility to pull dilute CO₂ from ambient air. You just need to capture what&rsquo;s already coming off an existing industrial process. That&rsquo;s cheaper, simpler, and more energy-efficient.</p><h2 id=europe-is-building-while-the-us-debates>Europe is building while the US debates<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#europe-is-building-while-the-us-debates>#</a></h2><p>Northern Lights now has operational CO₂ transport and storage infrastructure that any European emitter or CDR project can plug into. It&rsquo;s shared infrastructure — like a highway for carbon dioxide.</p><p>The US, meanwhile, is still fighting over pipeline permits and Class VI well approvals. The EPA has issued a handful of Class VI permits ever. Europe isn&rsquo;t just ahead on policy — they&rsquo;re ahead on pipes in the ground and wells that are actually injecting.</p><p>Norway turned its sewage into a carbon removal asset. That&rsquo;s the kind of unsexy, infrastructure-heavy CDR that actually scales.</p><hr><h3 id=sources>Sources<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#sources>#</a></h3><ul><li>Inherit Carbon Solutions / HoopCO2 / Northern Lights JV announcement, March 2026</li><li>Northern Lights project operations documentation (operational since 2025)</li><li>Puro.earth credit certification</li><li>Veas wastewater treatment plant, Slemmestad, Norway</li></ul><hr><h3 id=-related-reading>🔗 Related Reading<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#-related-reading>#</a></h3><ul><li><a href=/posts/carbon-centric-beccs-certification/>Norway&rsquo;s Carbon Centric BECCS Plant Moves Toward Puro.earth Certification</a></li><li><a href=/posts/svante-acquires-carbon-alpha-beccs/>Svante Acquires Carbon Alpha: BECCS Meets First Nations Partnership</a></li><li><a href=/posts/eu-industrial-accelerator-act/>The EU&rsquo;s Industrial Accelerator Act: What It Means for Carbon Removal</a></li><li><a href=/posts/mercedes-f1-cdr-portfolio/>Mercedes F1 Goes All-In on Carbon Removal — 7 New Projects Across 6 Pathways</a></li></ul>
