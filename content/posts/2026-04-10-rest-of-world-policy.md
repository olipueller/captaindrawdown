Most CDR policy coverage fixates on the US and EU, but Carbon Brief's rest-of-world tracker shows dozens of countries quietly building their own frameworks. The gap between what gets attention and what's actually happening is striking. If you think carbon removal policy is a rich-country story, you're missing most of the map.
