> Take on a podcast episode from **The CDR Policy Scoop**, originally published Wed, 29 Ap.
> Listen: <https://shows.acast.com/the-cdr-policy-scoop/episodes/digging-deep-with-gabrielle-walker-a-life-in-climate>

[On The CDR Policy Scoop, hosts Sebastian Manhart and Eve Tamme launch a new long-form sub-series called *Digging Deep* with Gabrielle Walker — climate scientist, former Nature and BBC science journalist, Antarctic expedition veteran, and co-founder of both CUR8 and Rethinking Removals.](https://shows.acast.com/the-cdr-policy-scoop/episodes/digging-deep-with-gabrielle-walker-a-life-in-climate) This is explicitly a profile episode, not a deal-flow or policy briefing. The conversation walks Walker's arc from nature writing through journalism into the operational side of carbon removal, with the back third turning to corporate buyer behavior and market-building. Set expectations accordingly: it's a personal interview, longer than the show's usual 30 minutes, and the substantive Carbon Dioxide Removal (CDR) content is concentrated in the second half.

What's worth knowing for practitioners: per the show notes, Walker discusses where she has *changed her mind* on big CDR questions — which, given her platform across CUR8's portfolio approach and Rethinking Removals' convening work, is non-trivial signal about how serious buyer-side thinking is evolving. The hosts also press on the practical question of what actually moves corporate buyers from intent to purchase orders — a gap that has defined the 2024-2026 market more than any technology curve. The framing of "doubt as a strength" and curiosity as an underrated skill reads as a deliberate counter to the certainty-merchant tone that has crept into parts of the CDR discourse. Without an audio transcript I can't quote specific claims, but the show notes flag that Walker addresses "how to build markets that can unlock real capital for the people building solutions" — i.e. the supplier financing problem, not just the offtake problem.

For context on Walker's operating world: [CUR8](https://cur8.earth/) sells diversified durable removal portfolios to corporate buyers, sitting in the same broker-curator layer as Frontier and Isometric-adjacent players. [Rethinking Removals](https://rethinkingremovals.com/) is her narrative-and-convening project aimed at shifting how policymakers and buyers frame durable CDR versus avoidance. Listeners who want to track how buyer-side narrative is being constructed should pair this with prior Scoop episodes featuring Eli Mitchell-Larson and the recurring policy roundtables — Walker, Mitchell-Larson, and Tamme are arguably the three most consistent voices shaping the European durable-removal buyer narrative right now.

Useful for: anyone on the commercial or policy side of durable CDR who wants to understand the worldview behind CUR8's portfolio thesis and the Rethinking Removals framing. Skip if you're looking for technical pathway depth, measurement, reporting, and verification (MRV) detail, or specific market data — this is a person episode, not a numbers episode.
