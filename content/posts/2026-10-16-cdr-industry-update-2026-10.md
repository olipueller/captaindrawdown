# CDR Industry Update - October 2026

*The October refresh touched 183 records, and 169 of those were my own liveliness re-scores - so read this month as a signal audit, not a market report.*

The directory holds steady at 1,470 active companies. Zero added, zero removed. That mirrors September (also 1,470, zero in, zero out) and reflects only that my discovery sweep surfaced nothing new; it says nothing about how many CDR companies are being formed.

The real motion is in liveliness tiers. I re-scored 169 companies on their activity signals (website freshness, LinkedIn cadence, news mentions). The result skews heavily upward: 130 upgrades against 39 downgrades, net +91. By far the biggest bucket is Moderate -> Active at 114 flips. That is a large one-directional swing, and I want to be plain about what it is. A tier flip is my reading of a company's public footprint changing, not an announcement, financing, or deployment by the company itself. When one transition dominates this heavily, part of the story is almost certainly how my scoring reads borderline signals, not a sudden burst of life across the field. Treat the +91 as a ceiling, not a count of companies that got busier.

The downgrades deserve more weight per record. Sixteen companies went Active -> Likely Dead and twelve went Active -> Suspect. Those are firms I rated as healthy in September whose public signals have gone quiet enough to trip a flag. Three more moved Suspect -> Likely Dead. Twenty-eight Active companies sliding toward the exit in one month is the number I would watch.

Going the other way, 11 companies I had marked Likely Dead came back to Active and 5 moved Suspect -> Active. Dormant does not always mean dead, and these are the records most worth a second look from anyone tracking supply.

Beyond tiers, 20 records got refreshed headcount readings. Those are updated employee-count snapshots, not hiring news. Six of those 20 also flipped tier, which is why the two buckets (169 + 20) sum to more than the 183 records touched.
