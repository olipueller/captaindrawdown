<p>ClimeFi just dropped its 2026 durable carbon removal market insights, and the headline number is striking: 66% of CDR projects across all pathways are now at the commercialization stage.</p><p>That&rsquo;s across biochar, DACCS, BECCS, biomass approaches, marine CDR, and mineralization. Two-thirds of the industry has moved past R&amp;D and pilots into actual commercial operations.</p><h2 id=the-supply-shift>The Supply Shift<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#the-supply-shift>#</a></h2><p>Perhaps more important than the commercialization milestone is where the growth is coming from. ClimeFi signals that 2026 credit issuances are expected to shift away from the dominant biomass-based removals toward a more diversified supply:</p><ul><li><strong>Large-scale DACCS and BioCCS</strong> projects coming online</li><li><strong>Smaller <a href=/posts/what-is-enhanced-weathering/>ERW</a> and marine CDR</strong> projects entering the market</li><li>A general <strong>broadening of the supplier base</strong> beyond biochar&rsquo;s early dominance</li></ul><p>This matters because market concentration has been a vulnerability. When one pathway dominates, the whole market inherits its risks — reversal concerns, feedstock limits, whatever. Diversification makes the entire CDR credit market more resilient.</p><h2 id=what-it-means-for-buyers>What It Means for Buyers<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#what-it-means-for-buyers>#</a></h2><p>For corporate buyers building CDR portfolios, the message is clear: you have real options now. Two years ago, buying durable carbon removal meant Climeworks or bust. Today, the menu spans a half-dozen pathways with commercial-stage projects in each.</p><p>Pricing pressure should follow. More suppliers competing across more pathways means better economics for buyers, which should drive more procurement, which funds more scale. That&rsquo;s the flywheel the CDR market has been waiting for.</p><h2 id=the-fine-print>The Fine Print<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#the-fine-print>#</a></h2><p>&ldquo;Commercialization stage&rdquo; doesn&rsquo;t mean gigatonne scale. Most of these projects are still small by any industrial standard. And commercialization without bankable policy support remains fragile — as the $1.2B US DAC hub cancellation reminded everyone last week.</p><p>But the direction is undeniable. The CDR market is maturing, diversifying, and growing. Two-thirds commercial is a number that would have seemed wildly optimistic even 18 months ago.</p><p><strong>Source:</strong> <a href=https://carbonherald.com/climefi-publishes-2026-insights-on-the-durable-carbon-removal-market/>Carbon Herald — ClimeFi Publishes 2026 Insights On The Durable Carbon Removal Market</a></p><hr><h3 id=-related-reading>🔗 Related Reading<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#-related-reading>#</a></h3><ul><li><a href=/posts/biochar-quiet-giant-of-cdr/>Biochar: The Quiet Giant of Carbon Removal</a></li><li><a href=/posts/mercedes-f1-cdr-portfolio/>Mercedes F1 Goes All-In on Carbon Removal — 7 New Projects Across 6 Pathways</a></li><li><a href=/posts/eu-first-cdr-certification-standard/>EU Launches World&rsquo;s First Voluntary Standard for Permanent Carbon Removals</a></li><li><a href=/posts/germany-98m-cdr-budget/>Germany Puts €98 Million Into CDR — Its First Dedicated Federal Budget Line Ever</a></li></ul>
