Methane removal isn't just "CDR but for CH4." Tanaka et al. argue it's a genuinely distinct third class of climate intervention. The kicker: unlike CO2 removal, AMR's cooling effect isn't durable. Stop removing methane and the warming benefit fades fast because CH4's atmospheric lifetime is only ~12 years. But it's less dangerous to stop than SRM, which snaps back hard. Interesting wrinkle: AMR also cuts tropospheric ozone, with effects that vary depending on local pollution levels.
