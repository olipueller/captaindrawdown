Most energy planning models treat negative emissions as an afterthought. DECO2, an open-source tool from Nair, Tan, Foo et al., actually builds CCS and NETs into bottom-up energy planning at the individual plant scale. That distinction matters: you can't hit net-zero by 2050 if your planning software pretends carbon removal doesn't exist. Open-source means anyone can audit and extend it.
