**Carbon Herald just published [1PointFive Sells Over 15k Tonnes Of CDR Credits Through Channel Partners](https://carbonherald.com/1pointfive-sells-over-15k-tonnes-of-cdr-credits-through-channel-partners/).**

Carbon Herald reports that 1PointFive, the direct air capture subsidiary of Occidental, has to date agreed to sell over 15,000 tonnes of carbon dioxide removal credits via channel partners rather than through direct corporate deals. The channel partner model routes credits through intermediaries such as sustainability platforms and brokers that aggregate smaller buyers who would not negotiate a bilateral offtake. The credits are tied to removals expected from the company's Stratos facility in Ector County, Texas, which is designed to capture up to 500,000 tonnes per year once fully operational. The figure sits alongside 1PointFive's larger direct offtakes with buyers including Microsoft, Amazon and Airbus, and signals an effort to widen the buyer base beyond a handful of large anchor customers.

_Our take (Context):_ The headline number is a cumulative sales total, not delivered tonnes, so it says more about demand aggregation than about removals achieved. The piece leaves out pricing, which partners are involved, delivery vintages and how these volumes relate to Stratos ramp-up timing. Without those details it is hard to judge how material 15,000 tonnes is against the plant's nameplate capacity.
