Most MOF thermal property calculations are probably wrong. Kamath and Persson show that spurious imaginary phonon modes in DFT calculations corrupt heat capacity estimates for Metal-organic Frameworks, the very numbers we use to figure out energy penalties in temperature swing adsorption for direct air capture. If your MOF screening pipeline doesn't account for this, your DAC energy cost projections are built on sand.
