**Carbon Herald just published [Uniper Picks SLB Capturi For 1.38 GW Carbon Capture Power Project In UK](https://carbonherald.com/uniper-slb-capturi-carbon-capture-power-project-uk/).**

Carbon Herald reports that Uniper has chosen SLB Capturi as the technology provider for carbon capture at a 1.38 GW power project planned in Deeside, United Kingdom. SLB Capturi will supply its amine-based capture system for the site. The project would be one of the larger gas-with-capture installations in the UK if built out, aligning with the country's broader industrial decarbonization and CCUS cluster plans. Details on final investment decision timing, storage arrangements, and capture rate targets are covered in the original article.

_Our take (Heads-up):_ Amine capture at gigawatt scale in the UK is a serious commitment, but the numbers that matter - capture rate, energy penalty, cost per tonne, and CO2 offtake route - are what will decide whether this holds up. Vendor selection is an early milestone, not a final investment decision, so treat the 1.38 GW figure as project scope rather than confirmed capacity delivered.
