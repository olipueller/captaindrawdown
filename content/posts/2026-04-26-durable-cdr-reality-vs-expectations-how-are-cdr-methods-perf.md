Biochar quietly beat the hype. The flashy moonshots did not.
---
Back in 2021, forecasts had direct air capture and bioenergy with carbon capture (BECCS) leading delivery by now. Reality: biochar and other biomass-based removal (BiCRS) methods have done the actual tonnage. DAC and BECCS hit higher costs and slower scaling than projected.
---
The interesting twist: the binding constraint is no longer cost or permanence. It is measurement, reporting, and verification. MRV is what gates trust, pricing, and procurement. Methods that can prove a tonne cheaply and credibly are pulling ahead of methods that cannot.
---
Caveat: outperforming a 2021 forecast is not the same as being at climate-relevant scale. Biochar delivers today but has supply ceilings. DAC and BECCS still matter for the long run. The portfolio thesis holds. The delivery curve just looks different than people drew it.
