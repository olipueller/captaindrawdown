First proper uncertainty analysis of direct air capture costs is here, and the range is brutal. Chiani et al. find that policy support assumptions alone can swing DACCS deployment projections wildly, yet most integrated assessment models treat these as fixed inputs. Translation: the modeled pathways policymakers rely on are building on sand. The paper uses global sensitivity analysis to show technological learning rates matter less than financial and policy drivers. Worth sitting with that.
