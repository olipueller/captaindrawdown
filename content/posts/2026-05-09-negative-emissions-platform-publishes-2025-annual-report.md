**NEP just published [Negative Emissions Platform publishes 2025 Annual Report](https://www.negative-emissions.org/updates/negative-emissions-platform-publishes-2025-annual-report).**

NEP's 2025 Annual Report tracks the trade body's activity across European policy and international convening over the past year. The report cites 41 policymaker meetings, 20 public consultation responses, six position papers and reports, and 18 new members joining across technology, project development, MRV, market infrastructure, buyers, and CO2 transport and storage. NEP ran Advocacy Days with 27 representatives from 21 companies and held 10 Invest in Carbon Removals events drawing 290 participants. Internationally, NEP acted as Committee Lead for the CDR30 Pavilion at COP30, coordinated with a coalition of more than 140 organisations across 12 days of programming.

_Our take (Context):_ The activity metrics are useful for tracking sector mobilisation, but meetings and consultation responses are inputs rather than outcomes. The harder question, whether any of this translated into firmer EU procurement, target architecture, or finance for permanent removals, is not answered by headcount. Worth reading alongside concrete policy text rather than as a standalone scorecard.
