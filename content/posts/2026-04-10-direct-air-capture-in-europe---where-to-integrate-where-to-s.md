Bernecker & Müsgens just dropped a model that actually separates DAC, CO2 transport, and storage across Europe's energy system instead of treating DACCS as one black box. The key finding everyone should internalize: electricity cost is the dominant variable driving the massive spread in DACCS cost estimates. Integrating DAC into long-term energy system planning changes where you build and what you pay. Most cost projections skip this entirely.
