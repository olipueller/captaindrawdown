**Carbon Herald just published [Nasdaq Launches New Carbon Issuance Calendar In Collaboration With Relae](https://carbonherald.com/nasdaq-launches-new-carbon-issuance-calendar-in-collaboration-with-relae/).**

Carbon Herald reports that Nasdaq, working with carbon market data firm Relae, has released the Nasdaq Carbon Issuance Calendar. The tool is described as a one-stop platform where buyers can see upcoming carbon credit issuances in one place rather than tracking announcements across registries and project developers. Nasdaq already has a footprint in carbon removal through its majority stake in the Puro.earth registry, and this calendar extends its push into market infrastructure and data services. The outlet frames the launch as a transparency and planning tool aimed at corporate buyers and intermediaries. Details on coverage, pricing for users, and which registries or credit types are included were not spelled out in the material we saw.

_Our take (Useful):_ A shared issuance calendar fills a real gap - buyers currently piece together supply timing from scattered sources. What matters is scope: whether it covers all major registries or leans toward Nasdaq's own ecosystem, how issuance dates are sourced and verified, and whether it is free or gated. Treat it as a useful signal of supply, not a quality screen.
