<p>Frontier — the advance market commitment (AMC) backed by Stripe, Alphabet, Shopify, Meta, and McKinsey Sustainability — has opened applications for its <a href=https://frontierclimate.com/apply/innovation>2026 Innovation program</a>.</p><p>The program sits within Frontier&rsquo;s broader commitment to purchase <strong>more than $1 billion</strong> worth of permanent carbon removal between 2022 and 2030.</p><h2 id=whats-on-offer>What&rsquo;s on Offer<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#whats-on-offer>#</a></h2><p>Two tracks:</p><ul><li><strong>Prepurchases</strong> ($250K–$1.5M): Upfront funding to help early-stage projects move from lab to field deployment</li><li><strong>R&amp;D grants</strong> ($250K–$750K): Support for research tackling critical technical challenges</li></ul><p>Applications are rolling, with selections expected twice during 2026.</p><h2 id=priority-areas>Priority Areas<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#priority-areas>#</a></h2><p>The 2026 program zeroes in on three bottlenecks that could limit CDR scalability:</p><ol><li><strong>Mineralization processes</strong> — improving the speed and efficiency of carbon mineralization</li><li><strong>Ocean and inland water alkalinity enhancement</strong> — advancing OAE beyond pilot stage</li><li><strong>MRV tools</strong> — building stronger monitoring, reporting, and verification for open-system pathways</li></ol><p>Frontier is also open to novel approaches that could outperform existing technologies.</p><h2 id=why-it-matters>Why It Matters<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#why-it-matters>#</a></h2><p>In 2025, Frontier portfolio companies began constructing projects capable of removing approximately <strong>1.4 million tonnes of CO₂ annually</strong> — a sharp increase from 2024 capacity.</p><p>But significant technical hurdles remain around cost, measurement, and large-scale deployment. The innovation program specifically targets the early-stage experimentation and field trials needed to bridge those gaps.</p><p>The focus on OAE and open-system MRV is notable. These are pathways with enormous theoretical potential but limited real-world data — exactly the kind of risky, high-reward work that AMCs were designed to fund.</p><p>For CDR startups working on mineralization, ocean carbon, or measurement technology: <a href=https://frontierclimate.com/apply/innovation>applications are open</a>.</p><p><strong>Source:</strong> <a href=https://carbonherald.com/frontier-launches-call-for-2026-carbon-removal-innovation-program/>Carbon Herald</a> · <a href=https://frontierclimate.com/apply/innovation>Frontier</a></p><hr><hr><h3 id=-related-reading>🔗 Related Reading<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#-related-reading>#</a></h3><ul><li><a href=/posts/biochar-quiet-giant-of-cdr/>Biochar: The Quiet Giant of Carbon Removal</a></li><li><a href=/posts/erw-promising-but-not-simple/>Enhanced Rock Weathering: Promising, But Not Simple</a></li><li><a href=/posts/microsoft-93-percent-cdr-credits/>Microsoft Bought 93% of All Carbon Removal Credits in 2025</a></li><li><a href=/posts/erw-uncertainties-nature-paper/>ERW Under Fire: What the Nature Paper on Uncertainties Actually Says</a></li></ul>
