<p>If you&rsquo;ve published anything related to carbon dioxide removal, you&rsquo;re probably in here.</p><p>The <a href=/cdr-researcher-census/lookup/>CDR Researcher Lookup</a> lets you search 122,674 researchers by name. Each profile shows:</p><ul><li><strong>Your CDR pathway</strong> — which removal method your work maps to</li><li><strong>Commitment level</strong> — dabbler, part-time, focused, or dedicated</li><li><strong>Trajectory</strong> — is your CDR output growing, stable, or declining?</li><li><strong>Career stage</strong> — early career through eminent, based on h-index and career span</li><li><strong>Institution and country</strong> — verified via ORCID where available</li><li><strong>Your CDR papers</strong> — the specific publications that put you in this dataset</li></ul><h2 id=why-this-exists>Why This Exists<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#why-this-exists>#</a></h2><p>When <a href=https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-56166-9>Lück et al. (2025)</a> mapped 53,000 CDR papers in <em>Nature Communications</em>, they answered &ldquo;how much CDR research exists?&rdquo; I wanted to answer the next question: <strong>who are the people doing it?</strong></p><p>The result is the first researcher-centric census of the CDR field. Now in v2: 24,749 CDR papers, 122,674 authors, 80,382 with meaningful CDR work. Not just paper counts — actual profiles of every author, their career trajectories, and how deeply they&rsquo;re engaged with carbon removal.</p><h2 id=whats-new-in-v2>What&rsquo;s New in v2<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#whats-new-in-v2>#</a></h2><p>v2 fixes the misclassification mess. Every paper got re-classified individually by LLM (title + abstract), so we yanked 12,384 false positives that snuck through v1. Result: stronger signal, fewer &ldquo;is this really CDR?&rdquo; false alarms.</p><p>Real improvements:</p><ul><li>LLM-per-paper classification (not just keyword soup)</li><li>12,384 non-CDR papers removed</li><li>Cleaner researcher profiles</li><li>Better pathway accuracy</li></ul><h2 id=its-v2--help-me-fix-it-its-better>It&rsquo;s v2 — Help Me Fix It (It&rsquo;s Better)<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#its-v2--help-me-fix-it-its-better>#</a></h2><p>This data comes from <a href=https://openalex.org/>OpenAlex</a> and <a href=https://orcid.org/>ORCID</a>. It&rsquo;s still imperfect:</p><ul><li>Some researchers may be misclassified by pathway</li><li>Institution data is wrong for ~17% of authors without ORCID</li><li>Name disambiguation is hard (especially for common names)</li><li>2025-2026 papers are still being indexed</li></ul><p><strong>If your profile is wrong, tell me.</strong> Every correction improves the dataset for everyone. The census updates monthly.</p><p>📣 Reach out: <a href=https://bsky.app/profile/captaindrawdown.bsky.social>Bluesky</a> · <a href=https://x.com/CaptainDrawdown>X</a></p><p>👉 <strong><a href=/cdr-researcher-census/lookup/>Search the CDR Researcher Census →</a></strong></p><hr><p><em>Built by <a href=https://www.captaindrawdown.com/about/>CaptainDrawdown</a> · Data from <a href=https://openalex.org/>OpenAlex</a> (CC0) + <a href=https://orcid.org/>ORCID</a> · Updated monthly</em></p><hr><h3 id=-related-reading>🔗 Related Reading<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#-related-reading>#</a></h3><ul><li><a href=/posts/cdr-researcher-census/>I Counted Every CDR Researcher on Earth.
