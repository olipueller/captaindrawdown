DAC's biggest cost problem is thermal energy for sorbent regeneration. Fan & Xu's new paper models solar-thermal DAC systems that use concentrated solar heat directly, bypassing the electricity-to-heat conversion losses that plague current designs. The approach could reshape where DAC gets built, favoring high-DNI regions over cheap-grid regions. With 159 DAC companies in our tracker, the ones paying attention to solar-thermal integration may have a real edge.
