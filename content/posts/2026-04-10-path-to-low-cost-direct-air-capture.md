Global Thermostat's Peter Eisenberger and Matthew Realff published a paper laying out how DAC could hit costs low enough to justify Manhattan Project-scale deployment. The key argument: we already accept gigatonne-scale CDR is necessary, but cost uncertainty is paralyzing policy. GT claims they have a path forward. Whether you buy their numbers or not, the framing is right. Delay is itself a choice with consequences.
