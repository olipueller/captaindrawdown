**Carbon Herald just published [Offshore Heavyweight Castorone Arrives In North Sea To Lay Crucial CO2 Pipeline](https://carbonherald.com/offshore-heavyweight-castorone-arrives-in-north-sea-to-lay-crucial-co2-pipeline/).**

Carbon Herald reports that the Northern Endurance Partnership has started heavy offshore work on a CO2 transport pipeline in the UK North Sea, a key piece of infrastructure for Britain's industrial decarbonization plans. The arrival of Saipem's Castorone, one of the largest pipelay vessels in operation, marks the start of the subsea installation phase. The pipeline is intended to move captured CO2 from Teesside emitters to permanent offshore storage as part of the East Coast Cluster. The project is a central element in the UK government's CCS deployment strategy and its funding commitments to the sector.

_Our take (Useful):_ Getting steel in the water is a real milestone for UK CCS, which has faced years of delays and cost debates. The open questions remain on the capture side: whether the connected industrial emitters deliver expected volumes, and whether unit costs land where FID assumed. Worth tracking pipeline commissioning dates against the cluster's stated 2027-2028 start.
