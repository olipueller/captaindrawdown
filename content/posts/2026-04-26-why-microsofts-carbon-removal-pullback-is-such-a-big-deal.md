**Heatmap News just published [Why Microsoft’s Carbon Removal Pullback Is Such a Big Deal](https://heatmap.news/podcast/shift-key-s3-e43-microsoft-carbon-removal).**

Heatmap reports Microsoft is pausing new carbon removal purchases after having bought 72 million tons, over 40 times more than any other buyer. A podcast with Columbia's Jack Andreasen Cavanaugh discusses what the pullback means for CDR suppliers and why federal policy may be needed to sustain demand.

_Our take (Heads-up):_ Worth a listen if you track CDR market structure - Microsoft's dominance has always been the elephant in the room, and this conversation takes the buyer-concentration risk seriously.
