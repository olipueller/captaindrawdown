And 36% of China&rsquo;s CDR researchers are <em>actively growing</em> their output, the highest rate among major countries.</p><p>The total pool: <strong>80,382 researchers</strong> across all countries, publishing in <strong>24,749 papers</strong> classified as CDR-relevant using LLM semantic analysis.</p><h2 id=how-this-compares-to-lück-et-al>How This Compares to Lück et al.<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#how-this-compares-to-lück-et-al>#</a></h2><p>Lück et al. tracked first-author countries for 13,366 papers. Their data shows China at 23% and the US at 19%. My data shows China at 32% and the US at 11%.</p><p>Why the difference? Lück counts <em>papers</em> (first-author country), I count <em>people</em>. Chinese research teams tend to be larger — more co-authors per paper. When you count people instead of papers, China&rsquo;s share increases significantly. Our <a href=/directory/>CDR Directory</a> tracks 820 CDR companies. The geographic mismatch between researchers and companies tells a story:</p><p><strong>China:</strong> 32% of researchers, but a much smaller share of CDR startups in Western databases. Chinese CDR companies may be underrepresented in English-language directories — or the science-to-startup pipeline may work differently there.</p><p><strong>United States:</strong> 11% of researchers, but the largest concentration of DAC companies (Climeworks US operations, Heirloom, CarbonCapture Inc., etc.). The US converts a smaller research base into more commercial activity — or attracts companies regardless of where the science originates.</p><p><strong>India:</strong> 7.3% of researchers (5,881 people) and growing at 30%. But relatively few CDR companies. This is a massive untapped talent pool.</p><h2 id=the-fastest-growing-cdr-research-countries>The Fastest-Growing CDR Research Countries<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#the-fastest-growing-cdr-research-countries>#</a></h2><p>Countries where the highest percentage of CDR researchers have a &ldquo;growing&rdquo; trajectory (increasing CDR output):</p><table><thead><tr><th>Country</th><th>Total</th><th>Growing</th><th>Growth Rate</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>🇷🇴 Romania</td><td>316</td><td>133</td><td>42.1%</td></tr><tr><td>🇭🇰 Hong Kong</td><td>471</td><td>181</td><td>38.4%</td></tr><tr><td>🇲🇦 Morocco</td><td>283</td><td>108</td><td>38.2%</td></tr><tr><td>🇸🇬 Singapore</td><td>421</td><td>154</td><td>36.6%</td></tr><tr><td>🇨🇳 China</td><td>26,081</td><td>9,471</td><td>36.3%</td></tr><tr><td>🇬🇷 Greece</td><td>386</td><td>129</td><td>33.4%</td></tr><tr><td>🇰🇷 South Korea</td><td>1,602</td><td>524</td><td>32.7%</td></tr><tr><td>🇪🇬 Egypt</td><td>574</td><td>187</td><td>32.6%</td></tr></tbody></table><p>The Global South is entering CDR research rapidly.
