**Carbon Herald just published [Global CCS Institute Maps Latest Carbon Capture Technologies As CCS Sector Expands](https://carbonherald.com/global-ccs-institute-maps-latest-carbon-capture-technologies-as-ccs-sector-expands/).**

Carbon Herald reports that the Global CCS Institute has released an updated survey of carbon capture technologies, cataloguing both established approaches and emerging capture methods entering the market. The mapping arrives as CCS project pipelines expand globally and as developers and technology providers push newer capture routes toward commercial deployment. According to the report, the exercise is meant to give policymakers, investors, and project developers a clearer picture of where different capture technologies sit on the maturity curve. The piece frames the release against a broader trend of growing interest in CCS as a decarbonization tool for hard-to-abate sectors.

_Our take (Context):_ Technology mapping exercises from the Global CCS Institute are useful reference points, but readers should remember the Institute is an advocacy body for the sector, so maturity claims deserve independent scrutiny. The key questions - cost per tonne, capture rates achieved in practice, and deployment timelines - are where these surveys tend to be thinnest. Worth reading with that lens.
