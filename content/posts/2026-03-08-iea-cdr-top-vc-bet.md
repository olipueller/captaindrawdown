<p>The International Energy Agency just dropped a data point that the carbon removal industry has been waiting for: CDR is officially one of the seven emerging energy sectors that have offset the decline in EV venture funding since 2021.</p><p>In its <a href=https://carbonherald.com/iea-carbon-management-technologies-gain-funding-and-policy-support/>State of Energy Innovation 2026</a> report, the IEA maps the full innovation pipeline — from early research to commercial-scale demonstration — and carbon removal sits alongside nuclear, next-gen geothermal, and critical minerals as the new magnets for venture capital.</p><h2 id=what-the-numbers-say>What the Numbers Say<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#what-the-numbers-say>#</a></h2><p>The shift is structural, not anecdotal. As electric vehicle VC funding cooled from its 2021 peak, seven sectors absorbed the redirected capital. CDR is explicitly named among them — and unlike some of the others, it&rsquo;s still in its early commercial phase, meaning the growth curve ahead is steeper than what we&rsquo;ve already seen.</p><p>The report also flags the &ldquo;missing middle&rdquo; problem: CDR projects are too expensive for venture capital alone but too risky for traditional project finance. Denmark&rsquo;s $4.2 billion CCS fund, offering 15-year contracts for capture, transport, and storage, is cited as one model for bridging that gap — though its <a href=https://carbonherald.com/denmarks-ccs-tender-failure-or-reality-check/>recent tender produced just two bids</a> from an initial field of ten.</p><h2 id=why-this-matters>Why This Matters<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#why-this-matters>#</a></h2><p>The IEA isn&rsquo;t a hype machine. When it categorizes CDR alongside nuclear and geothermal as a defining VC trend, that&rsquo;s a signal to institutional capital, not just climate tech enthusiasts. It also reinforces what <a href=https://www.carbon-drawdown.de/blog/2026-2-24-cdr-must-shift-from-speed-amp-scale-to-prove-amp-learn>CDI has argued</a>: the industry needs to prove before it scales.</p><p>For investors still treating carbon removal as a niche bet: the IEA just told you it&rsquo;s a structural shift. For CDR companies struggling to close their Series B: this report is the kind of institutional validation that moves LP conversations.</p><p>The real question isn&rsquo;t whether CDR will attract capital. It&rsquo;s whether the industry can build the project finance structures to deploy it fast enough.</p><hr><p><em>Source: <a href=https://carbonherald.com/iea-carbon-management-technologies-gain-funding-and-policy-support/>Carbon Herald — IEA: Carbon Management Technologies Gain Funding and Policy Support</a> · <a href=https://iea.blob.core.windows.net/assets/d24ccc77-ef68-491c-848d-b9c0ec0c484b/TheStateofEnergyInnovation2026.pdf>IEA State of Energy Innovation 2026 (PDF)</a></em></p><hr><h3 id=-related-reading>🔗 Related Reading<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#-related-reading>#</a></h3><ul><li><a href=/posts/us-dac-hub-funding-cancelled/>$1.2 Billion in US DAC Hub Funding: Cancelled.
