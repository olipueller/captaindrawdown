**Heatmap News just published [Transcript: Why Microsoft’s Carbon Removal Pullback Is Such a Big Deal](https://heatmap.news/podcast/shift-key-s3-e43-microsoft-carbon-removal-transcript).**

Heatmap News published a transcript of its Shift Key podcast examining Microsoft's decision to pause carbon dioxide removal purchases, news that Heatmap first reported and that Bloomberg and Carbon Herald have since confirmed. Host Robinson Meyer notes Microsoft has bought more than 70 million tons of CDR since 2020, roughly 40 times more than any other buyer, making it the dominant customer for the sector. Microsoft's chief sustainability officer says the program has not ended and that the company is adjusting pace and volume rather than abandoning its goals. Meyer is joined by Jack Andreasen Cavanaugh of Columbia's Center on Global Energy Policy to discuss what the pullback means for the industry's path forward.

_Our take (Heads-up):_ The reporting lines up across multiple outlets, and the scale figure on Microsoft's purchases is the key data point worth tracking. What is less clear is whether this is a true demand shock or a procurement reset, since Microsoft's own statement leaves wide room for interpretation. The transcript format is useful for context but light on independent numbers beyond Microsoft itself.
