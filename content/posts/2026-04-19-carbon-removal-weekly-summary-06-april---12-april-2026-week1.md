Microsoft has reportedly paused all new carbon removal purchases. No timeline for resuming. Existing contracts stand, but the buyer that has underwritten most of this market just stopped writing new checks.
---
Microsoft has been the dominant offtaker across DAC, BECCS (bioenergy with carbon capture), ERW (enhanced rock weathering), and biochar. Multi-million ton deals with Stockholm Exergi, Chestnut, Terradot, and others built much of the current pipeline.
---
One buyer should never be a market. When a single company can freeze demand for an entire sector by pausing procurement, that is a structural weakness, not a setback. CDR needs compliance demand and more corporate buyers, fast. Remember: removal is for residual emissions, not
---
Caveat: existing contracts continue delivering, and a pause is not a cancellation. But suppliers raising capital this quarter just lost their anchor reference. Watch what happens to project financing and final investment decisions over the next two quarters.
