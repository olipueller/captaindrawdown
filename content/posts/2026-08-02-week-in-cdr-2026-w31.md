_Captain Drawdown's weekly Sunday selection. Each link carries our 1-2 sentence take so you don't have to click everything to know what's there._

The week's signal is less about deals closing and more about the rulebooks tightening around them: SBTi's revamped standard is reshaping how biochar gets counted, a new enhanced rock weathering (ERW) paper puts numbers on which feedstocks carry real metal-accumulation risk, and Sylvera is opening forest carbon data at a moment when buyers are demanding auditable measurement, reporting and verification (MRV) inputs. The UN's Article 6.4 body, meanwhile, let grid-connected renewables into the compliance channel.

## Methodology and standards
- **Carbon Herald**: [Opinion: How The Revamped SBTi Standard Reinforces The Dual Purpose Of Biochar](https://carbonherald.com/opinion-how-the-revamped-sbti-standard-reinforces-the-dual-purpose-of-biochar-in-companies-supply-chains/). The Corporate Net-Zero Standard v2.0 (published June) adds an Ongoing Emissions Responsibility framework and explicitly counts "landscape or other agricultural interventions within a supply shed" toward Scope 3, which is why food, beverage and fashion buyers are now asking about insetting biochar rather than buying offsets.
- **CDRXIV**: [Metal Accumulation Risks in Enhanced Rock Weathering: Risk Assessment Framework and Recommendations](https://cdrxiv.org/preprint/546). Olagaray, Lambert and Thorlakson built the ERW Metal Accumulation Calculator, a conservative mass-balance model covering 16 metals: under basalt deployment, modelled soil concentrations stay below most regulatory thresholds (though 8 metals rise above baseline), while under peridotite, nickel and chromium often exceed the median threshold. The useful move is expressing metal load per tonne of potential CDR, which varies by roughly an order of magnitude across metals and feedstocks.
- **Rainbow Standard (LinkedIn)**: [Bio-oil in asphalt module finalised under BiCRS](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:share:7488866058926268416/). Independent crediting for bio-bitumen unlocks the pyrolysis co-product economics that biochar producers have struggled to monetise; worth watching whether other registries follow with parallel modules.
- **Carbon Herald**: [Renewable Energy Methodology Cleared In Major Expansion Of UN Carbon Market](https://carbonherald.com/renewable-energy-methodology-cleared-in-major-expansion-of-un-carbon-market/). The Article 6.4 Supervisory Body approved a grid-connected renewable electricity methodology on July 30, adding it to a PACM roster that so far held only landfill-gas methane and nitric-acid N₂O. If avoidance credits scale in the compliance channel, the price premium for removals has to be defended on integrity rather than scarcity.

## Data and infrastructure
- **Carbon Herald**: [Sylvera Launches Open Carbon Data Project Backed By Rockefeller And Meta](https://carbonherald.com/sylvera-launches-first-of-its-kind-open-carbon-data-project-backed-by-the-rockefeller-foundation-and-meta/). A ratings agency giving away benchmark-grade forest carbon data for the Brazilian Atlantic Forest, on $900k from The Rockefeller Foundation and Meta with the State University of Santa Cruz, WRI and the Symbiosis Coalition, is a notable business-model bet: Sylvera appears to be wagering that the moat is in analytics and buyer relationships, not the underlying maps.

## New pathways in the lab
- **Berkeley Lab**: [Pulling Graphite Out of Thin Air](https://newscenter.lbl.gov/2026/07/30/pulling-graphite-out-of-thin-air/). Berkeley Lab, UC Berkeley and Estonia's National Institute of Chemical Physics and Biophysics watched molten-salt electrochemistry convert captured carbon into graphite (published in Nature Communications), with battery-grade material as the stated end goal rather than a result achieved. Since nearly all US graphite is currently imported, a tunable synthesis route would give captured CO₂ a co-product with an actual industrial buyer.
- **Carbon Drawdown Initiative (LinkedIn)**: [CREW Carbon and wastewater alkalinity enhancement](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:share:7488578005435314177/). Wastewater alkalinity enhancement piggybacks on existing municipal infrastructure and enclosed, monitored reactors, sidestepping the ocean-MRV problem that has slowed marine alkalinity enhancement; the constraint is per-plant tonnage, not measurement.

## Policy and framing
- **Dr Tom Harris**: [The 3 levers that could stabilise the climate](https://drtomharris.substack.com/p/the-3-levers-that-could-stabilise). A clear framing of how mitigation, CDR and solar radiation management (SRM) interact on the Earth energy imbalance, useful for practitioners who need to explain to non-technical audiences why CDR alone doesn't cool the planet on a meaningful timescale.

The dominant signal this week is methodology infrastructure catching up with pathway proliferation: SBTi, BiCRS, ERW risk frameworks, Article 6.4 and open forest data all landed in the same seven days. Conspicuously absent: any large purchase announcements. The hyperscaler buying cadence that defined Q1 and Q2 has visibly slowed, and the industry appears to be using the quiet to firm up what it's actually selling.
