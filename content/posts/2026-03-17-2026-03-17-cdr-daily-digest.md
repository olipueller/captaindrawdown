<p>A blockbuster Tuesday for carbon removal. The private sector doubled down, two governments put real money on the table, infrastructure got creative with biochar, and the first global rulebook for CDR credits started taking shape. Five stories, one throughline: the demand signal for permanent carbon removal is getting louder from every direction.</p><hr><h2 id=today-on-captaindrawdown>Today on CaptainDrawdown<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#today-on-captaindrawdown>#</a></h2><h3 id=-big-tech-carbon-credit-purchases-explode--up-181-in-one-year>💰 Big Tech Carbon Credit Purchases Explode — Up 181% in One Year<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#-big-tech-carbon-credit-purchases-explode--up-181-in-one-year>#</a></h3><p>The numbers are staggering. Big Tech purchased 68.4 million permanent carbon removal credits in 2025, up 181% from the previous year. Microsoft leads the pack, but Amazon, Google, and Meta are all scaling their commitments fast.</p><p>The driver? AI infrastructure. Data centers are eating electricity at a pace that makes corporate net-zero targets mathematically impossible without carbon removal. As one CEO put it: the AI buildout has made CDR not optional, but existential for corporate climate pledges.</p><p>This isn&rsquo;t philanthropy — it&rsquo;s procurement. And that distinction matters enormously for the CDR industry&rsquo;s path to commercial scale.</p><p><strong><a href=/posts/big-tech-carbon-credits-explode-ai-race/>Read our full coverage →</a></strong></p><hr><h3 id=-canada-becomes-first-national-government-to-buy-cdr-credits>🇨🇦 Canada Becomes First National Government to Buy CDR Credits<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#-canada-becomes-first-national-government-to-buy-cdr-credits>#</a></h3><p>History made in Ottawa. Canada launched the first dedicated national government procurement program for carbon dioxide removal credits — $10 million CAD allocated for federal departments to purchase credits from Canadian DAC, BECCS, biochar, and enhanced mineralization projects.</p><p>This is exactly the demand signal the industry has been waiting for from governments. Corporate buyers have led CDR procurement so far, but sovereign demand adds a different kind of credibility and scale potential. Other governments are watching closely.</p><p><strong><a href=/posts/canada-first-government-cdr-procurement/>Read our analysis →</a></strong></p><hr><h3 id=-sweden-commits-34m-to-negative-emissions-under-industriklivet>🇸🇪 Sweden Commits $34M to Negative Emissions Under Industriklivet<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#-sweden-commits-34m-to-negative-emissions-under-industriklivet>#</a></h3><p>The Nordics continue to quietly lead on CDR policy. Sweden announced two new funding calls under its Industriklivet program: $32 million (SEK 300M) for scaling and commercializing negative emissions technologies, plus $1.6 million (SEK 15M) for research. Bio-CCS, DAC, and geological storage are all eligible across the full value chain. Applications close April 29.</p><p>While the US political debate stalls on whether climate change is real, Sweden is funding the scale-up of solutions. The contrast speaks for itself.</p><p><strong><a href=/posts/sweden-negative-emissions-funding-industriklivet/>Read our breakdown →</a></strong></p><hr><h3 id=-roads-as-carbon-sinks-verde-locks-in-major-biochar-supply>🛣️ Roads as Carbon Sinks: Verde Locks In Major Biochar Supply<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#-roads-as-carbon-sinks-verde-locks-in-major-biochar-supply>#</a></h3><p>What if the roads you drive on were actively storing carbon? Verde just secured 38,500 tons per year of engineered biochar supply for its BioAsphalt™ technology — asphalt that incorporates biochar to sequester CO₂. They&rsquo;ve already earned the world&rsquo;s first carbon removal credits from an asphalt application, verified by Puro.earth.</p><p>Infrastructure-based CDR is quietly becoming real. When carbon removal piggybacks on things we&rsquo;re building anyway — roads, buildings, concrete — the economics start looking very different from standalone capture.</p><p><strong><a href=/posts/verde-bioasphalt-biochar-roads-carbon-sinks/>Read our coverage →</a></strong></p><hr><h3 id=-the-first-global-rules-for-cdr-credits-are-being-written-right-now>📜 The First Global Rules for CDR Credits Are Being Written Right Now<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#-the-first-global-rules-for-cdr-credits-are-being-written-right-now>#</a></h3><p>Perhaps the most consequential story of the day, even if it lacks the flashy numbers. The first global rulebook for carbon removal credits is being drafted, driven by the IPCC&rsquo;s finding that large-scale CDR is necessary for every pathway to net-zero by 2050.</p><p>The stakes are enormous: how we define, verify, price, and trade permanent carbon removal will shape the entire market for decades. Get the rules right, and CDR scales with integrity.
