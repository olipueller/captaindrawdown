**Heatmap News just published [Transcript: Anthropic and the Future of the Buzzy Carbon Removal Buyer’s Club](https://heatmap.news/podcast/shift-key-s3-e54-frontier-transcript).**

Heatmap News published a transcript of its Shift Key podcast with Frontier head Hannah Bebbington Valori. Frontier, the Advanced Market Commitment coalition that includes Stripe, Google, McKinsey, H&M, JPMorgan Chase, and Salesforce, announced a new round of more than $900 million, roughly doubling its available capital for carbon removal purchases. The coalition is branding this a growth AMC and says it will concentrate the next phase of buying on a small set of companies it believes can scale to billions of tons of removal. Anthropic is joining Frontier as a new member. The episode also addresses Microsoft pausing its purchases after buying about 70 million tons historically, and what that shift means for demand.

_Our take (Context):_ The growth AMC framing is worth watching since concentrating spend on a few suppliers raises the stakes on technology selection and could pull capital away from earlier-stage approaches. Anthropic's entry is notable given how little public climate disclosure AI labs have offered so far. The Microsoft pause is the bigger structural story behind this announcement.
