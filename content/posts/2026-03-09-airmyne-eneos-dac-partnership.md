<p><strong>ENEOS Holdings</strong> — parent company of Japan&rsquo;s largest energy firm — has made a strategic investment in <strong>AirMyne</strong>, a Berkeley, California-based direct air capture startup. And they&rsquo;re not just writing a check: the two companies have begun a joint technical evaluation with plans to explore industrial integration and project development in the US, Japan, and globally.</p><h2 id=the-technology>The Technology<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#the-technology>#</a></h2><p>AirMyne is developing a <strong>liquid solvent-based DAC system</strong> designed around three priorities: low energy consumption, supply-chain security, and operational simplicity. That last one matters more than it sounds — many first-generation DAC systems are engineering-intensive to operate, which drives up costs and limits where they can be deployed.</p><p>The company plans to break ground on a <strong>commercial pilot and demonstration plant next year</strong>, with funding support from the California Energy Commission.</p><h2 id=the-trend-japanese-energy-giants-go-dac>The Trend: Japanese Energy Giants Go DAC<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#the-trend-japanese-energy-giants-go-dac>#</a></h2><p>This isn&rsquo;t an isolated move. Japanese energy and chemical companies are systematically building positions in DAC:</p><ul><li><strong>Sumitomo Corporation</strong> invested in Global Thermostat</li><li><strong>Mitsui & Co.</strong> backed Heirloom Carbon Technologies</li><li><strong>ENEOS</strong> itself previously partnered with Sumitomo and 44.01 on carbon mineralization</li></ul><p>Japan&rsquo;s energy transition strategy clearly treats DAC as strategic infrastructure, not speculative cleantech. When the parent company of your country&rsquo;s biggest energy firm invests, that&rsquo;s an industrial signal.</p><p>AirMyne also recently brought on <strong>Jan Huckfeldt</strong>, former Chief Commercial Officer at Climeworks, as interim CCO — a hire that suggests commercial scaling is the next priority, not just R&amp;D.</p><p><strong>Source:</strong> <a href=https://carbonherald.com/airmyne-eneos-direct-air-capture/>Carbon Herald</a></p><hr><h3 id=-related-reading>🔗 Related Reading<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#-related-reading>#</a></h3><ul><li><a href=/posts/us-dac-hub-funding-cancelled/>$1.2 Billion in US DAC Hub Funding: Cancelled.
