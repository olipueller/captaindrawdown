**Carbon Herald just published [Isometric Targets Low-Carbon Materials With EAC Launch](https://carbonherald.com/isometric-targets-low-carbon-materials-with-eac-launch/).**

Carbon Herald reports that Isometric, known for certifying engineered carbon removal, is expanding into Environmental Attribute Certificates (EACs) for low-carbon materials. The move positions the registry as a certifier in a market segment that includes lower-emission cement, steel, and other industrial inputs where buyers want verified environmental claims tied to specific units of production. Isometric is framing itself as a first mover in applying its measurement-led approach to this category. The launch broadens the registry's scope from removals into avoided or reduced emissions associated with material production, and aims to give corporate buyers a tracked instrument for procurement claims.

_Our take (Heads-up):_ EACs for materials are useful only if the underlying baselines and chain-of-custody rules are tight, and that is where these schemes usually struggle. Worth watching how Isometric defines additionality and prevents double counting against existing product-level EPDs and Scope 3 accounting. The first-mover claim matters less than whether buyers and standards bodies accept the methodology.
