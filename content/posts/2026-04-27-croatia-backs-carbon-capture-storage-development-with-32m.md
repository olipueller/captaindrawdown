**Carbon Herald just published [Croatia Backs Carbon Capture & Storage Development With $3.2M](https://carbonherald.com/croatia-backs-carbon-capture-storage-development-with-3-2m/).**

Carbon Herald reports that Croatia has allocated about 2.7 million euros, equivalent to roughly 3.2 million US dollars, to support carbon capture and storage activities in the country. The funding is intended to back project development in the CCS space, signaling Croatia's intent to participate in the broader European push toward industrial decarbonization through capture technologies. The piece frames the move as part of a wider regional effort, where smaller EU member states are beginning to direct dedicated public budgets toward CCS rather than relying solely on EU-level mechanisms.

_Our take (Heads-up):_ The figure is modest by CCS standards, where individual project costs typically run into the hundreds of millions, so this looks more like a seeding allocation than a buildout commitment. Worth watching what specific projects or studies the funds attach to, and whether storage geology or capture-side work is prioritized.
