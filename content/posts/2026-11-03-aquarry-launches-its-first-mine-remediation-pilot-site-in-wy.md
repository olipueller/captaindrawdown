**Carbon Herald just published [Aquarry Launches Its First Mine Remediation Pilot Site In Wyoming](https://carbonherald.com/aquarry-launches-its-first-mine-remediation-pilot-site-in-wyoming/).**

Carbon Herald reports that Aquarry, a Colorado-based deep tech climate startup, has launched its first mine remediation pilot in Wyoming. The company is developing a technology aimed at treating legacy mine sites, and this pilot marks the move from lab and bench work to a real field location. Aquarry has positioned its approach at the intersection of mine cleanup and carbon management, arguing that remediation of mine water and waste can be paired with durable carbon storage. The announcement frames the Wyoming site as a proving ground for operational performance ahead of any larger deployment. Carbon Herald does not provide detailed figures on site scale, tonnage, or timeline in the summary available to us.

_Our take (Heads-up):_ A field pilot is a meaningful step for any startup, but the announcement is thin on specifics - no throughput, no CO2 figures, no monitoring plan, no partner or regulator named. Mine remediation plus carbon removal is a promising pairing on paper, yet verification of both the cleanup and the storage claim is the hard part. Treat this as a heads-up, not a result.
