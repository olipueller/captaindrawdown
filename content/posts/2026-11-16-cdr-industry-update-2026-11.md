# CDR Industry Update - November 2026

*November 2026 was a re-scoring month: no company entered or left the directory, but I changed my read on how alive 101 of them look.*

The directory holds at 1,470 companies, with 0 added and 0 removed, the same flat line as October. Underneath, 145 records changed, down from 183 last month. Most of that is liveliness: 101 records got a new tier, 95 of them with no other edit.

The direction is negative. I logged 59 downgrades against 42 upgrades, a net of -17. The largest single move is 32 companies going from Active to Likely Dead. Against that, 21 went the other way, from Likely Dead back to Active.

Now the caveat. A tier change is my own re-scoring of activity signals (website, LinkedIn, news), not something the company did. It is not a closure announcement. And when 21 companies return from Likely Dead in the same month that 32 fall into it, part of what I am measuring is noise in my own signals. Treat a Likely Dead tag as a prompt to check, not a verdict.

The second thread is cdr_focus, which I revised on 46 records, including Greengine, Acorn Bioenergy, SkyVac, Carbon Utility, Matterak and Global Algae. Six records changed both focus and tier, among them Taihei Dengyo Kaisha, Direct Carbon and Aramco/Siemens Energy. These are my classification calls, not company pivots. Zero additions means my discovery sweep added nothing this month, and it says nothing about company formation. So what for the field: a flat 1,470 hides uncertainty about who is still operating, so size the field by tier, not by row count.
