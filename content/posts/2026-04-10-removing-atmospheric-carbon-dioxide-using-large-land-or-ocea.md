Marston and Ibarra raise a point CDR boosters (myself included) need to take seriously: spreading rock dust or altering ocean chemistry at climate-relevant scale means changing the reflectivity of huge swaths of Earth's surface. That radiative forcing from albedo shifts could partially offset or amplify the CO2 removal benefit. ERW on croplands, mCDR across ocean surfaces, both change how sunlight gets absorbed. Net climate impact isn't just tons removed.
