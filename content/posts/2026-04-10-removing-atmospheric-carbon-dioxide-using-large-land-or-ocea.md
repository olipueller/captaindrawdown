Marston and Ibarra raise a point CDR folks need to take seriously: spreading crushed rock over huge land areas or altering ocean surface chemistry at scale will change Earth's albedo. The radiative forcing from reflectance changes could either help or hurt your net climate impact, depending on the approach. ERW on dark soils might add cooling; mCDR could go either way. If you're not accounting for albedo in your LCA, your carbon math is wrong.
