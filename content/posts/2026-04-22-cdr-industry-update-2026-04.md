# CDR Industry Update - April 2026

*The directory nearly doubled this month, but most of the growth is methodology, not industry boom.*

The headline number - 825 active entries to 1,471, a net +646 - looks like a blockbuster quarter for carbon removal. It isn't. The bulk of this jump comes from a baseline rebuild (v1_baseline) that replaced the old "unclassified" bucket (462 entries) with a proper focus taxonomy, and introduced "Enabling Tech" as a first-class pathway. That one category alone absorbed 487 new classifications. Read the Biochar and BECCS deltas the same way: Biochar moved 372 to 377, BECCS 87 to 85. The actual industry is roughly flat month-over-month.

What *is* real is the reshuffling underneath. We now separate suppliers (570) from ecosystem players (211), side-businesses (154), and excluded buyers (258). The old 319 "suppliers" number was noisier than it looked: a chunk of those are now reclassified as ecosystem or side-business, and 240 entries sit in a review-needed queue pending a second pass. Expect that queue to shrink, not the supplier count to grow, over the next two updates.

Genuine new entries worth naming: **FS Fueling Sustainability** (Brazil, 1,642 employees, BECCS) is the largest addition and a reminder that Brazilian ethanol-with-capture is scaling faster than Western press covers. **Kanadevia** (ex-Hitachi Zosen) and **Öresundskraft** add industrial BECCS heft in Japan and Sweden. On the reclassification side, **CyanoCapture** moved pathway and focus simultaneously, and **Carbon Lockdown** and **Arrhenius** shifted primary pathway - worth a look if you track specific suppliers.

So what for the field: if you've been citing directory counts as proxy for industry growth, stop. Use pathway-level supplier counts from v1_baseline forward as your new zero.
