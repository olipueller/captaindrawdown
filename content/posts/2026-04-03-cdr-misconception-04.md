The critics aren&rsquo;t wrong about these specific projects.</p><h2 id=the-reality>The Reality<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#the-reality>#</a></h2><p>But here&rsquo;s what the &ldquo;all scams&rdquo; crowd misses:</p><p>DAC+storage credits from Climeworks or 1PointFive remove CO₂ you can measure, verify, and store in geological formations for millions of years.</p><p>The molecule either IS in the rock or it ISN&rsquo;T.</p><p>There&rsquo;s no counterfactual dispute. No baseline inflation. No permanence risk.</p><p>This is categorically different from a forest offset.</p><h2 id=the-real-risk>The Real Risk<a hidden class=anchor aria-hidden=true href=#the-real-risk>#</a></h2><p>The quality framework that matters:</p><p>Avoidance (REDD+, cookstoves) → Counterfactual, fragile, risky

→ Biochar: centuries
→ <a href=/posts/what-is-enhanced-weathering/>Enhanced weathering</a>: millennia<br>→ DAC+geological storage: geological timescales</p><p>Microsoft, Stripe, Shopify all buy CDR only.
