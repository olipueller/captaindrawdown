# CDR Industry Update - November 2026

*November 2026's jump from 913 to 1,062 published companies reflects my search catching up with the field, not the field growing.*

The published count rose by 149 this month: 103 new entries, 50 existing records visible again after a re-check or reclassification, and 4 no longer listed. This is the regular monthly run, but the last company search before it was the directory build of 2026-04-18. The additions are what the search found since then, which is why the number is large. A new entry is a company I found this month, not one founded this month, so do not read this as company formation.

The search was wide: 9 sources named 2,194 companies, 393 of them not yet in the database. Of those, 337 had enough evidence for a web check and classification, and 103 passed. The other 234 are not carbon removal, duplicates, or still under review, and I do not count them as additions.

Biochar dominates the intake with 52 of the 103 new entries, ahead of Enabling Tech (20) and bioenergy with carbon capture and storage (BECCS) (14). The United States supplied 32 and India 11, including DeCarbo Cleantech Private Limited. Biochar now holds 453 of 1,062 published companies. So what: pathway shares in this directory measure my coverage as much as market structure, so treat the biochar lead with care.

The less flattering pattern is in activity ratings. I re-scored activity signals (website, LinkedIn, news) on existing records and got 83 downgrades against 49 upgrades, net -34. 36 companies moved from Active to Likely Dead, and 22 moved the other way. HerbaCarbo, Carbonloop and Biochar Rendsburg each had their activity rating and listing status revised. These are my readings of public signals, not actions the companies took, but the direction suggests headline company counts overstate how many are operating.
