**OneStopESG just published [Pure DC Launches World&#x27;s First Integrated Carbon Removal Platform from the Data Centre Sector Through AHE Subsidiary](https://onestopesg.com/esg-news/pure-dc-ahe-biochar-carbon-removal-platform).**

Pure DC has launched an integrated carbon removal platform through its climate subsidiary A Healthier Earth (AHE), targeting hyperscalers and institutional buyers across Europe. The platform aggregates biochar projects into tranches governed by a single set of standards, with credits certified under the Isometric Standard and tracked using Mangrove Systems' digital monitoring, reporting and verification software. Pure DC says the approach addresses fragmentation in the biochar market, where inconsistent methodologies have limited large-scale procurement. Supply comes from both AHE's own production and partner-developed projects under centralised governance. Executives from Pure DC, Isometric and Mangrove framed the offering as infrastructure-grade carbon removal designed for hyperscale buyers.

_Our take (Heads-up):_ The platform play here is real - bundling biochar projects under one standard and MRV stack does respond to a known procurement problem. The open questions are durability claims for biochar at scale, project-level land and feedstock sourcing, and whether tranche-level governance actually constrains lower-quality suppliers. Worth tracking once delivery volumes and pricing are disclosed.
