**Carbon Herald just published [Carbon Removal Ireland Highlights Great National CDR Potential, Urging For Swift Action](https://carbonherald.com/carbon-removal-ireland-highlights-great-national-cdr-potential-urging-for-swift-action/).**

Carbon Herald reports that Carbon Dioxide Removal Ireland (CDRi) is highlighting what it sees as significant scope for scaling carbon removal technologies across the country. The group is urging policymakers and industry to act quickly to develop the sector, arguing that Ireland has the natural and industrial conditions to support a meaningful CDR build-out. The piece frames CDRi's position as both an assessment of opportunity and a warning that delay could erode that advantage. Specific pathways, capacity figures, and policy asks are detailed in the original article.

_Our take (Context):_ Ireland has land-use, agricultural, and offshore storage angles worth taking seriously, but advocacy-group estimates of national potential tend to run optimistic until siting, permitting, and MRV details are pinned down. Worth tracking what concrete policy mechanisms CDRi is proposing and whether the projected potential is technical, economic, or sustainable.
