**Carbon Herald just published [Caravel Bio Advances Low-Cost Carbon Capture Technology](https://carbonherald.com/caravel-bio-advances-low-cost-carbon-capture-technology/).**

Carbon Herald reports that US startup Caravel Bio has made progress on enzyme-based carbon capture by significantly improving the stability of the enzymes used in the process. Enzyme stability has historically been a barrier to commercial deployment, as the biological catalysts degrade under industrial conditions and need frequent replacement. The company positions its approach as a path to lower-cost capture compared with conventional amine solvents. The article frames the development within the broader push to bring down the cost curve of point-source carbon capture technologies.

_Our take (Heads-up):_ Enzyme degradation is a real and well-known bottleneck, so durability gains matter. That said, the piece does not appear to include third-party validation, pilot-scale runs, or specific cost-per-ton figures, which are the metrics that distinguish a lab claim from a deployable technology. Worth tracking once independent data shows up.
