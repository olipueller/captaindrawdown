**Carbon Herald just published [CarbonBlue And CTRL-S To Carry On SeaO2’s Legacy](https://carbonherald.com/carbonblue-and-ctrl-s-to-carry-on-seao2s-legacy/).**

Carbon Herald reports that SeaO2, a Netherlands-based developer of direct ocean carbon removal technology, has gone bankrupt, and that co-founder Ruben Brands has indicated the company's work will not disappear with it. According to the outlet, two other companies, CarbonBlue and CTRL-S, are set to carry forward elements of what SeaO2 built. CarbonBlue is an ocean-based carbon removal developer working on water-side CO2 extraction, and CTRL-S is another startup in the same space. The piece frames this as a continuation of SeaO2's technical and commercial legacy rather than a straightforward acquisition, with Brands positioning the handover as a way to keep the underlying approach moving toward deployment despite the original company's failure.

_Our take (Heads-up):_ The useful signal here is that ocean CDR intellectual property and know-how are being redistributed rather than lost, which matters for a sector with few field-tested teams. What is missing is specifics: which assets, patents or staff go where, on what terms, and whether any pilot infrastructure survives. Until those details surface, treat 'legacy continues' as a founder's framing, not a verified transfer.
