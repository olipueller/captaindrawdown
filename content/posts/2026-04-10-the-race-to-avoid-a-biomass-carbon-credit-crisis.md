Biomass carbon removal credits could face a credibility meltdown if the industry doesn't fix how it accounts for the carbon in feedstock. The core problem: if you claim credit for storing carbon from waste biomass, you need to prove that biomass would have actually released its carbon otherwise. Get the counterfactual wrong and you're selling hot air. Heatmap has the details on the accounting fight that will define whether BiCRS becomes real or becomes the next junk offset.
